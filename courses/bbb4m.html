Chapters

Chapter 1
The Global Business Environment
Forces driving globalization, factors that shape international business, comparative advantage, political/legal/economic/socio-cultural systems, and the role of trade agreements
Chapter 2
International Marketing
Market research, the 4 Ps in global markets, standardization vs adaptation, product/promotion strategies, branding across cultures, and pricing in foreign markets
Chapter 3
Sales, Production & Distribution
Modes of market entry, distribution channels, logistics & supply chain, modes of transport, Incoterms 2020, sourcing and global supply networks
Chapter 4
Trade Documentation & Finance
Commercial invoice, bill of lading, certificate of origin, letters of credit, customs and tariffs, foreign exchange, methods of payment, and managing currency risk
Chapter 5
Working in International Business
Skills needed for global careers, cross-cultural competence, ethics and corporate social responsibility, expatriate assignments, and the Culminating Country/Market Profile
