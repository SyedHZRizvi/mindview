Chapters

Chapter 1
Unit 1: Foundations of Culture
Defining culture, material vs non-material culture, cultural universals, ethnocentrism vs cultural relativism, social-science lenses (anthropology, sociology, psychology)
Chapter 2
Unit 2: Cultural Expressions
Art and aesthetics across cultures, language and oral tradition, religion and worldview, ritual and ceremony, music and dance as cultural carriers
Chapter 3
Unit 3: Identity & Diversity
Socialization and identity formation, gender and sexuality across cultures, race and ethnicity, intersectionality, multicultural Canada and Indigenous identity
Chapter 4
Unit 4: Globalization & Cultural Change
Cultural diffusion and globalization, media and digital culture, cultural appropriation vs appreciation, migration and diaspora, cultural revitalization movements
Chapter 5
Unit 5: Social-Science Inquiry Skills
Inquiry process, ethnographic fieldwork, surveys and interviews, primary vs secondary sources, research ethics, citing in APA, communicating findings
