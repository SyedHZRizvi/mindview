Chapters

Chapter 1
Unit 1: Earth Systems
The four spheres (lithosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere); plate tectonics, weather and climate, the water cycle, soil formation
Chapter 2
Unit 2: Biomes & Ecosystems
World biomes, energy flow and trophic pyramids, nutrient cycles, biodiversity, ecological succession, Canadian ecozones
Chapter 3
Unit 3: Human-Physical Interactions
Resource use, agriculture and food systems, urbanization and land-use change, natural hazards and risk, ecological footprint
Chapter 4
Unit 4: Sustainability
Renewable vs non-renewable resources, sustainable agriculture and fisheries, energy transitions, conservation policy, Indigenous stewardship
Chapter 5
Unit 5: Geographic Issues & Inquiry
Climate change, water scarcity, deforestation, environmental justice, the geographic inquiry process, GIS and remote sensing
