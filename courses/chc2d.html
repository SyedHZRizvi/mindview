Chapters

Chapter 1
Unit 1: Canada 1914–1929 — War & the Roaring Twenties
The Great War, Vimy Ridge, conscription crisis, the Halifax Explosion, women's suffrage, the Winnipeg General Strike, prosperity, persons case
Chapter 2
Unit 2: Canada 1929–1945 — Depression & World War II
The Great Depression, dust bowl, Bennett's New Deal, Dieppe, D-Day, Japanese-Canadian internment, home front, Holocaust response
Chapter 3
Unit 3: Canada 1945–1982 — Postwar Transformation
Baby boom, Cold War, NATO/NORAD, immigration shifts, Quiet Revolution, FLQ & October Crisis, multiculturalism, residential schools era
Chapter 4
Unit 4: Canada 1982–Present — Charter Era
Constitution Act & Charter, Meech Lake & Charlottetown, 1995 referendum, NAFTA, 9/11 and Afghanistan, Truth & Reconciliation, residential-school apology
Chapter 5
Unit 5: Historical Inquiry & Skills
The six historical thinking concepts (Seixas), evaluating sources, framing inquiry questions, citation, the historian's craft
