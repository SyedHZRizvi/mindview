Chapters

Chapter 1
Unit 1: Adult Identity, Self-Awareness & Goal-Setting
Erikson's adult stages, values clarification at 18+, strengths inventory, 1/5/10-year goal horizons, locus of control, resilience, mental-health self-advocacy
Chapter 2
Unit 2: Strategies for Post-Secondary Academic & Career Success
University vs college vs apprenticeship, critical reading at speed, academic integrity, executive function without imposed structure, citation managers, support services
Chapter 3
Unit 3: Workplace, Leadership & Adult Communication
Professional email, feedback giving/receiving, emotional intelligence at work, cross-cultural & intergenerational communication, Harvard negotiation, leading without authority
Chapter 4
Unit 4: Financial Literacy & Independent Living
50/30/20 budget, credit scores, OSAP, Ontario tenant rights, insurance, T1 General & TFSAs/RRSPs, banking, OHIP, transportation choices
Chapter 5
Unit 5: Civic Engagement & Lifelong Learning
Voting as an adult, volunteer engagement, lifelong learning frameworks, portfolio careers, automation & labour markets, mentorship, defining personal "enough"
