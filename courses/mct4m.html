Chapters

Chapter 1
Exponential & Logarithmic Functions
Exponent laws with rational exponents, exponential graphs, the meaning of a logarithm, log laws, and solving exponential and logarithmic equations.
Chapter 2
Polynomial Equations & Inequalities
Polynomial operations, factoring techniques, the remainder and factor theorems, solving polynomial equations, and polynomial inequalities.
Chapter 3
Trigonometric Functions
Radian measure, the unit circle, graphs of sine and cosine, sinusoidal modelling, the sine and cosine laws, and trigonometric identities.
Chapter 4
Applications of Geometry
Vectors in two dimensions, geometric properties of circles, optimization of perimeter and area, and surface area and volume of pyramids, cones, and spheres.
Chapter 5
Modelling with Mathematics
Choosing the right function family for technical data, regression and curve-fitting, error and precision, and a culminating cross-strand modelling task.
