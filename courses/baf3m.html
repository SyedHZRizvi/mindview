Chapters

Chapter 1
Accounting Equation & Debits/Credits
The purpose of accounting, GAAP, the equation \( \text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Owner's Equity} \), T-accounts, the rules of debit and credit, normal balances
Chapter 2
The Journal & the Ledger
Source documents, the general journal, posting to the general ledger, account numbers, cross-referencing, opening and closing entries
Chapter 3
Trial Balance & the Worksheet
Preparing a trial balance, locating errors, the eight-column worksheet, adjusting entries (supplies, prepaid, depreciation, accrued)
Chapter 4
Financial Statements
The income statement, statement of owner's equity, classified balance sheet, working capital, current ratio, accounting cycle close
Chapter 5
Internal Control & Ethics
Internal-control principles, bank reconciliation, petty cash, segregation of duties, ethics, the CPA code of conduct, financial-statement users
