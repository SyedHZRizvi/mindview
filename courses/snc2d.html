Unit 3: Climate Change

The electromagnetic spectrum, reflection (plane & curved mirrors), refraction, Snell's law, lenses, the human eye, and optical instruments.

Cross-strand integration: scientific method, experimental design, lab safety (WHMIS), data analysis, STSE issues, and career pathways in science.
