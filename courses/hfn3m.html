Chapters

Chapter 1
Nutrition Basics
The six classes of nutrients, macronutrients (carbohydrates, lipids, proteins), micronutrients (vitamins, minerals), water, energy balance, kilocalories per gram
Chapter 2
Digestion & Metabolism
Mechanical & chemical digestion, GI tract structure & function, enzymes, absorption, ATP & energy metabolism, BMR & total daily energy expenditure
Chapter 3
Canada's Food Guide & Meal Planning
Canada's Food Guide (2019), food group recommendations, plate proportions, reading nutrition facts tables, daily value, meal planning across the lifespan
Chapter 4
Food Sources, Preparation & Safety
From farm to fork, food production & processing, cooking methods & nutrient retention, foodborne illness, the "Clean–Separate–Cook–Chill" rule, food labelling
Chapter 5
Food Trends & Issues
Food security & insecurity, sustainable food systems, dietary patterns (vegetarian, Mediterranean), Indigenous foodways, advertising & body image, eating disorders
