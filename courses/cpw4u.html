Chapters

Chapter 1
Unit 1: Power, Governance, and Political Systems
The modern state, sovereignty, democratic/authoritarian/totalitarian systems, Canadian federalism, Westminster vs. presidential, the Charter, judicial review, separation of powers
Chapter 2
Unit 2: Political Ideologies and Citizenship
Liberalism, conservatism, socialism, fascism, populism, Indigenous political thought, citizenship, political parties, civil society and social movements
Chapter 3
Unit 3: Human Rights and Human Welfare
UDHR (1948), ICCPR, ICESCR, refugee law, Charter and provincial codes, Truth and Reconciliation, UNDRIP, the welfare-state debate
Chapter 4
Unit 4: Conflict and War
Causes of war (security dilemma, ethnic nationalism), just war theory, international humanitarian law, R2P, case studies (Rwanda, Bosnia, Ukraine), Canadian peacekeeping and NATO
Chapter 5
Unit 5: Cooperation and Global Issues
The UN system, ICJ, WTO; international institutions; climate governance (Paris Agreement); global health (WHO); humanitarian intervention vs. sovereignty; Canada's development footprint; future of multilateralism
