Chapters

Chapter 1
Unit 1: 1450–1650 — Renaissance, Reformation, and the First Global Age
Italian Renaissance, Northern humanism, Luther and the religious revolutions, the Columbian Exchange, Spanish empire and African slavery, scientific revolution begins
Chapter 2
Unit 2: 1650–1789 — Absolutism, Enlightenment, and the Atlantic World
Louis XIV and absolutist France, English constitutional revolutions, the Scientific Revolution matures, the Enlightenment, Atlantic slave economies, Qing China and Tokugawa Japan
Chapter 3
Unit 3: 1789–1914 — Age of Revolutions, Industry, and Empire
French and Haitian revolutions, Napoleon, industrial revolution, nationalism and unification, abolitionism and reform, the new imperialism, Meiji Japan
Chapter 4
Unit 4: 1914–1945 — The Age of World Wars and Ideologies
First World War and its peace, Russian Revolution, interwar dictatorships, the Great Depression, fascism and Nazism, Second World War, the Holocaust, Hiroshima
Chapter 5
Unit 5: 1945–Present — Cold War, Decolonisation, and Globalisation
Cold War origins and proxy wars, decolonisation in Asia and Africa, civil rights movements, fall of the USSR, post-1989 globalisation, climate & the 21st-century world
Chapter 6
Unit 6: Historical Inquiry & Skills Across the Course
Historical thinking concepts, primary vs secondary sources, source corroboration and bias, periodisation, causation, continuity and change, and writing the research essay
