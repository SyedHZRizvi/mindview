Unit 3: Memory, Study & Test-Taking Strategies

Communication styles, active listening, non-verbal cues, assertiveness, constructive feedback, group work, conflict resolution, digital etiquette
Chapter 5
Unit 5: Goal-Setting, Time Management & Pathways
SMART goals, backwards planning, time-blocking, Pomodoro, OSSD requirements, post-secondary pathways, cover letters, résumés, interviews, portfolios
