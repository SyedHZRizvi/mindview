Chapters

Chapter 1
Heritage & Sources of Canadian Law
Ancient codes (Hammurabi, Mosaic, Roman), British common law, the Constitution Act 1867/1982, Indigenous legal traditions, division of powers, statute vs case law
Chapter 2
The Charter of Rights and Freedoms
Fundamental, democratic, mobility, legal, equality, and language rights; section 1 limits; section 33 notwithstanding clause; landmark Charter cases
Chapter 3
Criminal Law & Procedure
Elements of a crime (actus reus / mens rea), categories of offences, criminal trial process, defences, the Youth Criminal Justice Act, sentencing principles
Chapter 4
Civil Law & Dispute Resolution
Torts (negligence, intentional torts), contract law, family law, employment law, alternative dispute resolution (ADR), regulatory tribunals and small claims court
Chapter 5
Legal Inquiry & Citizenship Skills
Legal research and case briefs, the IRAC framework, statutory interpretation, mooting and persuasive legal argument, citizenship and access to justice
