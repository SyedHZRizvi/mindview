🔍 Chapter 3: Quantities in Chemical Reactions

Assessment FOR Learning — Pre-Chapter Diagnostic
📋 Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Check your math skills (significant figures, unit conversions, ratios) and prior chemistry (balanced equations) before stoichiometry.
Prerequisite Skills
Question 1 [2 marks]
Round 5.6789 g to (a) 3 significant figures; (b) 2 significant figures.
Reveal expected answer
(a) 5.68 g; (b) 5.7 g.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Convert 250 mL to L; convert 0.0035 kg to g.
Reveal expected answer
0.250 L; 3.5 g.
Question 3 [2 marks]
Balance: \( \text{C}_2\text{H}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \).
Reveal expected answer
2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O.
Question 4 [2 marks]
If 3 mol of A reacts with 1 mol of B (ratio A:B = 3:1), how many mol of B reacts with 12 mol of A?
Reveal expected answer
12 × (1/3) = 4 mol B.
Question 5 [2 marks]
What is Avogadro's number, approximately? What does it count?
Reveal expected answer
\(N_A \approx 6.022 \times 10^{23}\,\text{particles/mol}\). It is the number of particles (atoms, molecules, ions, …) in one mole of substance.
Question 6 [3 marks]
Compute (without calculator if you can): \(0.250 \times 24.0\) and report to 3 significant figures.
Reveal expected answer
0.250 × 24.0 = 6.00.
Question 7 [3 marks]
Express in scientific notation: (a) 0.000045; (b) 6 022 000 000 000 000 000 000.
Reveal expected answer
(a) 4.5 × 10⁻⁵; (b) 6.022 × 10²¹.
Reflection
Question 8 [Reflection]
Rate your comfort with: significant figures, scientific notation, mole-mass conversions, balancing equations, ratio reasoning. What specifically is unclear?
