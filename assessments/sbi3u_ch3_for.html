Chapter 3: Genetic Processes — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from Grade 10 biology (cell division, basic inheritance) before starting Ch 3 Genetic Processes. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Cell division
[3 marks]
Briefly describe what happens during mitosis. State the chromosome number in the daughter cells if the parent cell had 8 chromosomes.
Question 2 Prereq · DNA structure
[3 marks]
Name the four DNA bases and state which pairs with which. Approximately how many H-bonds form between A and T versus G and C?
Question 3 Prereq · Vocabulary
[4 marks]
Define each of: gene, allele, genotype, phenotype. Use a simple example (e.g., flower colour or eye colour) to illustrate.
Question 4 Prereq · Inheritance
[3 marks]
Two heterozygous tall pea plants (Tt) are crossed. T (tall) is dominant to t (short). What fraction of the offspring are expected to be short (tt)?
Question 5 Skills · Probability
[3 marks]
Two independent events have probabilities P(A) = 1/4 and P(B) = 1/2. What is the probability that both A AND B occur? Show your reasoning.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Describe two ways a person can be heterozygous for the ABO blood group gene and have type A blood. (You may use allele notation IA, IB, i.)
Question 7 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Why are X-linked recessive disorders (e.g., red-green colour blindness, haemophilia) more common in males than in females?
Question 8 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Ch 3 topic do you feel least confident about: (a) meiosis stages, (b) Punnett squares for di- or sex-linked crosses, (c) non-Mendelian inheritance patterns, (d) chromosomal abnormalities, or (e) introductory biotechnology? Explain what specifically is unclear.
