HFN3M Chapter 5: Food Trends & Issues — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Pre-chapter check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify your prior awareness of food-system issues before starting Chapter 5.
Question 1 Prior · Food Security
[3 marks]
What do you understand by "food insecurity"? Do you think it is common in Canada? Estimate the percentage of Canadian households affected.
Question 2 Prior · Climate
[3 marks]
Name one type of food whose production contributes significantly to greenhouse-gas emissions, and one personal change someone could make to reduce their food-related carbon footprint.
Question 3 Prior · Trends
[3 marks]
Pick one popular eating trend (e.g., keto, intermittent fasting, vegan, paleo, "clean eating") that you have heard about. Summarise what it advises and one claimed benefit.
Question 4 Prior · Indigenous
[3 marks]
Name one traditional food eaten by Indigenous peoples in what is now Canada. What does "food sovereignty" mean to you?
Question 5 Skills · Media
[3 marks]
When you see a food-related post on social media (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), what are two questions you could ask yourself to decide whether the information is reliable?
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Name one eating disorder you have heard of. List one early warning sign and one Canadian source of support or help.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 5 topic do you find most personally relevant: food security, climate & food, dietary trends, Indigenous foodways, advertising & body image, or eating disorders? Why?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
