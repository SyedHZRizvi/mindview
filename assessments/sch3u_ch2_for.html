Purpose: Check your prerequisite skills from Ch 1 (formulas, polyatomic ions) before learning to balance and classify reactions.
Prerequisite Skills (Chapter 1 + SNC2D)
Question 1 [2 marks]
Write the formula for: (a) magnesium chloride, (b) potassium sulfate, (c) ammonium nitrate.
Reveal expected answer
(a) MgCl₂; (b) K₂SO₄; (c) NH₄NO₃.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Balance the equation: \(\text{N}_2 + \text{H}_2 \rightarrow \text{NH}_3\).
Reveal expected answer
N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃. (N: 2=2 ✓, H: 6=6 ✓)
Question 3 [2 marks]
Why is it incorrect to "balance" 2H₂ + O₂ → H₂O₂?
Reveal expected answer
Changing H₂O to H₂O₂ alters the identity of the product (water → hydrogen peroxide). Only coefficients can be added; subscripts must remain fixed.
Question 4 [2 marks]
List the 7 diatomic elements (the "BrINClHOF" group).
Reveal expected answer
Br₂, I₂, N₂, Cl₂, H₂, O₂, F₂.
Question 5 [3 marks]
Predict whether each pair will react: (a) Mg(s) + HCl(aq); (b) Au(s) + HCl(aq); (c) Zn(s) + AgNO₃(aq). (Hint: use the activity series.)
Reveal expected answer
(a) Yes — Mg above H. (b) No — Au below H. (c) Yes — Zn above Ag.
Question 6 [2 marks]
Predict the products of the combustion of ethanol (\(\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}\)) in excess oxygen.
Reveal expected answer
Excess O₂ → complete combustion → CO₂ + H₂O. C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O.
Question 7 [3 marks]
What does each state symbol mean: (s), (l), (g), (aq)?
Reveal expected answer
(s) solid, (l) pure liquid, (g) gas, (aq) aqueous = dissolved in water.
