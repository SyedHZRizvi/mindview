Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
The "grapevine" in an organization refers to:
Solution:
The grapevine = informal network. Research shows it is 70–90% accurate but rumour spreads fastest when leadership is silent.
Question 2 [2 marks]
A manager prefers face-to-face conversation when delivering bad news, instead of email. The choice is based on:
Solution:
Richer messages (bad news, complex change) need richer channels (face-to-face).
Question 3 [2 marks]
"Anchoring" is a cognitive bias where:
Solution:
Anchoring = over-weighting first information; classic example is the first number quoted in a negotiation.
Question 4 [2 marks]
Which is NOT a Thomas-Kilmann conflict style?
Solution:
Five styles: Competing, Collaborating, Compromising, Avoiding, Accommodating. Coaching is a leadership style, not a conflict one.
Question 5 [3 marks]
List the eight steps of Kotter's change model in order (one or two words each, comma-separated).
Solution:
(1) Urgency, (2) Coalition, (3) Vision, (4) Army, (5) Enable, (6) Wins, (7) Accelerate, (8) Anchor.
Question 6 [2 marks]
The 8-step rational decision-making model begins with:
Solution:
Step 1: identify and define the problem. Most bad decisions are answers to the wrong question.
