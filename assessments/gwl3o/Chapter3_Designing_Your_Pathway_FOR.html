Chapter 3: Designing Your Pathway — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C — C1.2, C1.3, C2.1, C2.3, C3.1, C3.3
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Help your teacher (and you) identify what you already know about postsecondary pathways, goal-setting, career research, financial planning, résumés/interviews, and transitions before Chapter 3 instruction begins. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest — this works only if you write what you genuinely think.
Question 1 Pathways
[3 marks]
Name the five Ontario postsecondary destinations recognised after the OSSD. For one of them, write a sentence about why a student might choose it.
Question 2 Self-Plan
[3 marks]
What is your current first-choice postsecondary destination? Why? Be honest — if you don't know, that's also a fine answer (write what you've considered and what feels unclear).
Question 3 Goals
[3 marks]
Write one goal you have for the next 12 months. Now look at what you wrote: does it have a specific number or outcome, a deadline, and a first-step action you could take tomorrow? If not, revise it.
Question 4 Career Research
[3 marks]
Name two careers you are currently curious about. For each, write down what you know — and one specific thing you would still want to find out before committing.
Question 5 Money
[3 marks]
Roughly, how much do you think it would cost to attend one year of (a) college, (b) university with residence, or (c) apprenticeship in Ontario? Take your best guess for any one of the three. (We'll compare your guess to actual figures during the chapter.)
Question 6 Résumé
[3 marks]
Take one job, volunteer role, or significant school responsibility you've held. Write a one-sentence résumé bullet for it. Include the role, where/when, and one specific accomplishment.
Question 7 Transitions
[3 marks]
Describe one transition (school change, move, family change, job change) you've gone through. What helped you adjust? What didn't? Two or three sentences.
Question 8 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which part of Chapter 3 are you most uncertain about going in: postsecondary pathways, SMART goals, NOC career research, financial planning & OSAP, résumés/interviews, or managing transitions? One sentence is enough.
📖 Complete Answer Key & Marking Rubric

Q1 — 3 marks. Five destinations: apprenticeship, college, university, workplace, community living. 0.5 mark per destination + 0.5 for thoughtful rationale.

Q2 — 3 marks. Honest self-knowledge scores higher than performance. Award full marks for clear current preference with one reason, or for an honest "I don't know — here's what I've considered." Use answers to spot students drifting toward poor-fit choices (e.g., university as default with no apparent fit).

Q3 — 3 marks. Strong responses are SMART-like (specific number/outcome, deadline, named first action). Diagnostic: most students will write a wish; the chapter will teach them to convert to SMART. 1 mark goal + 1 measurable element + 1 first-step action.

Q4 — 3 marks. Look for awareness that career research is more than self-perception — students who name a specific research question (wages, training, daily duties, employment outlook) demonstrate readiness for the NOC/Job Bank work in 3.3. 1 per career + 1 for research question quality.

Q5 — 3 marks. Diagnostic, not factual — see what students think postsecondary actually costs. Actual ranges for reference: college $18,000–$24,000/yr resident; university $24,000–$32,000/yr resident; apprenticeship $1,500–$3,500 tools/year but paid throughout. Use the gap between guess and reality as a hook into 3.4.

Q6 — 3 marks. Strong bullets have numbers, action verbs, specific accomplishments. 1 mark role/title + 1 specific evidence + 1 conventions. Vague responses ("Worked at restaurant") get partial credit and feedback to rewrite.

Q7 — 3 marks. Look for awareness of what helped (social support, routines, time, professional support, internal reframes) and what didn't (rushing, isolation, denial). 1 mark scenario + 2 marks insight.

Q8 — Ungraded. Use answers to target instruction. Common starting gaps: OSAP/RESP/CESG mechanics; specific NOC research; STAR interview structure.

Feedback rubric: Provide descriptive feedback rather than a grade. Focus on one strength + one next step.
