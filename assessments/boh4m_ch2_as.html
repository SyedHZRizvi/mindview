Topic 2.1 — Leadership vs Management & Lewin
Question 1
According to Kotter, leadership primarily produces:
Solution:
Kotter: Management produces order/consistency. Leadership produces change/movement.
Question 2
During a medical emergency in an ICU, which of Lewin's leadership styles is most appropriate?
Solution:
Crises with trained staff and time pressure favour autocratic. After the crisis, switch to democratic for debrief.
Topic 2.2 — Behavioural & Contingency Theories
Question 3
Blake and Mouton's Managerial Grid (9,9) is the:
Solution:
(9,9) = high concern for production AND people = team management, Blake & Mouton's ideal.
Question 4
In Hersey & Blanchard's Situational Leadership, a low-ability, low-willingness follower needs which style?
Solution:
Low ability + low willingness = S1 Directing (high directive, low supportive). Coaching (S2) fits low ability + high willingness.
Topic 2.3 — Transformational Leadership
Question 5
Which is NOT one of Bass's "four I's" of transformational leadership?
Solution:
Four I's: Idealized influence, Inspirational motivation, Intellectual stimulation, Individualized consideration. Incentive-based exchange is transactional.
Topic 2.4 — Tuckman's Team Stages
Question 6
In Tuckman's model, which stage features open disagreement about goals, roles, and process?
Solution:
Storming = conflict. Healthy if managed; teams that skip storming rarely produce honest work.
Question 7
A new member joins a team that has been performing well. Most likely, the team will:
Solution:
Adding a member typically resets the team to Forming, then it must work back through Storming and Norming.
Topic 2.5–2.6 — Motivation Theories
Question 8
In Herzberg's two-factor theory, salary is best classified as a:
Solution:
Salary is a hygiene factor — its absence dissatisfies, but its presence does not strongly motivate. Achievement, recognition, growth = motivators.
Question 9
Vroom's expectancy theory: Motivation = ?
Solution:
Vroom: \( M = E \times I \times V \). Multiplicative — if any term is zero, motivation collapses.
Question 10
In Adams's equity theory, an employee who feels under-rewarded relative to a coworker is most likely to:
Solution:
Under-rewarded employees act to restore equity: reduce effort, ask for more, or leave.
