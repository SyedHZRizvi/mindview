Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Which document classifies a product for tariff and regulatory purposes?
Solution:
The HS (Harmonized System) code on the customs declaration drives the duty rate and any regulatory requirements.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Under an irrevocable letter of credit, the issuing bank's commitment to pay can be changed:
Solution:
"Irrevocable" means the L/C cannot be modified without consent of all parties involved. That predictability is the whole point.
Question 3 [2 marks]
A 9.5% ad valorem tariff on a CIF value of \(C\$1{,}200\) is:
Solution:
\(0.095 \times 1200 = C\$114\).
Question 4 [2 marks]
The CAD/JPY rate is 110 yen per Canadian dollar. To convert JPY 550,000 to CAD, you get:
Solution:
\(\dfrac{550{,}000}{110} = C\$5{,}000\).
Question 5 [2 marks]
Under a "Documents Against Payment" (D/P) collection, the buyer:
Solution:
Under D/P, the buyer must pay before the documents (and thus title to goods) are released by the bank.
Question 6 [3 marks]
CFPOA is Canada's law that:
Solution:
The Corruption of Foreign Public Officials Act (CFPOA) criminalizes bribery of foreign officials by Canadian persons/companies, with steep penalties.
