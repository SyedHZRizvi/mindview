Topic 3.1 — Trial Balance
Question 1
A trial balance lists accounts in:
Solution:
Order is the chart-of-accounts order, which mirrors the order they will appear on the financial statements.
Question 2
A balanced trial balance proves:
Solution:
It proves arithmetical balance only — many other errors (omission, wrong account on the correct side, etc.) can leave Dr=Cr.
Topic 3.2 — Locating Errors
Question 3
A trial balance is out by \$54. Is the difference divisible by 9?
Solution:
\(54 \div 9 = 6\) → integer. Difference is divisible by 9, classic sign of a transposition (e.g., 81 swapped for 27 or similar) or a slide.
Question 4
A trial balance is out by \$200. Apply the ÷ 2 test. Search for an account with a balance equal to:
Solution:
\(200 \div 2 = 100\). An account with a \$100 balance is likely placed in the wrong column.
Topic 3.3 & 3.4 — Worksheet & Adjustments
Question 5
Supplies asset balance \$240. Physical count at month-end \$60. Supplies used during the month equals:
Solution:
Supplies used = beginning balance − ending balance = \(240 - 60 = 180\). Adjustment: Dr. Supplies Expense 180; Cr. Supplies 180.
Question 6
Equipment costs \$5 000, residual value \$500, useful life 5 years (straight-line). Annual depreciation =
Solution:
\( \dfrac{5\,000 - 500}{5} = \dfrac{4\,500}{5} = 900 \) per year.
Question 7
"Accumulated Depreciation" is best classified as a:
Solution:
Contra-asset — credit balance, shown on the balance sheet as a deduction from the related asset to give net book value.
Topic 3.5 — Accruals & Unearned Revenue
Question 8
On 1 April a parent prepays \$600 for 6 weeks of tutoring. At 30 April, 4 weeks have been delivered. Amount of revenue earned at 30 April =
Solution:
\( \tfrac{4}{6} \cdot 600 = 400 \). Adjusting entry: Dr. Unearned Revenue 400; Cr. Tutoring Revenue 400.
Question 9
Salaries earned by employees for the last week of a month, payable next month: at month-end, the adjusting entry is:
Solution:
Accrued expense: recognise the expense in the period earned by employees; the offset is a payable (liability) since cash will be paid later.
Topic 3.6 — Closing Entries
Question 10
Closing entries reduce which accounts to zero?
Solution:
Temporary accounts (revenue, expense, drawings) are closed at period-end. Their balances net to Capital via Income Summary.
