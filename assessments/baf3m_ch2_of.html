Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
A cheque written by the business is supported by:
Solution:
The cheque stub or duplicate copy retained by the company supports the credit to Cash.
Question 2 [2 marks]
In the general journal, the explanation is written:
Solution:
The narrative explanation goes below the credited account, indented further than the credit.
Question 3 [2 marks]
A "compound journal entry" has:
Solution:
Compound entry = three or more lines (more than one debit OR more than one credit), still balanced.
Question 4 [2 marks]
The book of original entry is the:
Solution:
Journal = book of original entry; ledger = book of final entry.
Question 5 [2 marks]
After posting, the journal's P.R. column should contain:
Solution:
The journal's P.R. is the ledger account number; the ledger's P.R. is the journal page (e.g., J1).
Question 6 [3 marks]
A business has the following A/R subsidiary ledger balances: A. Tan \$300 Dr; B. Singh \$240 Dr; C. Lee \$180 Dr. What should the A/R control account in the general ledger show? Show your calculation.
Solution:
Sum = \(300 + 240 + 180 = 720\). Control account = \$720 Dr.
