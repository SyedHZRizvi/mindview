Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Net sales rose from \$ 500,000 to \$ 565,000. Percentage change is:
Solution:
\( (565{,}000 - 500{,}000)/500{,}000 = 65{,}000/500{,}000 = 13\% \).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Current assets \$ 360,000; current liabilities \$ 120,000. Current ratio is:
Solution:
\( 360{,}000 / 120{,}000 = 3.0 \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
COGS \$ 480,000; average inventory \$ 60,000. Inventory turnover is:
Solution:
\( 480{,}000 / 60{,}000 = 8.0 \) times per year. Days' inventory \( = 365/8 \approx 45.6 \) days.
Question 4 [2 marks]
EBIT \$ 360,000; interest expense \$ 60,000. Times-interest-earned is:
Solution:
\( 360{,}000 / 60{,}000 = 6.0 \) times.
Question 5 [2 marks]
Total liabilities \$ 540,000; total equity \$ 360,000. Debt-to-equity ratio is:
Solution:
\( 540{,}000 / 360{,}000 = 1.50 \). \$ 1.50 of debt per \$ 1 of equity.
Question 6 [3 marks]
EPS \$ 3.00; share price \$ 48; annual dividend \$ 1.20. The P/E ratio is:
Solution:
\( 48 / 3.00 = 16.0 \). Investors pay \$ 16 per dollar of trailing earnings.
