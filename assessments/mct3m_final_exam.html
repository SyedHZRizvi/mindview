Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [25 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Evaluate \( 81^{3/4} \).
Solution:
\( 81^{3/4} = (\sqrt[4]{81})^3 = 3^3 = 27 \).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Solve \( 4^{x+1} = 64 \) for \( x \).
Solution:
\( 64 = 4^3 \). \( x + 1 = 3 \Rightarrow x = 2 \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
For \( y = 2(x - 4)^2 + 5 \), state the vertex.
Solution:
Vertex \( (h, k) = (4, 5) \).
Question 4 [2 marks]
Solve \( x^2 - 6x + 8 = 0 \). The smaller root is:
Solution:
\( (x-2)(x-4) = 0 \). Roots: 2 and 4. Smaller is 2.
Question 5 [2 marks]
Factor \( x^3 + 64 \).
Solution:
Sum of cubes with \( a = x, b = 4 \): \( (x + 4)(x^2 - 4x + 16) \).
Question 6 [2 marks]
The exact value of \( \sin 30° \) is:
Solution:
From 30-60-90 triangle: \( \sin 30° = 1/2 \).
Question 7 [2 marks]
Convert \( 5\pi/4 \) radians to degrees.
Solution:
\( 5\pi/4 \times 180/\pi = 5 \times 45 = 225° \).
Question 8 [3 marks]
A cylinder has radius 4 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume (round to nearest integer cm³).
Solution:
\( V = \pi(16)(10) = 160\pi \approx 503 \) cm³.
Question 9 [2 marks]
Convert 0.045 m to mm.
Solution:
1 m = 1000 mm. \( 0.045 \times 1000 = 45 \) mm.
Question 10 [2 marks]
Two similar cubes have side ratio 1:3. The ratio of their volumes is:
Solution:
Volume ratio = \( (1/3)^3 = 1/27 \). So 1:27.
Question 11 [2 marks]
For \( y = 5 \sin(2x) + 3 \), the period (in degrees) is:
Solution:
Period = \( 360°/k = 360°/2 = 180° \).
Question 12 [2 marks]
A sphere has radius 6 cm. Find its surface area (round to nearest integer cm²).
Solution:
\( SA = 4\pi r^2 = 4\pi(36) = 144\pi \approx 452 \) cm².
