Topic 2.1–2.2 — Quadratic Forms & Solving
Question 1
For \( y = -2(x - 3)^2 + 8 \), the vertex is:
Solution:
Vertex form \( y = a(x - h)^2 + k \): \( h = 3, k = 8 \). Vertex \( (3, 8) \), opens down (since \( a = -2 < 0 \)).
Question 2
Solve \( x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \). The smaller root is:
Solution:
\( (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0 \). Roots: 2 and 3. Smaller is 2.
Question 3
The discriminant of \( 2x^2 - 5x + 1 = 0 \) is:
Solution:
\( D = b^2 - 4ac = 25 - 4(2)(1) = 25 - 8 = 17 \).
Topic 2.3 — Completing the Square
Question 4
Convert \( y = x^2 + 6x - 4 \) to vertex form. The vertex is:
Solution:
Half of 6 is 3; square is 9. \( y = (x^2 + 6x + 9) - 9 - 4 = (x + 3)^2 - 13 \). Vertex \( (-3, -13) \).
Question 5
A revenue function is \( R(p) = -5p^2 + 100p \). The price that maximises revenue is:
Solution:
Vertex at \( p = -100/(-10) = 10 \).
Topic 2.4 — Applications
Question 6
A ball's height (m) at time \( t \) (s) is \( h(t) = -5t^2 + 20t + 1.5 \). Maximum height occurs at \( t = \):
Solution:
Vertex at \( t = -20/(-10) = 2 \) s.
Question 7
For the same \( h(t) \), what is the maximum height (m)?
Solution:
\( h(2) = -5(4) + 20(2) + 1.5 = -20 + 40 + 1.5 = 21.5 \) m.
Topic 2.5–2.6 — Cubic Functions & Factoring
Question 8
Factor \( x^3 - 8 \).
Solution:
Difference of cubes: \( a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2) \). With \( a = x, b = 2 \): \( (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4) \).
Question 9
Factor \( 27x^3 + 1 \).
Solution:
Sum of cubes: \( a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - ab + b^2) \). With \( a = 3x, b = 1 \): \( (3x + 1)(9x^2 - 3x + 1) \).
Question 10
For \( P(x) = x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6 \), is \( x = 2 \) a root? (yes/no)
Solution:
\( P(2) = 8 - 24 + 22 - 6 = 0 \). So \( x = 2 \) is a root and \( (x - 2) \) is a factor.
Question 11
Solve \( 2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0 \). The larger root is:
Solution:
\( (2x - 1)(x - 3) = 0 \). Roots: \( 1/2 \) and 3. Larger is 3.
Question 12
The maximum number of real zeros of a cubic function is:
Solution:
A polynomial of degree \( n \) has at most \( n \) real zeros. A cubic (degree 3) has at most 3.
