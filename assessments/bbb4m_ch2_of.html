Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
The acronym STP stands for:
Solution:
STP is the standard framework: segment the market, choose target segments, position the brand against competitors.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Which of the following is a PRIMARY research method?
Solution:
Focus groups are original data the firm collects itself = primary research. The others use pre-existing data = secondary.
Question 3 [2 marks]
A landed cost of \(C\$50\) with a retailer who takes a 30% gross margin gives a shelf price of:
Solution:
Retail price = cost / (1 − margin) = \(50 / 0.7 \approx C\$71.43\).
Question 4 [2 marks]
"Pure standardization" of a global brand BEST suits which of the following?
Solution:
Luxury goods like Rolex sell globally as a consistent symbol of premium quality; adaptation would erode the brand.
Question 5 [2 marks]
Which pricing strategy charges a HIGH initial price to harvest premium customers, then lowers price over time?
Solution:
Price skimming sets a high price to capture early adopters and high-WTP segments before lowering.
Question 6 [3 marks]
A product costs \(C\$40\) CIF the destination port. Duty is 10% of CIF. The local VAT is 8% of (CIF + duty). What is the landed cost?
Solution:
Duty = \(0.10 \times 40 = 4\). Subtotal = \(44\). VAT = \(0.08 \times 44 = 3.52\). Landed = \(44 + 3.52 = C\$47.52\).
