Question 1
The Truman Doctrine (March 1947):
Solution: Initially aimed at Greece and Turkey, the Truman Doctrine articulated US containment of communism — Kennan's "long telegram" had laid the strategic basis the year before. NATO followed in April 1949.
Question 2
The Cuban Missile Crisis (October 1962):
Solution: The closest the world came to nuclear war. Kennedy's blockade ("quarantine") and back-channel diplomacy with Khrushchev resolved it. A US-Soviet hotline was established afterward.
Question 3
The partition of British India in August 1947:
Solution: The Radcliffe boundary, drawn in five weeks, separated communities. The largest forced migration in human history was accompanied by communal violence killing 1-2 million. Gandhi was assassinated by a Hindu nationalist in January 1948.
Question 4
Apartheid in South Africa:
Solution: Apartheid was the National Party's 1948 systematisation of racial segregation. Mandela's ANC led the struggle; he was imprisoned 1962–1990; the first all-race elections in April 1994 brought him to the presidency.
Question 5
The 1948 Universal Declaration of Human Rights was drafted with major contributions from:
Solution: The Canadian John Humphrey wrote the first draft; Eleanor Roosevelt chaired the drafting committee; René Cassin (France) and Charles Malik (Lebanon) made major contributions. Adopted 10 December 1948.
Question 6
Deng Xiaoping's "Reform and Opening Up" in China began:
Solution: Deng's reforms began at the 11th Party Congress 3rd Plenum (December 1978). Over four decades, Chinese GDP grew at ~9% average; ~800 million people moved out of extreme poverty.
Question 7
The Berlin Wall fell on:
Solution: 9 November 1989; East German authorities opened the border in confusion after a press conference. German reunification followed 3 October 1990; the USSR dissolved 25 December 1991.
Question 8
The 1994 Rwandan Genocide killed approximately:
Solution: ~800,000 in ~100 days from 7 April 1994. Canadian general Roméo Dallaire commanded the UN mission that was tragically under-resourced; his account Shake Hands with the Devil became a key witness document.
Question 9
The Canadian Truth and Reconciliation Commission (2008–2015) addressed:
Solution: The TRC documented residential schools (1831-1996) and issued 94 Calls to Action in 2015. The 2021 confirmation of unmarked graves at Kamloops, Marieval, and other sites led to national mourning.
Question 10
The 2015 Paris Agreement on climate change:
Solution: Paris combined a global goal with country-specific Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs). Current NDCs even if fully delivered are inconsistent with 1.5°C; the threshold is likely to be crossed in the late 2020s.
