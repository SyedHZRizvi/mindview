Unit 5: Cold War & Globalisation — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand F
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from Units 1–4 before starting Unit 5. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Chronology
[3 marks]
Place in order: (a) Berlin Wall falls; (b) Korean War; (c) Cuban Missile Crisis; (d) Indian independence; (e) Vietnam War ends.
Question 2 Terms
[3 marks]
Define each in one sentence: (a) containment, (b) decolonisation, (c) globalisation.
Question 3 Source analysis
[4 marks]
"I have a dream that one day this nation will rise up and live out the true meaning of its creed: 'We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal.'" — Martin Luther King Jr., "I Have a Dream" speech, 28 August 1963
For this passage: (a) which document is King quoting? (b) what is the rhetorical move he is making? (c) name two pieces of US Civil Rights legislation that followed this speech.
Question 4 Knowledge · Geography
[3 marks]
For each, name the country: (a) Saigon (1975); (b) Sharpeville (1960); (c) Tiananmen Square (1989).
Question 5 Cause/effect
[3 marks]
Suggest two reasons the European overseas empires collapsed quickly after 1945. (You will refine in Unit 5.)
Question 6 Knowledge
[2 marks]
Identify (a) the year of the partition of India, and (b) the year apartheid ended in South Africa.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 5 topic do you feel least confident about: the Cold War, decolonisation, civil-rights movements, the rise of Asia, globalisation, or the contemporary world? Explain.
