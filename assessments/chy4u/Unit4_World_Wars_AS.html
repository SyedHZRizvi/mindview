Question 1
The short-term trigger for World War I was the assassination in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 of:
Solution: The assassination triggered Austria-Hungary's ultimatum to Serbia; alliance commitments and German "blank check" support produced general war within five weeks.
Question 2
Approximately how many soldiers died in the First World War?
Solution: ~9-11 million combatant deaths, ~7-13 million civilians. The 1918-1920 influenza pandemic added perhaps 50-100 million.
Question 3
The Bolsheviks seized power in Petrograd in:
Solution: 25-26 October Old Style / 7-8 November New Style 1917. The February Revolution earlier the same year had overthrown the Tsar and established the Provisional Government.
Question 4
The Holodomor of 1932–1933:
Solution: Soviet grain seizures during collectivisation produced famine concentrated in Ukraine; many historians and governments (including Canada in 2008) classify it as a genocide.
Question 5
Article 231 of the Treaty of Versailles (1919) — the "war guilt clause" — assigned responsibility for the war to:
Solution: Article 231 specified Germany and her allies' "responsibility for all the loss and damage." Reparations were initially set at 132 billion gold marks in 1921; the article was a political grievance the Weimar Republic could not shake.
Question 6
Adolf Hitler was appointed German Chancellor on:
Solution: Hindenburg appointed Hitler Chancellor in a coalition deal on 30 January 1933. The NSDAP had been the largest party since July 1932 but never won a majority in a free election; conservatives believed they could use Hitler as a front man.
Question 7
Operation Barbarossa, launched 22 June 1941, was:
Solution: 3.8 million Axis troops invaded the USSR; the war on the Eastern Front (1941–1945) killed perhaps 25 million Soviet citizens. Stalingrad (Aug 1942 – Feb 1943) was the turning point.
Question 8
The Wannsee Conference (20 January 1942):
Solution: Wannsee did not initiate the Holocaust (Einsatzgruppen mass-shootings of Soviet Jews began June 1941) but coordinated its industrial implementation across ministries. The minutes are an extraordinary primary source.
Question 9
Approximately how many Jewish people were murdered in the Holocaust?
Solution: Approximately 6 million Jews murdered — two-thirds of European Jewry — plus 5+ million non-Jewish victims. Auschwitz-Birkenau alone killed ~1.1 million. The Holocaust is the most fully documented genocide in history.
Question 10
Japan surrendered to end the Second World War on:
Solution: Germany surrendered 8 May (V-E Day); Japan 15 August (V-J Day) after a week that included two atomic bombings and Soviet declaration of war. Formal surrender ceremony was on USS Missouri 2 September.
