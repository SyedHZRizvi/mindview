Unit 1: Renaissance & Reformation — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from prior history coursework (basic chronology, source analysis, and key terms) before starting Unit 1. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Chronology
[3 marks]
Place the following events in chronological order: (a) Luther's 95 Theses; (b) Columbus's first voyage; (c) Fall of Constantinople to Ottomans; (d) Gutenberg's printing press becomes operational.
Question 2 Prereq · Terms
[3 marks]
Define each in one sentence: (a) feudalism, (b) crusade, (c) heresy. Use your own words; if unsure, write "unsure" — that is useful diagnostic information.
Question 3 Prereq · Geography
[3 marks]
Name three Italian city-states central to the Renaissance, and one major European trading city that became important to Atlantic commerce after 1500.
Question 4 Skills · Source analysis
[4 marks]
"It would be more secure to be both [feared and loved], but because it is difficult to put them together, it is much safer to be feared than loved when one of the two must be dispensed with." — Machiavelli, The Prince, Chapter 17 (1513)
For this passage, identify: (a) the author and date; (b) one piece of historical context relevant to interpreting it; (c) one question you would want to investigate before using it as evidence.
Question 5 Skills · Causation
[4 marks]
Suggest two long-term causes and one short-term trigger that you think contributed to the Protestant Reformation. You will refine these in Unit 1; this is just a baseline.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Without using a search engine, name (a) one Renaissance artist, (b) one major reformer of the Protestant Reformation, and (c) one Asian empire that existed between 1450 and 1650.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 1 topic do you feel least prepared for: Renaissance art and humanism, Reformation theology, Atlantic slavery, non-European empires, or the Scientific Revolution? Explain specifically what is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
