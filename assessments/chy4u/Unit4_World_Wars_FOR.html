Unit 4: World Wars — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from Units 1–3 before starting Unit 4. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Chronology
[3 marks]
Place in order: (a) Russian Revolution; (b) Treaty of Versailles; (c) outbreak of WWI; (d) outbreak of WWII; (e) Hitler appointed Chancellor.
Question 2 Terms
[3 marks]
Define each in one sentence: (a) totalitarianism, (b) genocide, (c) appeasement.
Question 3 Source analysis
[4 marks]
"My friend, you would not tell with such high zest / To children ardent for some desperate glory, / The old Lie: Dulce et decorum est / Pro patria mori." — Wilfred Owen, "Dulce et Decorum Est" (1917)
For this poem: (a) translate the Latin phrase; (b) identify Owen's stance toward the war; (c) what makes his perspective valuable as a primary source; (d) what is one limitation.
Question 4 Causation
[4 marks]
Suggest two long-term causes and two short-term triggers of WWI. (You will refine in Unit 4.)
Question 5 Knowledge · Geography
[2 marks]
Name the four empires that did not survive WWI.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Identify (a) the international body created in 1920 in response to WWI; (b) the major economic event of October 1929; (c) the year of Kristallnacht.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 4 topic do you feel least confident about: WWI causes, Russian Revolution, interwar dictatorships, WWII campaigns, or the Holocaust? Explain.
