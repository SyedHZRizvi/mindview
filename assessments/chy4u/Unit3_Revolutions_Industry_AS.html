Question 1
The Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen was adopted by the National Assembly in:
Solution: August 1789, the same month the Assembly abolished feudal privileges. "Men are born and remain free and equal in rights" is its opening claim.
Question 2
Olympe de Gouges (1791) is best known for:
Solution: De Gouges wrote her parallel declaration in 1791 to extend revolutionary rights to women; she was guillotined in 1793.
Question 3
The Haitian Revolution (1791–1804) is historically distinctive because it was:
Solution: Hundreds of slave revolts occurred in the Americas; only Haiti's produced an independent state (1 January 1804, Dessalines). Toussaint Louverture led the army that defeated French, Spanish, and British forces.
Question 4
James Watt's improved steam engine (patented 1769) was first widely deployed for:
Solution: Watt's separate-condenser design was first applied to mine pumping (where coal was free), then to factory spinning and weaving. The railway revolution came later (Stockton & Darlington 1825, Liverpool & Manchester 1830).
Question 5
Friedrich Engels's The Condition of the Working Class in England (1845) most directly documented:
Solution: Engels documented Manchester's working-class districts based on residence in his father's factory there; the book became a foundational text of socialist analysis and would shape his collaboration with Marx.
Question 6
Otto von Bismarck's strategy for German unification is often summarised in which famous phrase?
Solution: Bismarck's 1862 speech to the Prussian Budget Commission: "The great questions of the day will not be decided by speeches and resolutions of majorities — that was the great mistake of 1848 and 1849 — but by iron and blood." German unification completed January 1871 in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles.
Question 7
The Meiji Restoration of 1868:
Solution: The 1868 Restoration overthrew the shogunate, restored nominal imperial authority, abolished the samurai class (1873), promulgated a constitution (1889), and undertook the most successful crash modernisation in history. By 1905 Japan had defeated Russia.
Question 8
The Berlin Conference of 1884–1885:
Solution: Convened by Bismarck at Leopold II's request, the conference set rules for "effective occupation" claims. Within 30 years, every African state except Ethiopia and Liberia was a European colony.
Question 9
The Battle of Adwa (1896) is historically significant because:
Solution: Menelik II's victory at Adwa preserved Ethiopian independence until 1936 (Italian re-invasion under Mussolini, ended 1941). Adwa was a major symbol of African resistance to imperialism.
Question 10
The "drain theory" associated with Indian economists like Dadabhai Naoroji argued that:
Solution: The drain theory (Naoroji's Poverty and Un-British Rule in India, 1901, with earlier articulations) argued that taxes, remittances, and unequal trade transferred Indian wealth to Britain. Major famines (1876-8: ~5.5 million dead; 1896-1902: 6-10 million) made the theory's stakes clear.
