Unit 3: Revolutions, Industry, Empire — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from Units 1-2 before starting Unit 3. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Chronology
[3 marks]
Place these events in chronological order: (a) Industrial Revolution begins in Britain; (b) French Revolution begins; (c) Italian unification completed; (d) Berlin Conference; (e) US Declaration of Independence.
Question 2 Prereq · Terms
[3 marks]
Define each in one sentence: (a) nationalism, (b) liberalism, (c) imperialism.
Question 3 Geography
[3 marks]
Name (a) the country whose 1791 slave revolt produced Haiti; (b) two of the four major Caribbean sugar-producing islands of the 18th century; (c) the German city where the 1871 unification proclamation took place.
Question 4 Source analysis
[4 marks]
"Take up the White Man's burden — / Send forth the best ye breed — / Go bind your sons to exile / To serve your captives' need…" — Rudyard Kipling, "The White Man's Burden" (1899)
For this poem, identify: (a) the explicit message about empire; (b) what the title's metaphor implies about colonised peoples; (c) one event in 1899 that Kipling was responding to.
Question 5 Cause/effect
[3 marks]
Suggest three long-term causes of the French Revolution. (You will refine in Unit 3.)
Question 6 Knowledge
[2 marks]
Identify (a) the European political settlement of 1815 (after Napoleon's defeat) and (b) the year of the major European wave of liberal-nationalist revolutions.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 3 topic do you feel least confident about: the French Revolution, the Haitian Revolution, the Industrial Revolution, 1848 and unification, Asia in the long 19th century, or the new imperialism? Explain.
