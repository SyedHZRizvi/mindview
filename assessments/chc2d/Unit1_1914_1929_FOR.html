Unit 1: Canada 1914–1929 — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in Grade 9 Geography prerequisites and prior knowledge of WWI before starting Unit 1. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Geography
[3 marks]
List the ten Canadian provinces and three territories in order from east to west (then north). Identify the capital of each.
Question 2 Prereq · Government
[3 marks]
Briefly define each: (a) Parliament, (b) Prime Minister, (c) Governor General. What is the role of each in the Canadian system?
Question 3 Prior Knowledge
[3 marks]
"The lamps are going out all over Europe; we shall not see them lit again in our lifetime." — British Foreign Secretary Sir Edward Grey, 3 August 1914
What do you already know about the First World War? In 3–4 sentences, describe what you know about why it began and which countries fought.
Question 4 Prior Knowledge
[3 marks]
What do you know about Canada's relationship to Britain in 1914? Was Canada an "independent country"? Explain in 2–3 sentences.
Question 5 Skills · Source Analysis
[4 marks]
A 1915 Canadian recruitment poster shows a soldier in the trenches looking backward at a young man in civilian clothes sitting on a porch in a small town. The caption reads: "Why don't they come?"
In 3–4 sentences, identify: (a) who likely produced this poster; (b) what emotion it tries to provoke; (c) who is the implied target audience; (d) one thing the poster does not show that a complete picture of WWI Canada would include.
Question 6 Skills · Cause-Effect
[2 marks]
Imagine a friend asks: "Why did Canada send so many soldiers to a European war?" In 2–3 sentences, identify two reasons.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which of these Unit 1 topics do you feel least confident about: (a) WWI itself; (b) the conscription crisis; (c) the Halifax Explosion; (d) women's suffrage and the Persons Case; (e) the Winnipeg General Strike? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
