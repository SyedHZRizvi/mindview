Unit 4: Canada 1982–Present — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from Unit 3 (especially Calder 1973, the Quiet Revolution, and the 1971 Multiculturalism Policy) before starting Unit 4. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Unit 3
[3 marks]
From Unit 3: (a) what is Calder v. BC (1973)? (b) how does it set up the constitutional questions of 1982?
Question 2 Prior Knowledge
[3 marks]
What do you already know about the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms? Identify 2–3 rights or freedoms it protects.
Question 3 Prior Knowledge
[3 marks]
"The Constitution Act, 1982 ... shall be interpreted in a manner consistent with the preservation and enhancement of the multicultural heritage of Canadians." — section 27 of the Charter.
In 2–3 sentences, what does it mean to "interpret the Constitution in light of multicultural heritage"? Provide your initial understanding (we will refine it in the unit).
Question 4 Prior Knowledge
[3 marks]
What do you know about 9/11? In 3 sentences, describe what you know about the events of 11 September 2001 and Canada's response in the weeks afterward.
Question 5 Skills · Source Reading
[3 marks]
A photograph from 1 September 1990 shows a Mohawk Warrior in camouflage and a Canadian soldier in uniform face-to-face, separated by about 30cm, at the Oka standoff.
In 3 sentences: (a) what does the photograph capture? (b) what makes it an iconic Canadian image? (c) what does it leave out that a complete account of Oka would include?
Question 6 Reflection
[2 marks]
What do you know about the Truth and Reconciliation Commission? In 2–3 sentences, identify what you have learned about residential schools in earlier grades or other contexts.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 4 topic do you feel least confident about: (a) patriation and the Charter; (b) Meech, Charlottetown, and 1995; (c) Oka, Nisga'a, and the TRC; (d) NAFTA and free trade; (e) 9/11, Afghanistan, the Iraq decision; (f) climate, COVID-19, and contemporary politics? Explain.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
