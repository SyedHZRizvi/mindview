Market Research & STP
Question 1
Statistics Canada's Trade Data Online and the UN Comtrade database are best classified as:
Solution:
Secondary research uses data already collected by another party. Primary research means data the firm gathers itself (surveys, interviews, focus groups).
Question 2
"Health-conscious millennials" is an example of segmentation based primarily on:
Solution:
"Health-conscious" is a value/lifestyle (psychographic); "millennials" is an age cohort (demographic). The segment is a blend of both.
The 4 Ps in International Markets
Question 3
A new product is launched in a foreign market at a LOW price to gain share quickly, accepting thin margins. This is BEST called:
Solution:
Penetration pricing sets a low introductory price to gain share. Skimming sets a high price to harvest premium customers first.
Question 4
A Canadian backpack costs \(C\$25\) at the factory. After packaging, freight, duties, and a 20% distributor markup the landed-plus-distributor cost is \(C\$45\). The retailer then takes a 40% gross margin. What is the shelf price?
Solution:
Retailer takes 40% gross margin, so retail = cost / (1 − 0.40) = \(45 / 0.6 = C\$75\).
Standardization vs Adaptation
Question 5
McDonald's selling the Big Mac globally but offering the McAloo Tikki in India is an example of:
Solution:
Glocalization means keeping a global core (brand, format, key products) while adapting locally (menu items, packaging, price).
Question 6
Which category of goods is MOST likely to benefit from standardization rather than adaptation?
Solution:
Industrial goods like machinery and semiconductors tend to standardize globally because buyers value performance and interoperability, not local taste. Consumer goods adapt more.
Pricing & Landed Cost
Question 7
A product has factory cost \(C\$10\), shipping/insurance \(C\$2\), and a destination duty of 15% on CIF value. Calculate the landed cost.
Solution:
CIF = \(10 + 2 = 12\). Duty = \(0.15 \times 12 = 1.80\). Landed = \(12 + 1.80 = C\$13.80\).
Question 8
A grey market arises when:
Solution:
Grey markets exploit cross-border price differences: buyers in cheaper markets resell in more expensive ones, bypassing the official channel.
Branding & Promotion
Question 9
In Japan, the colour WHITE often symbolizes:
Solution:
In Japan (and much of East Asia) white is associated with mourning, not purity. In the West, white is associated with weddings/purity.
Question 10
Why is a "linguistic and cultural audit" important when launching a brand in a new country?
Solution:
Literal translations can become embarrassing or offensive (e.g., Chevy Nova in Spanish-speaking markets). The audit catches problems before launch.
