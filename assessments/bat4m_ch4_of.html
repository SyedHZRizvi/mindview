Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
The five components of the COSO framework include Control Environment, Risk Assessment, Control Activities, and Monitoring. What is the fifth?
Solution:
Information & Communication is the fifth COSO component.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Which of the following is NOT one of the four "incompatible" duties to be segregated?
Solution:
The four duties are authorization, custody, recordkeeping, and reconciliation. Marketing is not one of them.
Question 3 [2 marks]
An auditor who is unable to obtain enough evidence to form an opinion issues a:
Solution:
A disclaimer means the auditor is unable to express any opinion due to a major scope limitation or independence issue.
Question 4 [2 marks]
The three legs of the fraud triangle are pressure, opportunity, and:
Solution:
The fraud triangle: Pressure + Opportunity + Rationalization.
Question 5 [2 marks]
An "audit committee" is composed primarily of:
Solution:
Per NI 52-110, the audit committee is composed of independent directors with at least one "financial expert."
Question 6 [3 marks]
A "skimming" fraud involves:
Solution:
Skimming = stealing cash before it is recorded; the books look normal because the cash never entered them.
