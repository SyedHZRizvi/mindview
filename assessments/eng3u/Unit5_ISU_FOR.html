Unit 5: ISU — Diagnostic / Proposal

Assessment FOR Learning · Strand B + C + A
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: This is your ISU proposal. Use the questions below to draft your topic, research question, working bibliography, and project timeline. Your teacher will return descriptive feedback before you begin researching in depth.
Question 1 Topic
[3 marks]
State your proposed ISU topic in one sentence. Then write 2–3 sentences explaining why this topic interests you enough to sustain six to eight weeks of work.
Question 2 Research Question
[3 marks]
Write your research question. A good ISU question can be answered three or four different ways, each of them defensible. If your draft can be answered yes/no in one sentence, revise it.
Question 3 Sources
[3 marks]
List three sources you plan to consult. For each, identify whether it is primary or secondary, the type of source (peer-reviewed article, book chapter, reputable news article, primary text), and one sentence on why this source is useful for your question.
Question 4 Sample Annotation
[3 marks]
Draft one full annotated-bibliography entry for any one source you have already begun reading. Include: (a) MLA citation, (b) 2–3-sentence summary of the source's argument, (c) one sentence on how this source will function in your essay (support, context, or a position you will contest).
Question 5 Timeline
[3 marks]
Sketch a six-to-eight-week timeline for your ISU. Identify what milestone you will hit each week (e.g., "Week 2: complete annotated bibliography of five sources"). If your timeline shows the first draft completed in the final 48 hours, revise it.
Question 6 Reflection
[ungraded]
What part of the ISU do you most expect to find challenging — topic focus, evaluating sources, MLA citation, writing at length, or the oral presentation? Explain.
