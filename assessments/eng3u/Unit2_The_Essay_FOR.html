Unit 2: The Essay — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your writing skills (thesis-building, paragraph structure, conventions) before starting Unit 2. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Skills · Thesis
[3 marks]
Identify which of the following is a thesis statement (and explain why the others are not): (a) "Macbeth is about ambition." (b) "Was Macbeth a victim?" (c) "Shakespeare presents Lady Macbeth's confidence in Act 1 as a borrowed strength that the play spends four acts undoing."
Question 2 Prereq · Paragraph
[3 marks]
Define each part of the PEEL paragraph structure: Point, Evidence, Explain, Link. Use one sentence per part.
Question 3 Skills · Topic Sentence
[3 marks]
Write a strong analytical topic sentence for an essay arguing that "In a short story you have read, the setting acts as a force on the characters rather than as a backdrop." You can use any short story you know.
Question 4 Prereq · Conventions
[3 marks]
Identify the error in each of the following sentences and rewrite each to repair it: (a) "Macbeth hesitates, Lady Macbeth pushes him forward." (b) "Because the witches plant the idea." (c) "Each character must guard their tongue."
Question 5 Skills · Forms
[3 marks]
Define and distinguish expository, persuasive, and personal essays. Write one short example thesis for each, on any topic of your choice.
Question 6 Knowledge
[2 marks]
What are Aristotle's three rhetorical appeals? Give a one-sentence definition of each.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 2 skill do you feel least confident about: thesis-writing, paragraph structure, choosing essay form, or revising and editing? Explain what is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
