Entry Modes & Channels
Question 1
Rank the following entry modes from LOWEST to HIGHEST capital commitment:
Solution:
Indirect exporting requires no foreign capital; FDI requires the most. Direct exporting is more committed than indirect; JV is more committed than direct exporting.
Question 2
A foreign DISTRIBUTOR differs from a foreign AGENT in that:
Solution:
A distributor buys, warehouses, and resells (takes title and inventory risk). An agent solicits orders on commission without taking title.
Supply Chain & Sourcing
Question 3
"Near-shoring" describes the practice of:
Solution:
Near-shoring shortens supply chains by locating suppliers in geographically/politically closer countries — reducing transport time, FX exposure, and disruption risk.
Question 4
A "lean" supply chain prioritizes:
Solution:
"Lean" minimizes inventory and cost — often relying on JIT and a small number of suppliers. The opposite philosophy is "resilient", which keeps redundancy.
Modes of Transport
Question 5
Roughly what share of global merchandise trade by VOLUME moves by ocean?
Solution:
Ocean shipping moves about 80% of global trade by volume. Air freight is ~1% by volume but ~35% by value.
Question 6
A "TEU" is:
Solution:
The TEU (Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit) is the standard unit for container volumes worldwide — a 40-foot container counts as 2 TEU.
Question 7
A wine case weighs 15 kg. Air freight quote: \(C\$2.50\)/kg. What is the air freight cost per case?
Solution:
\(15 \times 2.50 = C\$37.50\) per case.
Incoterms 2020
Question 8
Under EXW (Ex Works), the buyer is responsible for:
Solution:
EXW puts the maximum responsibility on the buyer: the seller only makes the goods available at its premises; the buyer arranges loading, export clearance, transport, and import.
Question 9
Under CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight), where does risk transfer from seller to buyer?
Solution:
Under CIF, seller pays freight + insurance to the destination port BUT risk passes at the origin port when goods are loaded on board the vessel. Cost and risk transfer at different points.
Question 10
Which Incoterm puts the MOST responsibility on the SELLER (delivery to buyer's location, import duties paid)?
Solution:
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) makes the seller responsible for delivery to the buyer's named place AND for import clearance and duties. Maximum seller obligation.
