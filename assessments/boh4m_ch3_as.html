Topic 3.1–3.2 — Communication Process & Channels
Question 1
Which is NOT a component of the Shannon-Weaver communication model?
Solution:
Components: sender, encoding, channel, receiver, decoding, feedback, noise. Profit margin is not part of the model.
Question 2
Communication that flows between coworkers at the same level is called:
Solution:
Peer-to-peer = horizontal (lateral).
Question 3
Which is the strongest predictor of "good listening"?
Solution:
Active listening centres on paraphrasing / reflection to verify understanding.
Topic 3.3–3.4 — Decision-Making
Question 4
Herbert Simon's concept of "bounded rationality" says that real-world decision-makers:
Solution:
Simon: limited cognition + time + information → satisficing (good-enough decisions).
Question 5
Irving Janis's "groupthink" describes:
Solution:
Groupthink = cohesive group prizes harmony over reality; symptoms include illusion of invulnerability, self-censorship.
Question 6
Which technique uses anonymous, iterative rounds of expert opinion to converge on a forecast?
Solution:
Delphi technique: anonymous, iterative; removes status effects.
Topic 3.5 — Conflict
Question 7
Thomas-Kilmann's "collaborating" style is characterized by:
Solution:
Collaborating = high/high — win/win, ideal for important issues with time.
Question 8
A staff member yells at a customer (safety/ethics issue). Which Thomas-Kilmann style is the best initial response?
Solution:
Safety / ethical violation: competing — assertive intervention, no negotiation. Coaching happens later.
Topic 3.6–3.7 — Stress & Change
Question 9
Kurt Lewin's three-step change model is:
Solution:
Lewin: Unfreeze → Change → Refreeze.
Question 10
Amy Edmondson's "psychological safety" is best defined as:
Solution:
Edmondson: psychological safety = feeling safe to speak up, ask questions, admit mistakes without retaliation.
