Kinetic Molecular Theory & Gas Laws
Question 1
According to KMT, increasing temperature of a gas at constant V increases the:
Solution:
T ∝ average kinetic energy (KMT). Particle mass/size do not change with T.
Question 2
A gas at 100 kPa and 2.0 L is compressed to 1.0 L at constant T. The new pressure is:
Solution:
Boyle's law: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ → P₂ = 100×2.0/1.0 = 200 kPa.
Question 3
A balloon at 27 °C has V = 1.00 L. At constant P, heated to 327 °C, the new volume (in L) is:
Solution:
Charles's law in K: T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 600 K. V₂ = V₁ × T₂/T₁ = 1.00 × 600/300 = 2.00 L.
Question 4
Which gas law relates P and T at constant V and n?
Solution:
Gay-Lussac's: P/T = const at constant V, n.
Ideal Gas Law & Stoichiometry
Question 5
At STP (0 °C, 101.325 kPa), the volume of 1.00 mol of any ideal gas is:
Solution:
Molar volume at STP = 22.4 L/mol.
Question 6
What volume does 4.0 mol of N₂ occupy at SATP (25 °C, 100 kPa, V_m = 24.8 L/mol)?
Solution:
V = 4.0 × 24.8 = 99.2 L.
Question 7
Apply PV=nRT (R=8.314 L·kPa/(mol·K)). Find n for 5.00 L of CO₂ at 25 °C and 100 kPa.
Solution:
n = PV/RT = (100)(5.00)/(8.314 × 298) = 500/2478 = 0.202 mol.
Question 8
For \( \text{2H}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{2H}_2\text{O} \), how many litres of H₂(g) at STP react with 11.2 L of O₂(g) at STP?
Solution:
Volume ratio at same P, T equals mole ratio (Avogadro's law). H₂:O₂ = 2:1 → 22.4 L H₂ : 11.2 L O₂.
Atmospheric Chemistry
Question 9
Which gas is the primary greenhouse gas of human concern (anthropogenic)?
Solution:
CO₂ is the dominant anthropogenic greenhouse-gas driver (rising from ~280 ppm pre-industrial to over 420 ppm today).
Question 10
Stratospheric ozone is destroyed catalytically by:
Solution:
UV-released Cl atoms from CFCs catalyze O₃ → O₂. One Cl can destroy thousands of O₃ molecules. Banned under the Montreal Protocol (1987).
Question 11
Major precursors to acid rain are:
Solution:
SO₂ (from coal/metal smelting) → H₂SO₄; NO_x (from combustion) → HNO₃.
Question 12
Atmospheric CO₂ is currently approximately:
Solution:
Pre-industrial ~280 ppm; today ~420 ppm (rising ~2.5 ppm/yr).
