Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Which financial statement covers a period of time (not a single date)?
Solution:
Income statement (and statement of owner's equity) covers a period; balance sheet is at a single point in time.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities. For \(CA = \$22\,000\) and \(CL = \$13\,500\):
Solution:
\(WC = 22\,000 - 13\,500 = 8\,500\).
Question 3 [2 marks]
If \(CA = \$30\,000\) and \(CL = \$12\,000\), the Current Ratio (to 2 decimals) is:
Solution:
\(30\,000 / 12\,000 = 2.50\). Healthy liquidity (above the 2:1 benchmark).
Question 4 [2 marks]
COGS = Beg Inv + Net Purchases − End Inv. For Beg Inv \$4 200, Net Purchases \$28 000, End Inv \$3 600:
Solution:
\(4\,200 + 28\,000 - 3\,600 = 28\,600\).
Question 5 [2 marks]
For Net Sales \$80 000 and COGS \$48 000, Gross Profit is:
Solution:
\(GP = 80\,000 - 48\,000 = 32\,000\). The gross-profit margin is 40%.
