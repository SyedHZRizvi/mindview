Topic 5.1 — 2-D Optimisation
Question 1
36 m of fencing is used for a rectangular pen. What dimensions maximise the area?
Solution:
P = 36 ⇒ each side = 36/4 = 9 m. The square maximises area: 9 × 9 = 81 m².
Question 2
For the previous question, what is the maximum area in m²?
Solution:
9 × 9 = 81 m².
Question 3
For a rectangle along a wall (3 sides fenced, total 24 m), the width that maximises area is:
Solution:
If width = w (perpendicular to wall), length = 24 - 2w. Area = w(24 - 2w). Max at w = 6 m, length 12 m.
Topic 5.2 — 3-D Optimisation
Question 4
For a closed cylinder with fixed volume, what shape minimises surface area?
Solution:
Optimal cylinder has h = 2r (height equals diameter).
Question 5
A closed box must hold 1000 cm³. The minimum-SA shape is:
Solution:
Optimal closed box is a cube. \( s^3 = 1000 \Rightarrow s = 10 \) cm.
Topic 5.3 — Scale Drawings
Question 6
On a 1:100 architectural plan, a room measures 5 cm × 4 cm. Find the actual floor area in m².
Solution:
Actual length = 5 × 100 = 500 cm = 5 m. Actual width = 4 × 100 = 400 cm = 4 m. Area = 5 × 4 = 20 m².
Question 7
A topographic map has scale 1:25 000. Two points appear 8 cm apart on the map. Find the actual distance in km.
Solution:
8 × 25 000 = 200 000 cm = 2000 m = 2 km.
Topic 5.5 — Composite Figures
Question 8
A silo is a cylinder (r = 2 m, h = 10 m) topped with a hemisphere of radius 2 m. Find the total volume (round to nearest integer m³).
Solution:
Cyl: \( \pi(4)(10) = 40\pi \). Hemisphere: \( \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{4}{3}\pi(8) = \tfrac{16}{3}\pi \). Total: \( 40\pi + 16\pi/3 = 136\pi/3 \approx 142 \).
Question 9
A roof truss has a span of 8 m and a rise of 3 m. Find the rafter length (rounded to 2 dp).
Solution:
Half-span = 4. Rafter = \( \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \) m.
Question 10
For the same truss, find the pitch angle in degrees (round to 1 dp).
Solution:
\( \tan\theta = 3/4 = 0.75 \Rightarrow \theta = \tan^{-1}(0.75) \approx 36.9° \).
