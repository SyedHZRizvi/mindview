HFN3M Chapter 2: Digestion & Metabolism — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Pre-chapter check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your prior knowledge of human anatomy and energy metabolism before starting Chapter 2.
Question 1 Prior · Anatomy
[4 marks]
List, in order, the organs of the GI tract from mouth to anus. Distinguish parts of the tube from accessory organs.
Question 2 Prior · Enzymes
[3 marks]
What is an enzyme? Give one example of a digestive enzyme and what it breaks down.
Question 3 Prior · Cells
[3 marks]
What does ATP stand for? Where in the cell is most ATP made? Why is it called the "cellular energy currency"?
Question 4 Prior · Energy
[3 marks]
Define basal metabolic rate (BMR) in your own words. Name two factors that increase BMR and one that decreases it.
Question 5 Skills · Calculation
[3 marks]
A 60 kg adolescent burns about 1 400 kcal/day at rest. If she eats 2 200 kcal in one day and her total daily energy expenditure is 2 000 kcal, will she gain, lose, or maintain weight? Explain.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
What is "the gut microbiome"? Name one beneficial role it plays in the colon.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 2 topic do you find most intimidating: GI anatomy, enzymes & digestion, the gut microbiome, cellular respiration, or BMR/TDEE? Explain why, and what learning strategy you will use to address it.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
