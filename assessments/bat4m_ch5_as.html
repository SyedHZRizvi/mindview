Topic 5.1 — Computerized Accounting
Question 1
The "chart of accounts" is best described as:
Solution:
The chart of accounts is the master list of every account, each with a unique account number. It is the spine of the accounting system.
Question 2
In a computerized system, when a sales invoice is created, the software:
Solution:
Integration is the defining strength of computerized accounting: one user action triggers multiple linked entries.
Topic 5.2 — IT Controls
Question 3
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) requires:
Solution:
MFA combines something you know (password) with something you have (phone) or are (biometric). Two passwords would still be one factor.
Topic 5.3 — GAAP, IFRS, ASPE
Question 4
A Canadian publicly traded bank is required to follow:
Solution:
Publicly accountable enterprises (including banks) must follow IFRS in Canada since 2011.
Question 5
ASPE is used by:
Solution:
ASPE = Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises. Charities use ASNPO; governments use PSAS; public companies use IFRS.
Topic 5.4 — CPA Code of Conduct
Question 6
Which is NOT one of the five fundamental principles of the CPA Code?
Solution:
The five principles: Integrity, Objectivity, Professional Competence & Due Care, Confidentiality, Professional Behaviour. Profitability is not one.
Question 7
A CPA who has a family relationship with a client faces what kind of threat?
Solution:
Familiarity threat — close relationships impair objectivity. Safeguard: recuse and assign a different partner; disclose to the audit committee.
Topic 5.5 — Ethical-Decision-Making
Question 8
In a five-step ethical-decision framework, identifying stakeholders is step:
Solution:
The five steps: (1) recognize, (2) identify stakeholders, (3) generate alternatives, (4) evaluate, (5) decide and document.
Topic 5.6 — Emerging Technology
Question 9
"Continuous auditing" refers to:
Solution:
Continuous auditing uses live data access to identify exceptions in real time, complementing or replacing periodic sampling.
