Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [10 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Identify the most likely family: \( y = 4, 7, 10, 13, 16 \) at \( x=0,1,2,3,4 \).
Solution:
First differences: 3, 3, 3, 3 — constant. Linear.
Question 2 [2 marks]
A regression line has \( y = 2.5x + 1.2 \) with \( r^{2} = 0.88 \). Predict \( y \) at \( x=10 \).
Solution:
\( y=2.5(10)+1.2=26.2 \).
