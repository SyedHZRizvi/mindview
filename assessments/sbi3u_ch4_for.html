Chapter 4: Animals — Structure and Function — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your existing knowledge of cells, tissues, and basic physiology before starting Ch 4. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Levels of organization
[3 marks]
Place the following terms in order from smallest to largest: organ, tissue, cell, organ system, organism, organelle.
Question 2 Prereq · Tissues
[3 marks]
Name the four types of animal tissue. For each, give one location in the human body and one function.
Question 3 Prereq · Diffusion
[3 marks]
Define diffusion. Use it to explain how oxygen gets from air into your bloodstream in the lungs.
Question 4 Prereq · Homeostasis
[3 marks]
What is homeostasis? Give one example of a variable that the human body keeps stable, and one mechanism that controls it.
Question 5 Prereq · Vocabulary
[3 marks]
For each pair, briefly state the difference: (a) arteries vs veins, (b) inhalation vs exhalation, (c) sensory vs motor neurons.
Question 6 Skills · Lab
[3 marks]
You want to test how heart rate responds to exercise. Identify the independent variable, dependent variable, and one variable you must control to make a fair comparison.
Question 7 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Briefly describe what happens to a sandwich during digestion in the following organs (one sentence each): mouth, stomach, small intestine.
Question 8 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which body system do you feel least confident about: (a) digestive, (b) circulatory, (c) respiratory, (d) excretory, or (e) nervous? Explain what specifically is unclear.
