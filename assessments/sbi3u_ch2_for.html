Chapter 2: Evolution — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify prior knowledge from Ch 1 (diversity, classification) and Grade 10 (genetics, ecosystems) before starting Ch 2 Evolution. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Diversity
[3 marks]
Define "species" using the biological species concept and explain one situation where this definition breaks down (give an example).
Question 2 Prereq · Genetics
[3 marks]
Define gene, allele, and chromosome. How do these terms relate to inherited variation in a population?
Question 3 Prior · Vocabulary
[3 marks]
Briefly explain what each of these terms means in everyday biology: (a) adaptation, (b) survival, (c) reproduction. Which one does "fitness" measure in evolutionary biology?
Question 4 Prereq · Ecosystems
[3 marks]
Give one example of a predator–prey relationship and one example of a competitive relationship in a Canadian ecosystem. Predict how a change in the prey's habitat could affect the predator.
Question 5 Misconception check
[3 marks]
Many people say "humans evolved from monkeys." Is this statement accurate? Use your existing knowledge to explain.
Question 6 Skills · Data
[3 marks]
In 1977, biologists Peter and Rosemary Grant measured beak depth in Galápagos finches before and after a drought. After the drought, average beak depth had increased. What kind of selection (directional, stabilising, or disruptive) might explain this pattern, and why?
Question 7 Skills · Variables
[3 marks]
Bacteria in a lab become resistant to an antibiotic over many generations. List the four conditions for natural selection that must all be met for this to happen.
Question 8 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which evolution topic do you feel least confident about: (a) natural selection mechanisms, (b) evidence for evolution, (c) speciation, or (d) Hardy–Weinberg calculations? Explain what specifically is unclear.
