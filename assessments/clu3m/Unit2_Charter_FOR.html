Chapter 2: Charter Rights and Freedoms — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you know about rights and freedoms before starting Chapter 2.
Question 1 Prereq · Concepts
[3 marks]
In your own words, what is the difference between a "right" and a "freedom"? Give an example of each from Canadian life.
Question 2 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Name three rights you believe people have under the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms. (We will verify the accuracy in Chapter 2.)
Question 3 Reasoning
[3 marks]
Should freedom of expression be absolute? Briefly explain — what kinds of expression (if any) do you think can be limited, and why?
Question 4 Skills · Reading
[3 marks]
"Every individual is equal before and under the law and has the right to the equal protection and equal benefit of the law without discrimination..." — Charter s. 15(1) (excerpt)
Paraphrase this section in your own words. Then identify one practical example of what "equal benefit of the law" might mean.
Question 5 Application
[3 marks]
A municipality bans wearing religious symbols by public-sector workers in positions of authority. Identify one Charter section this might engage and explain why.
Question 6 Knowledge
[2 marks]
Briefly explain what you understand by "discrimination" in a legal sense. How is it different from "different treatment"?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 2 topic do you feel least confident about: Charter architecture, fundamental freedoms, legal rights, equality, section 1 limits, or the notwithstanding clause? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
