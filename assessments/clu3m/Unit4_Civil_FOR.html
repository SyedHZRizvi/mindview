Chapter 4: Civil Law & Dispute Resolution — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Diagnose what you know about private law and dispute resolution before starting Chapter 4.
Question 1 Prereq
[3 marks]
In your own words, what is the difference between a criminal case and a civil case? Give one example of each.
Question 2 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Define "contract" in your own words. List three things you think need to be true for a contract to be binding.
Question 3 Reasoning
[3 marks]
A grocery store has not replaced the wet mat at the entrance on a snowy day. A customer slips and breaks her wrist. The store says it was not negligent because "everyone knows the floor gets wet in winter."
Do you think the store is liable to the customer? Why or why not?
Question 4 Application
[3 marks]
Suppose you buy a phone online. The phone arrives broken. What rights do you think you have as a consumer? List three.
Question 5 Knowledge
[3 marks]
In your own words, what is the difference between marriage and a "common-law" relationship? Should the legal treatment differ? Briefly explain.
Question 6 Concepts
[2 marks]
Why do most civil cases settle without going to trial? List two reasons.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 4 topic do you feel least confident about: tort law, contract law, consumer protection, employment law, family law, or ADR? Explain.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
