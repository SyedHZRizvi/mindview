Unit 3: Human-Physical Interactions — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about how humans interact with physical systems through agriculture, cities, hazards, and resource use.
Question 1 Prereq · Agriculture
[3 marks]
Name three different types of agricultural systems (e.g., subsistence farming) and one country or region where each is most common.
Question 2 Prereq · Urbanization
[3 marks]
What percentage of the world's population currently lives in cities? What percentage lived in cities in 1950?
Question 3 Prereq · Hazards
[3 marks]
Distinguish between a natural hazard and a natural disaster. Give one example of each in Canada in the past decade.
Question 4 Prereq · Resources
[3 marks]
Identify three natural resources Canada exports in large quantities. What is the difference between a renewable and a non-renewable resource?
Question 5 Skills · Mapping
[3 marks]
Identify three Canadian cities and one major land-use change each has experienced in the last 50 years (e.g., loss of farmland, deindustrialisation, gentrification).
Question 6 Knowledge · Footprint
[3 marks]
What is an "ecological footprint"? Roughly how does Canada's per-capita footprint compare with the global average?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Of the Unit 3 topics (agriculture, urbanization, hazards, resources, footprint, supply chains), which feels most personally relevant to you and why?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
