Unit 5: Geographic Issues & Inquiry — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about contemporary geographic issues and the geographic inquiry process.
Question 1 Prereq · Climate
[3 marks]
List three lines of physical evidence that Earth's climate is warming.
Question 2 Prereq · Water
[3 marks]
Name two world regions facing severe water scarcity and one Canadian water-related crisis you know about.
Question 3 Prereq · Forests
[3 marks]
Name two tropical regions where major deforestation is occurring. What main commodities are driving forest loss in each?
Question 4 Prereq · Justice
[3 marks]
In your own words, what is "environmental justice"? Give one example.
Question 5 Skills · Tools
[3 marks]
What is GIS (Geographic Information System)? Give one example of how it is used in geographic research or government.
Question 6 Knowledge · Inquiry
[3 marks]
Describe a question you would like to investigate for your culminating geographic-inquiry project. Why does it matter geographically?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 5 topic do you feel most engaged by, and what specifically draws your interest? Be honest.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
