Unit 2: Biomes & Ecosystems — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about biomes, food webs, nutrient cycles, and Canadian ecology before starting Unit 2. You will receive descriptive teacher feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Biome
[3 marks]
Define "biome" and give three examples of biomes found in different parts of the world.
Question 2 Prereq · Food chains
[3 marks]
Draw or describe a four-link food chain that could exist in southern Ontario. Identify the producer, primary consumer, secondary consumer, and tertiary consumer.
Question 3 Prereq · Cycles
[3 marks]
Name the four most important biogeochemical cycles. Which one has been most disrupted by humans through fossil-fuel burning?
Question 4 Prereq · Biodiversity
[3 marks]
Why does biodiversity matter? Give two specific examples of ecosystem services that depend on biodiversity.
Question 5 Skills · Map
[3 marks]
On a map of Canada (mental or sketched), identify three different ecozones and one distinctive feature of each.
Question 6 Knowledge · Energy
[3 marks]
Explain the "10% rule" in ecology. Why are top carnivores always less abundant than herbivores?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 2 topic do you feel least confident about? Be specific about what is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
