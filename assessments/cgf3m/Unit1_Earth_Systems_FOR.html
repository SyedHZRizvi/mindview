Unit 1: Earth Systems — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about Earth's spheres, energy balance, and physical processes before starting Unit 1. Be honest about what is unclear. You will receive descriptive teacher feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Spheres
[3 marks]
Name Earth's four physical spheres and give one biological or geographic example of each.
Question 2 Prereq · Energy
[3 marks]
What is the difference between weather and climate? Give one example of each.
Question 3 Prereq · Plates
[3 marks]
What evidence convinced 20th-century geologists that continents move? Name at least two lines of evidence.
Question 4 Prereq · Landforms
[3 marks]
Identify one example each of (a) a landform created by glaciation, (b) a landform created by river erosion, and (c) a landform created by tectonic uplift.
Question 5 Skills · Map Reading
[3 marks]
On a world map, identify by name three regions that lie under subtropical desert and explain in one sentence the atmospheric reason they are dry.
Question 6 Knowledge · Cycle
[3 marks]
Label or describe the main components of the hydrologic cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, infiltration, runoff. Where does most of Earth's water reside?
Question 7 Knowledge · Soils
[3 marks]
Sketch (or describe in words) the layers of a typical soil profile, from surface down to bedrock. Roughly how long does it take to form 1 cm of topsoil?
Question 8 Reflection
[ungraded]
Of the six Unit 1 topics (the four spheres; plate tectonics; weathering; atmospheric circulation; water cycle; soils), which two do you feel most confident about and which two least? Be specific.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
