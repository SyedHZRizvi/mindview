Question 1
Atmospheric CO₂ has risen from 280 ppm before 1750 to over 420 ppm today. The continuous CO₂ measurement at Mauna Loa is called the:
Solution: Charles David Keeling began the continuous CO₂ measurement at Mauna Loa Observatory in 1958. The Keeling Curve is the most-cited graph in modern climate science.
Question 2
The Paris Agreement (COP21, 2015) committed signatory countries to:
Solution: Paris (2015) committed to "holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2 °C above pre-industrial levels and pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5 °C." Current trajectories on stated policies are around 2.7 °C.
Question 3
The probability of seeing a 100-year flood at least once in a 30-year mortgage period is approximately:
Solution: P = 1 − (1−0.01)³⁰ = 1 − 0.74 ≈ 26%. Climate change is making historical floodplain estimates increasingly outdated; updating maps is a key adaptation priority.
Question 4
Physical water scarcity describes regions where:
Solution: Physical scarcity = inadequate supply (Middle East, U.S. Southwest, Sahel). Economic scarcity = infrastructure cannot deliver available water (much of sub-Saharan Africa). The two need different policy responses.
Question 5
The largest single driver of Amazon rainforest deforestation is:
Solution: About 80% of cleared Amazon land is converted directly or indirectly to cattle pasture. Soybean cultivation (often as cattle feed) is the second-largest driver. Brazil is the world's largest beef exporter.
Question 6
Africville (Halifax) and Grassy Narrows (English-Wabigoon River) are both examples of:
Solution: Africville: predominantly Black community demolished by Halifax in the 1960s after the city sited polluting infrastructure adjacent to it. Grassy Narrows: Asubpeeschoseewagong First Nation suffering multigenerational mercury poisoning from 1960s industrial dumping.
Question 7
If satellite pixels show near-infrared (NIR) reflectance 0.45 and red reflectance 0.08, what is the NDVI?
Solution: NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red) = (0.45 − 0.08) / (0.45 + 0.08) = 0.37 / 0.53 ≈ 0.70. Healthy vegetation typically gives 0.5–0.9; bare soil ≈ 0; water has negative values.
Question 8
Which is a free open-source GIS platform widely used in education and government?
Solution: QGIS (Quantum GIS) is the leading free open-source GIS. It can perform most of the spatial analyses available in commercial ArcGIS and runs on any operating system. The open-source nature has democratised geographic analysis.
Question 9
A well-formulated geographic question is:
Solution: Good geographic questions are spatially explicit ("where?" and "why there?"), tractable (answerable with available evidence), significant (worth answering), and open (not closing prematurely on a known answer).
Question 10
As of 2024, the IPCC remaining carbon budget for a 50% chance of staying below 1.5 °C from January 2020 was approximately:
Solution: IPCC AR6 estimated about 400 Gt CO₂ remaining from January 2020 for a 50% chance of staying below 1.5 °C. With global emissions at ~40 Gt/yr, the budget is exhausted in roughly 10 years on a steady-emissions trajectory.
