Unit 4: Sustainability — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about sustainability, renewable energy, conservation, and Indigenous stewardship.
Question 1 Prereq · Resources
[3 marks]
Distinguish among renewable, non-renewable, and flow resources. Give one example of each.
Question 2 Prereq · Definition
[3 marks]
In your own words, what does "sustainability" mean? Use one example to illustrate.
Question 3 Prereq · Energy
[3 marks]
Name three renewable energy sources. What is the main challenge with intermittent (variable) renewables like solar and wind?
Question 4 Prereq · Conservation
[3 marks]
Give one example each of (a) sustainable forestry, (b) overfishing, (c) sustainable water management. You may draw on any region.
Question 5 Skills · Maps
[3 marks]
Identify two Indigenous-led protected areas in Canada (by name and location). What was the lead Indigenous nation in each case?
Question 6 Knowledge · Cycle
[3 marks]
Explain the difference between a linear economy (extract-make-use-dispose) and a circular economy. Give one example of a circular-economy strategy.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
What does sustainability look like in your own household? List three things you or your family already do that reduce your footprint and one thing you could change.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
