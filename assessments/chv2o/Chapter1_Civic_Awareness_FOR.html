Chapter 1: Civic Awareness — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in prior civics knowledge (from elementary social studies and current-events awareness) before starting Chapter 1. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Vocabulary
[3 marks]
Define each term in your own words and give one Canadian example: (a) democracy, (b) rights, (c) responsibilities.
Question 2 Prereq · Levels of Government
[3 marks]
Name Canada's three levels of government and one responsibility you associate with each. (You will refine these in the chapter — do your best now.)
Question 3 Prereq · Charter
[3 marks]
Have you heard of the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms before? In one or two sentences, write what you know about it — what it does, when it was created, and one right you think it protects. (No penalty for not knowing — this question maps what you already bring.)
Question 4 Prereq · Elections
[3 marks]
In a federal election in Canada, voters in each riding vote for an individual candidate, not for a prime minister or for a party directly. Briefly explain how, then, Canada ends up with a prime minister.
Question 5 Skills · Critical Thinking
[4 marks]
"Free speech is great, but no one should be allowed to say things that hurt people's feelings."
A friend posts this online. Without taking a position yourself, write one paragraph (4–6 sentences) that identifies which democratic values are in tension in this claim, and which questions a careful civics analysis would ask before agreeing or disagreeing.
Question 6 Knowledge · Indigenous
[2 marks]
What does "treaty" mean in the Canadian context? Have you heard of a specific treaty in or near your community? Briefly explain.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which of these topics do you feel least confident about: (a) the structures of Canadian government, (b) the Charter of Rights and Freedoms, (c) how Canadian elections work, (d) civic ideals like equity and the common good? Explain what specifically is unclear so your teacher can prioritise it in Chapter 1.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
