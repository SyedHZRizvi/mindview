Chapter 2: Civic Engagement & Action — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Map your current understanding of civic action, advocacy, media, and Indigenous-Crown relations before Chapter 2. Teacher feedback only — no grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Civic Engagement
[3 marks]
List four ways (besides voting) that a Canadian citizen could engage in civic life. Briefly describe the goal of each.
Question 2 Movements
[3 marks]
Name a person or movement in Canadian history that you think made the country fairer, more inclusive, or more democratic. Explain in 2–3 sentences what they did and why it mattered.
Question 3 Media
[3 marks]
A friend forwards you a screenshot claiming that "1 in 4 Canadians believes the moon landing was fake," citing "research."
Without searching for the actual claim, list three things you would want to know before sharing the screenshot. (You will refine these in section 2.3 — do your best now.)
Question 4 Indigenous
[4 marks]
Have you heard of the Truth and Reconciliation Commission or the Calls to Action? In 2–4 sentences, write what you know about: (a) why the TRC was created, (b) one specific impact it has had on Canadian schools, society, or government policy. (No penalty for partial knowledge — this question maps where you are.)
Question 5 Community Partnership
[3 marks]
Imagine you want to start a project to support newcomers at your school. What is the first practical step you would take? Why? Who would you consult first, and why? (One paragraph.)
Question 6 Civic Issue
[3 marks]
Name one local, national, or global civic issue you personally care about. In 2–3 sentences, identify why it matters to you and who you think has the power to address it.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
What is your biggest worry about doing civic action — fear of speaking publicly, not knowing how the system works, not having time, not believing it will make a difference, or something else? Explain so your teacher can support you in the project ahead.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
