Topic 5.1 — Food Security
Question 1
Approximately what percentage of Canadian households experienced food insecurity in 2022 (Statistics Canada)?
Solution: ~18.4% in 2022 — about one in six households. Rates are dramatically higher in single-parent, Indigenous, Black-identifying, and Northern households.
Question 2
The four pillars of food security (FAO 1996) are availability, access, utilisation, and:
Solution: Availability + access + utilisation + stability. (Affordability and diversity are encompassed in the access and utilisation pillars.)
Topic 5.2 — Sustainability
Question 3
The global food system accounts for approximately what share of all human greenhouse-gas emissions (Poore & Nemecek 2018)?
Solution: ~26% of all GHG emissions. Animal-source foods dominate; beef alone emits ~30× more CO₂-equivalent per gram of protein than legumes.
Question 4
What percentage of food is wasted in the Canadian supply chain (estimated)?
Solution: ≈30–40% (Value Chain Management International, 2019). Households are a major contributor; "best before" misinterpretation drives much avoidable waste.
Topic 5.3 — Dietary Patterns
Question 5
The Mediterranean diet was shown in the 2013 PREDIMED trial to reduce major cardiovascular events by approximately:
Solution: ~30% reduction in major cardiovascular events vs control (PREDIMED, Estruch et al., NEJM 2013, updated 2018). The Mediterranean pattern has the strongest evidence of any dietary pattern for CVD prevention.
Question 6
The ketogenic ("keto") diet has the most established medical evidence for which condition?
Solution: The ketogenic diet has decades of evidence in refractory paediatric epilepsy. Weight-loss applications show short-term efficacy but uncertain long-term cardiovascular outcomes.
Topic 5.4–5.5 — Indigenous Foodways & Eating Disorders
Question 7
The "Three Sisters" of Indigenous companion planting in the eastern woodlands of what is now Ontario are:
Solution: Corn (maize) provides a stalk for beans to climb; beans fix nitrogen for the corn; squash leaves shade the soil. Together they form a nutritionally balanced, sustainable trio.
Question 8
Anorexia nervosa has the highest mortality rate of any psychiatric disorder. The approximate mortality is:
Solution: ~5–10% mortality. Major causes: cardiac complications and suicide. Early evidence-based treatment (family-based therapy for adolescents) markedly improves outcomes.
Question 9
First-line treatment for adolescent anorexia nervosa is:
Solution: Family-based therapy (Maudsley method) is first-line for adolescent anorexia. CBT-E is well-supported for bulimia and BED. Medication is adjunct, not first-line for anorexia.
Question 10
Which Canadian provincial legislation has restricted commercial advertising to children under 13 since 1980?
Solution: Quebec's Loi sur la protection du consommateur has banned commercial advertising to children <13 since 1980 — associated with lower fast-food consumption in Quebec children. Sabrina's Law (2005, Ontario) deals with anaphylaxis policies in schools.
Question 11
The EAT–Lancet 2019 "planetary-health diet" calls for limiting red meat to approximately:
Solution: The planetary-health diet caps red meat at ≤ 14 g/day (about 100 g/week) — modest amounts, consistent with both human-health and planetary-boundary considerations.
