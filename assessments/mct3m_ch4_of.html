Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Convert 0.0024 m to micrometres (μm).
Solution:
1 m = 10⁶ μm. \( 0.0024 \times 10^6 = 2400 \) μm.
Question 2 [3 marks]
A rectangular prism is 12 cm × 8 cm × 5 cm. Find its surface area.
Solution:
\( SA = 2(12 \cdot 8 + 12 \cdot 5 + 8 \cdot 5) = 2(96 + 60 + 40) = 2(196) = 392 \) cm².
Question 3 [3 marks]
A sphere has surface area \( 144\pi \) cm². Find its radius.
Solution:
\( SA = 4\pi r^2 = 144\pi \Rightarrow r^2 = 36 \Rightarrow r = 6 \) cm.
Question 4 [2 marks]
A cube has side 5 cm. Its surface area in cm² is:
Solution:
\( SA = 6s^2 = 6(25) = 150 \) cm².
Question 5 [2 marks]
Compute \( 12.45 \times 3.2 \) and report with the correct number of significant figures.
Solution:
Product is 39.84. Least sig figs is 2 (from 3.2). Round to 2 sig figs: 40.
