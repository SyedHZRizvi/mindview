Topic 4.1 — Plan Types
Question 1
A 5-year roadmap for entering the electric-vehicle market is best classified as a:
Solution:
Long-term, set by top management, fundamental direction = strategic plan.
Question 2
A "no shoes, no service" rule is an example of a:
Solution:
Recurring rule = standing plan. Programs and budgets = single-use plans.
Topic 4.2 — SWOT & SMART
Question 3
In a SWOT analysis, "rising consumer interest in plant-based food" is classified as:
Solution:
External + favourable = opportunity. Internal factors are strengths or weaknesses.
Question 4
Which of the following is NOT a SMART goal?
Solution:
"Be the best" lacks Specific, Measurable, Time-bound criteria — not a SMART goal.
Topic 4.3 — Structures
Question 5
An organization where each employee belongs to a function (e.g., engineering) AND a product team simultaneously, with two bosses, is using a:
Solution:
Dual reporting = matrix structure. Common in aerospace and professional services.
Question 6
"Span of control" refers to:
Solution:
Span of control = number of direct reports to one manager.
Topic 4.4–4.6 — Hiring, Training, Appraisal
Question 7
Which interview format generally has the highest predictive validity?
Solution:
Structured behavioural interviews with rubric (STAR method) double or triple predictive validity vs. unstructured.
Question 8
Which question would be ILLEGAL under the Ontario Human Rights Code?
Solution:
Family status is a protected ground. Asking about plans for children is forbidden regardless of intent.
Question 9
The 70-20-10 development model says that adults learn:
Solution:
70-20-10: 70% on the job (experience), 20% from others (mentor, coach), 10% formal training.
Question 10
In a 360-degree feedback process, ratings come from:
Solution:
360-degree = all directions: manager + peers + direct reports + (often) customers.
