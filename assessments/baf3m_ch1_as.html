Topic 1.1 — Purpose of Accounting
Question 1
Which of the following best describes accounting?
Solution:
Accounting is the systematic process of identifying, measuring, recording, summarising, and communicating the financial information of a business. Tax preparation is one application but not the full definition.
Question 2
A bank loan officer evaluating a small-business loan application is an example of which kind of user of accounting information?
Solution:
A bank is outside the business and relies on the published financial statements. That makes the loan officer an external user of financial accounting information.
Topic 1.2 — GAAP
Question 3
A landscaping company finishes a \$1 800 job on June 28 but does not collect cash until July 15. Under the revenue-recognition principle, in which month is the \$1 800 recorded as revenue?
Solution:
Revenue is recognised when earned (when the service is performed), not when cash is collected. The \$1 800 is June revenue with a debit to Accounts Receivable on June 28.
Question 4
The owner pays for a personal grocery purchase using the business credit card. Which GAAP is violated if the bookkeeper records the grocery purchase as a business expense?
Solution:
The business-entity principle requires that the business be treated as separate from the owner. A personal expense is recorded as a withdrawal (Drawings), not as a company expense.
Topic 1.3 — Account Categories
Question 5
Classify each account: Accounts Receivable, Bank Loan Payable, Service Revenue, Drawings, Supplies Expense, Equipment. (Provide the count of asset accounts.)
Solution:
Assets: Accounts Receivable, Equipment → 2. Liabilities: Bank Loan Payable → 1. Revenue: Service Revenue → 1. Drawings: 1. Expense: Supplies Expense → 1.
Question 6
Drawings are best classified as:
Solution:
Drawings reduce the owner's capital but are not part of the cost of earning revenue, so they are NOT an expense. They are a separate equity-reduction account.
Topic 1.4 — The Accounting Equation
Question 7
A business has Assets \$25 000 and Liabilities \$8 500. What is Owner's Equity?
Solution:
\( OE = A - L = 25\,000 - 8\,500 = 16\,500 \).
Question 8
A company purchases supplies for \$400 cash. What is the effect on the accounting equation?
Solution:
Supplies (asset) up \$400; Cash (asset) down \$400. Net change in Assets = 0. Liabilities and Equity unchanged.
Topic 1.5 — Debits & Credits
Question 9
Which set of accounts has a normal debit balance?
Solution:
Debit-normal accounts: Assets, Drawings, Expenses. Credit-normal accounts: Liabilities, Capital, Revenue. Remember "DEAD-CLER" (Dr: Expenses, Assets, Drawings; Cr: Liabilities, Equity, Revenue).
Question 10
A business performs services for \$650 on account. Which entry is correct?
Solution:
"On account" means the customer will pay later — Accounts Receivable (asset, ↑, Dr). Service Revenue (revenue, ↑, Cr).
Topic 1.6 — Transaction Analysis
Question 11
The owner withdraws \$500 cash for personal use. The correct entry is:
Solution:
Drawings (Dr-normal, increase = Dr 500); Cash (asset, decrease = Cr 500). Note: while owners' withdrawals could theoretically be debited directly to Capital, a separate Drawings account is preferred to show the activity during the period.
Question 12
A landscape firm purchases a \$3 200 ride-on mower, paying \$800 cash and signing a note for the balance. Total dollar value of debits in this entry equals:
Solution:
Dr. Equipment 3 200; Cr. Cash 800; Cr. Notes Payable 2 400. Debits total = \$3 200; credits total = \$800 + \$2 400 = \$3 200. ✓
