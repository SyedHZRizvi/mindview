Export Documents
Question 1
Which document is BOTH a contract of carriage AND a document of title (allowing whoever holds the original to claim the goods)?
Solution:
The ocean B/L acts as a contract of carriage AND as a document of title. The Air Waybill (AWB) is a receipt only. The invoice is a bill; the packing list is itemization.
Question 2
A certificate of origin matters MOST because:
Solution:
Preferential tariffs under CUSMA, CETA, CPTPP, etc., require valid proof of origin. Without it, the importer pays the full MFN tariff.
Letters of Credit & Payment
Question 3
In a letter of credit (L/C), the term "beneficiary" refers to the:
Solution:
Beneficiary = the seller who benefits from the L/C (gets paid). The applicant is the buyer. Issuing bank is the buyer's bank.
Question 4
Of these payment methods, which provides the GREATEST security for the seller?
Solution:
Cash in advance protects the seller fully — money is in hand before goods ship. The other methods carry varying risk to the seller.
Tariffs & Duties
Question 5
A 12% ad valorem tariff on a CIF value of \(C\$300\) is:
Solution:
Ad valorem = % of value. \(0.12 \times 300 = C\$36\).
Question 6
The "Harmonized System" (HS) is:
Solution:
The Harmonized System (HS) is the WCO-maintained 6-digit (extended by countries to 8/10 digits) global goods-classification used to determine duty rates and regulations.
Foreign Exchange
Question 7
An importer needs to pay EUR 50,000 to a European supplier. The bank quote is EUR/CAD: bid 1.450 / ask 1.460. What does the importer pay in CAD?
Solution:
To buy euros, the importer uses the ASK side: \(50{,}000 \times 1.460 = C\$73{,}000\).
Question 8
A "spot rate" is the rate for:
Solution:
Spot = immediate (T+1/T+2 settlement). Forward = at a future date locked in today.
FX Risk Management
Question 9
An exporter expects to receive EUR 200,000 in six months. To lock in today's CAD value, the BEST tool is:
Solution:
A forward contract to sell the expected EUR 200,000 at an agreed rate eliminates FX uncertainty. Option (c) leaves the exporter exposed; (a) doesn't work because the euros aren't received yet.
Question 10
Export Development Canada (EDC) primarily provides:
Solution:
EDC insures foreign accounts receivable, finances export deals, and provides political-risk coverage — supports that help Canadian SMEs sell abroad safely.
