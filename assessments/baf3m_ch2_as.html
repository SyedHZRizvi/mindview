Topic 2.1 — Source Documents
Question 1
A duplicate sales invoice supports which side of a journal entry?
Solution:
A sales invoice is issued when the company sells on account; the company will receive cash later, so Accounts Receivable is debited (asset, ↑).
Question 2
A bank service charge is documented by:
Solution:
The bank issues a debit memo to inform the depositor that the account has been debited for a service charge.
Topic 2.2 — General Journal Format
Question 3
In a two-column general journal, the credited account is:
Solution:
Convention: debited account flush left, credited account indented one centimetre. The explanation is indented further.
Question 4
When is the Posting Reference (P.R.) column filled in for a journal line?
Solution:
P.R. is filled in after posting so that an empty P.R. always reveals an unposted line.
Topic 2.3 — Compound Entries
Question 5
The owner invests \$3 000 cash and \$1 200 of office equipment to start a business. Total debits in the opening entry equal:
Solution:
Dr. Cash 3 000; Dr. Office Equipment 1 200; Cr. Capital 4 200. Total Dr = 4 200.
Question 6
A sale of \$900 is half cash, half on account. Which entry is correct?
Solution:
Cash (asset, ↑ \$450), A/R (asset, ↑ \$450), Sales Revenue (Cr ↑ \$900).
Topic 2.4 — Chart of Accounts & Ledger
Question 7
Which range of account numbers is typically reserved for liability accounts?
Solution:
100s assets; 200s liabilities; 300s equity; 400s revenue; 500s expenses.
Question 8
A three-column ledger account has a Debit column, a Credit column, and a:
Solution:
The third column is the running Balance, recomputed after every posting.
Topic 2.5 & 2.6 — Posting & Subsidiary Ledgers
Question 9
The A/R control account shows \$5 200 Dr. The subsidiary ledger totals: A. Tan \$1 800; B. Singh \$1 600; C. Lee \$1 700. Difference between control and subsidiary total:
Solution:
Subsidiary total: \(1\,800 + 1\,600 + 1\,700 = 5\,100\). Control: \$5 200. Difference \(5\,200 - 5\,100 = 100\). The bookkeeper must locate a \$100 posting that updated only one of the two ledgers.
Question 10
When a customer payment is received, which two ledgers are affected?
Solution:
The general-ledger A/R control is reduced and the specific customer's card in the subsidiary ledger is reduced, keeping the reconciliation intact.
