Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Expand: \( (x-3)(2x+1) \).
Solution:
\( 2x^{2}+x-6x-3=2x^{2}-5x-3 \).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Factor: \( x^{2}-9x+20 \).
Solution:
Two numbers multiplying to 20, summing to -9: -4 and -5. So \( (x-4)(x-5) \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
Find the remainder when \( f(x)=x^{4}-2x^{3}+3x-1 \) is divided by \( x-1 \).
Solution:
\( f(1)=1-2+3-1=1 \).
Question 4 [1 mark]
If \( f(-3)=0 \), then a factor of \( f(x) \) is:
Solution:
Factor Theorem: \( f(-3)=0 \Leftrightarrow x-(-3)=x+3 \) is a factor.
Question 5 [2 marks]
The solution to \( (x-2)(x+5)<0 \) is:
Solution:

Question 6 [3 marks]
Find the value of \( k \) so that \( x-1 \) is a factor of \( f(x)=2x^{3}+kx^{2}-5x+4 \).
Solution:
By the Factor Theorem, \( f(1)=0 \). Compute: \( f(1)=2+k-5+4=k+1=0 \Rightarrow k=-1 \).
