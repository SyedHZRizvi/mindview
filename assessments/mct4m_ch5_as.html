Topic 5.1 — Choosing a Function Family
Question 1
Data has constant 2nd differences. What family is it?
Solution:
Constant \( n \)-th differences ⇒ polynomial of degree \( n \). Here \( n=2 \): quadratic.
Question 2
Data points have outputs \( 3, 6, 12, 24, 48 \) at equally spaced \( x \). Which family?
Solution:
Ratios \( 6/3, 12/6, 24/12, 48/24 \) all equal 2 ⇒ exponential, base 2.
Topic 5.2 — Regression
Question 3
A linear regression has \( r^{2}=0.998 \). This means:
Solution:
\( r^{2} \) is the coefficient of determination — fraction of variation explained. 0.998 is excellent.
Question 4
Best practice for choosing a regression family is to:
Solution:
Always plot first. The scatter pattern strongly hints at the right family.
Topic 5.3 — Significant Figures & Error
Question 5
A measurement \( L=12.345 \) cm has 5 significant figures. Round to 3 sig figs.
Solution:
12.345 → 12.3 (3 sig figs).
Question 6
A part is specified at 10.00 mm; a measurement gives 10.04 mm. The percent error is:
Solution:
\( |10.04-10.00|/10.00 \times 100\% = 0.04/10 \times 100\% = 0.4\% \).
Topic 5.4 — Cross-Strand Problems
Question 7
Water drains from a tank with depth \( h(t)=6(0.9)^{t} \) (in metres, hours). When is the depth 3 m?
Solution:
\( 3=6(0.9)^{t} \Rightarrow (0.9)^{t}=0.5 \Rightarrow t=\log 0.5/\log 0.9 \approx -0.301/-0.0458 \approx 6.58 \) h.
Question 8
From 50 m away, the angle of elevation to a flag at the top of a tower is 35°. How tall is the tower?
Solution:
\( h=50\tan 35°\approx 50(0.7002)\approx 35.0 \) m.
