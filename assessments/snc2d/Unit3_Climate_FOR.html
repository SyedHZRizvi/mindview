Unit 3: Climate Change — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from SNC1D (atmosphere basics, ecosystems, energy transfer) before starting Unit 3.
Question 1 Prereq · Atmosphere
[3 marks]
List the three most abundant gases in dry air and approximate percentages. Which (if any) are greenhouse gases?
Question 2 Prereq · Energy from sun
[3 marks]
Describe what happens to sunlight when it reaches Earth: list at least three things (reflection, absorption, etc.) and roughly what fraction is involved in each.
Question 3 Prereq · Photosynthesis
[3 marks]
Write the balanced equation for photosynthesis. Explain why this process is relevant to atmospheric \(CO_2\) levels.
Question 4 Prereq · Climate factors
[3 marks]
Explain why Vancouver, BC (49° N, on the Pacific) has milder winters than Winnipeg, MB (50° N, on the Prairies). List at least two factors.
Question 5 Skills · Graph
[3 marks]
A graph shows atmospheric \(CO_2\) at Mauna Loa from 1958 to 2024. (a) What is the overall trend? (b) Why are there small seasonal sawtooth oscillations? (c) State the approximate values at the start and end of the graph.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Define and give one example each: (a) renewable energy, (b) fossil fuel, (c) carbon footprint.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 3 topic do you feel least confident about: greenhouse effect mechanism, carbon cycle, or evaluating climate-policy options? Explain what is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
