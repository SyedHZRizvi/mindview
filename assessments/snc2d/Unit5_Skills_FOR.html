Unit 5: Integration & Skills — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand A
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Reflect on the scientific skills, safety knowledge and STSE thinking practiced across all four content units.
Question 1 Skills · Method
[3 marks]
List, in order, at least five steps of the scientific method. Briefly explain why each step matters.
Question 2 Skills · Variables
[3 marks]
In your own words, define independent variable, dependent variable, and controlled variable. Give an example from one of the experiments you have done this year.
Question 3 Safety · WHMIS
[3 marks]
Sketch (describe) the WHMIS pictograms for: (a) flammable, (b) corrosive, (c) acute toxicity. State what each warns you to do.
Question 4 Data
[3 marks]
Express the following with the indicated significant figures: (a) 0.045230 to 3 sf, (b) 102345 to 4 sf, (c) (4.5 × 2.0) to the appropriate number of sf.
Question 5 STSE
[3 marks]
Choose one STSE issue you encountered in Units 1–4 (e.g., stem cells, acid rain, climate policy, laser safety). Briefly outline a scientific consideration and an ethical consideration.
Question 6 Career
[3 marks]
Name three careers in science that interest you. For each, identify whether it typically requires college, university, or graduate/professional training.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Of all skills covered in SNC2D — experimental design, lab safety, data analysis, scientific writing — which do you feel you've grown the most in? Which still needs the most work?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
