Unit 2: Chemical Reactions — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from SNC1D (matter, periodic table, simple formulas) before starting Unit 2. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Periodic Table
[3 marks]
For potassium (K), give: (a) atomic number, (b) group number, (c) the charge of its most common ion.
Question 2 Prereq · Subatomic particles
[3 marks]
Name the three subatomic particles, state where each is located in an atom, and give the charge of each.
Question 3 Prereq · Formula
[3 marks]
How many atoms of each element are in one formula unit of \(Ca(NO_3)_2\)? Show how you counted.
Question 4 Prereq · Conservation of mass
[3 marks]
State the law of conservation of mass. Why does it require chemical equations to be balanced?
Question 5 Skills · Lab
[3 marks]
In a lab to test how temperature affects the rate of a reaction between zinc and HCl, identify (a) one independent variable, (b) one dependent variable, (c) one controlled variable.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Distinguish between a physical change and a chemical change. Give one example of each.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 2 topic do you feel least confident about: atomic structure / Bohr diagrams, naming compounds, or balancing equations? Explain what is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
