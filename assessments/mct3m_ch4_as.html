Topic 4.1–4.2 — SI Units & Precision
Question 1
Convert 3.5 km to centimetres.
Solution:
1 km = 10⁵ cm. So 3.5 km = 3.5 × 10⁵ = 350 000 cm.
Question 2
How many significant figures in 0.0420?
Solution:
Leading zeros not significant. Three sig figs: 4, 2, and trailing 0 (after the decimal).
Question 3
Convert 250 mg to kg.
Solution:
1 kg = 10⁶ mg. 250 mg ÷ 10⁶ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ = 0.000 25 kg.
Topic 4.3 — Surface Area & Volume
Question 4
A cylinder has radius 5 cm and height 12 cm. Find its volume (round to nearest integer).
Solution:
\( V = \pi r^2 h = \pi (25)(12) = 300\pi \approx 942 \) cm³.
Question 5
A sphere has radius 3 m. Find its volume (round to nearest integer).
Solution:
\( V = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi (27) = 36\pi \approx 113 \) m³.
Question 6
A cone has radius 4 cm and slant height 5 cm. Find its lateral surface area (round to 1 dp).
Solution:
Lateral SA = \( \pi r s = \pi(4)(5) = 20\pi \approx 62.8 \) cm².
Topic 4.4 — Similar Figures
Question 7
Two similar cubes have side lengths in the ratio 2:5. The ratio of their volumes is:
Solution:
Volume ratio = (linear ratio)³ = \( (2/5)^3 = 8/125 \).
Question 8
A scale model is 1/100 the size of an actual building. If 0.5 m² of surface area on the model is painted, find the equivalent area on the actual building.
Solution:
Area scale = 100² = 10 000. 0.5 × 10 000 = 5000 m².
Topic 4.5 — 3-D Trigonometry
Question 9
A box has dimensions 3 × 4 × 12. Find the body diagonal.
Solution:
\( d = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13 \).
Question 10
A square-based pyramid has base side 8 m and slant height 5 m. Find its vertical height.
Solution:
Half-base = 4. Slant 5, half-base 4, height \( h \): \( h = \sqrt{5^2 - 4^2} = \sqrt{25 - 16} = 3 \).
