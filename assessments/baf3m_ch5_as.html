Topic 5.1 — Internal Control
Question 1
"Segregation of duties" means:
Solution:
The three functions (authorise / custody / record) must be in different hands so no single employee can both commit and conceal an error or fraud.
Question 2
Which is an example of a physical internal control?
Solution:
Physical controls include safes, locks, alarms, and security cameras — devices that protect physical assets.
Topic 5.2 — Bank Reconciliation
Question 3
An outstanding cheque is added to or subtracted from which balance?
Solution:
Outstanding cheques have not yet been processed by the bank, so the bank balance is too high. Subtract them on the bank side.
Question 4
A \$15 bank service charge appears on the bank statement but has not been recorded by the company. After reconciliation, the company's journal entry is:
Solution:
Service charge is an expense; cash decreased.
Topic 5.3 — Petty Cash
Question 5
Petty-cash fund \$150. At month-end, vouchers total \$110 and cash on hand is \$40. The replenishment cheque is for:
Solution:
Cheque brings cash from \$40 back to \$150, so cheque = \$110. The Dr. is to the expense accounts on the vouchers; the Cr. is to Cash (the bank).
Question 6
During replenishment, the Petty Cash account is:
Solution:
The Petty Cash asset account changes only when establishing, increasing, or decreasing the fund. Replenishment debits expenses and credits Cash.
Topic 5.4 — Ethics & the CPA Code
Question 7
Which is NOT one of the five core values of the CPA Code of Professional Conduct?
Solution:
The five core values are integrity, due care, objectivity, professional competence, confidentiality. Profitability is a business goal, not a CPA-Code value.
Question 8
An accountant's supervisor instructs her to record some delivery orders as revenue before the goods ship. Which GAAP and/or CPA-Code value is being violated?
Solution:
Revenue must be earned (delivery) before recognition; reporting prematurely violates revenue-recognition. Deliberately doing so violates integrity and objectivity.
Topic 5.5–5.6 — Users & IT
Question 9
Which of the following is an external user of financial information?
Solution:
The bank is outside the business. Production manager, marketing director, and owner are inside.
Question 10
A new internal-control risk introduced by computerised accounting (compared with manual accounting) is:
Solution:
Computerised systems eliminate arithmetic errors but introduce hacking, ransomware, and unauthorised-access risks.
