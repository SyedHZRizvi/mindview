Chapter 1: Diversity of Living Things — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your Grade 10 knowledge of life processes, basic cell biology, and ecosystems before starting Chapter 1. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Life characteristics
[3 marks]
List five characteristics shared by all living things. For each, give one example of a process or structure that fulfils it.
Question 2 Prereq · Cells
[3 marks]
State one similarity and two differences between prokaryotic cells (e.g., bacteria) and eukaryotic cells (e.g., a human cheek cell).
Question 3 Prereq · Microscope
[3 marks]
A compound light microscope has 10× ocular lenses and a 40× objective lens engaged. What is the total magnification? What is one limit of the light microscope when viewing bacteria?
Question 4 Prereq · Ecosystems
[3 marks]
Define a producer, a primary consumer, and a decomposer, and give one organism for each category found in a Canadian forest ecosystem.
Question 5 Skills · Variables
[3 marks]
You want to compare the biodiversity of two patches of woodland — one young (~10 years) and one old-growth (~150 years). Identify one independent variable, one dependent variable, and one variable that must be controlled.
Question 6 Knowledge
[2 marks]
Carl Linnaeus introduced the binomial naming system in the 1700s. Write the scientific name for the modern human, with the correct capitalisation and formatting (the genus and species).
Question 7 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Briefly describe one defining characteristic for each of: kingdom Animalia, kingdom Plantae, and kingdom Fungi.
Question 8 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which area do you feel least confident about: (a) classifying organisms into kingdoms, (b) reading or constructing phylogenetic trees, (c) measuring biodiversity, or (d) human impacts on biodiversity? Explain what specifically is unclear.

📝 Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback here after reviewing your responses.
