Unit 4: Local Food Systems — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Find out what you already know about Canadian food production, labels, food security, and Indigenous food systems before starting Unit 4.
Question 1 Concept · Farm to Table
[3 marks]
Trace the steps between an apple growing on a tree in Ontario and arriving on a Toronto grocery shelf. Name at least four steps.
Question 2 Concept · Organic vs Conventional
[3 marks]
In your own words, explain how "organic" produce differs from "conventional" produce. Is organic always healthier or more environmentally friendly?
Question 3 Concept · GMOs
[3 marks]
What do you understand by "GMO"? Identify one common GM food and one reason genetic modification is used in agriculture.
Question 4 Skills · Labels
[3 marks]
When you pick up a packaged food, what three pieces of information do you currently look at on the label, and why?
Question 5 Concept · Food Insecurity
[3 marks]
In your own words, what does "food insecurity" mean? Do you think it is a problem in Canada? Explain why or why not.
Question 6 Concept · Indigenous Food
[3 marks]
Name two foods that are part of Indigenous (First Nations, Inuit, or Métis) food traditions in Canada. What does the phrase "food sovereignty" make you think about?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which topic in Unit 4 (food production, GMOs/pesticides, labels, food security, Indigenous food sovereignty) do you most want to understand better — and why?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
