Unit 5: Global Hunger & Food Security — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Find out where you stand on global hunger, climate-food connections, sustainability, and ethics before starting Unit 5.
Question 1 Concept · Hunger
[3 marks]
What do you think is the main reason hundreds of millions of people are hungry, when the world produces enough food? Briefly explain your reasoning.
Question 2 Concept · Malnutrition
[3 marks]
List three different forms of malnutrition. Are they all about "not enough food"?
Question 3 Concept · Climate
[3 marks]
Name two ways climate change is already affecting food production, and one way the food system contributes to climate change.
Question 4 Skills · Food Footprint
[3 marks]
Rank these foods from highest to lowest carbon footprint per kilogram: chicken, beef, lentils, cheese. Brief reasoning.
Question 5 Concept · SDGs
[3 marks]
Have you heard of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)? Name one related to food and one other goal. By what year were the SDGs set to be achieved?
Question 6 Concept · Ethics
[3 marks]
When you choose what to eat, do ethics ever come into the decision (e.g., animal welfare, fair trade, climate)? Briefly describe.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 5 topic — global hunger, climate-food, sustainability, food aid/SDGs, or ethical food choices — would you most like to dive into? What questions do you have?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
