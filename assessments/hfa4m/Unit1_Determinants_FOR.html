Unit 1: Determinants of Nutritional Health — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps before starting Unit 1 — what you remember from earlier health/food courses, and how comfortable you are with reading food labels, calculating kilocalories, and recognising nutrient classes. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Nutrient Classes
[3 marks]
List the six classes of nutrients and identify which provide energy (kilocalories) and which do not.
Question 2 Prereq · Calculation
[3 marks]
A snack contains 15 g carbohydrate, 3 g protein, and 7 g fat. Show step-by-step how you would calculate the total kilocalories.
Question 3 Prereq · Digestion
[3 marks]
Describe what happens to a piece of bread between your mouth and your bloodstream. Use the words "amylase," "small intestine," and "absorption."
Question 4 Skills · Label Reading
[3 marks]
When you look at a Canadian Nutrition Facts table, what does "%DV" mean, and what is a useful rule of thumb for whether a number is "a little" or "a lot"?
Question 5 Concept · Energy Balance
[3 marks]
Explain "energy balance" in your own words. What are the three main components of "energy out"?
Question 6 Concept · Food Choices
[3 marks]
Name three factors — drawn from different categories (biological, economic, social, cultural, environmental) — that influence what you ate yesterday.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 1 topic do you feel least confident about — nutrient classes, digestion/metabolism, food labelling, or Canada's Food Guide? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
