Topic 5.1 — Global Hunger & Malnutrition
Question 1
According to the 2024 UN SOFI report, approximately how many people worldwide face chronic hunger?
Solution: About 733 million people (1 in 11 globally; 1 in 5 in Africa) faced chronic hunger in 2023 per the 2024 SOFI report.
Question 2
Stunting in children primarily reflects:
Solution: Stunting (low height-for-age) reflects chronic undernutrition during the critical first 1000 days. Wasting (low weight-for-height) reflects acute undernutrition.
Question 3
"Hidden hunger" refers to:
Solution: Hidden hunger = adequate kilocalories but deficient in iron, vitamin A, iodine, zinc, or other micronutrients. Affects ~2 billion people globally.
Topic 5.2 — Climate Change & Food
Question 4
According to the IPCC, food systems contribute roughly what percentage of global anthropogenic greenhouse-gas emissions?
Solution: IPCC (2019, 2023): food systems contribute about 21–37% of global GHG emissions when production, land use, processing, transport, retail, consumption, and waste are counted.
Question 5
Which food has the HIGHEST greenhouse-gas footprint per kg according to Poore & Nemecek (Science 2018)?
Solution: Beef (~60 kg CO₂eq/kg) tops the chart, driven by enteric methane (cattle digestion) and land use. Plant foods like lentils are roughly 1 kg CO₂eq/kg — about 60× lower.
Topic 5.3 — Food Waste & Sustainability
Question 6
Globally, approximately what percentage of food available at retail and consumer levels is wasted (UNEP Food Waste Index 2024)?
Solution: About 19% of food at retail and consumer levels is wasted globally (UNEP Food Waste Index 2024) — roughly 1.05 billion tonnes/year. Canadian estimates of total food loss + waste reach 58% across the supply chain.
Question 7
If food waste were a country, it would be the third-largest emitter of greenhouse gases after:
Solution: Food waste's emissions footprint would rank third behind China and the US. Reducing food waste is among the highest-leverage climate actions individuals and food systems can take.
Topic 5.4 — SDGs & Food Aid
Question 8
SDG 2 ("Zero Hunger") was adopted by all UN member states in:
Solution: The 17 SDGs were adopted in 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. SDG 2 aims to end hunger by 2030 — but global progress has stalled.
Question 9
Cash transfers are increasingly preferred over in-kind food aid because they:
Solution: Cash transfers (when markets function and access is safe) are typically more cost-effective and dignifying, and support local food markets and farmer livelihoods. In-kind food remains essential during acute supply disruption.
Topic 5.5 — Ethical Food Choices
Question 10
Fair Trade certification primarily addresses:
Solution: Fair Trade certification (coffee, cocoa, bananas, tea, sugar) sets minimum prices, community premiums, and prohibits child/forced labour — improving income and conditions for farmers in low- and middle-income countries.
