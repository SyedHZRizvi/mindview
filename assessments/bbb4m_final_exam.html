Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [25 marks]
Question 1 [1 mark]
Approximately what trade-to-GDP ratio does Canada have today?
Solution:
Canada's trade-to-GDP ratio is around 60–65%, far higher than the US or Japan.
Question 2 [1 mark]
CUSMA replaced NAFTA in:
Solution:
CUSMA/USMCA took effect on 1 July 2020.
Question 3 [1 mark]
Comparative advantage is measured by:
Solution:
Comparative advantage = lower opportunity cost. Absolute advantage looks at raw productivity.
Question 4 [1 mark]
Hofstede's "individualism" dimension is HIGH in:
Solution:
Western Anglo cultures score highest on individualism. East Asian cultures lean collectivist.
Question 5 [1 mark]
A free-trade area differs from a customs union in that the customs union ALSO has:
Solution:
FTA removes internal tariffs. Customs union adds a common external tariff. Common market also has free factor movement. Economic union adds harmonized policy.
Question 6 [1 mark]
"Glocalization" means:
Solution:
"Think global, act local". McDonald's, IKEA, and Coca-Cola are classic glocal firms.
Question 7 [1 mark]
Penetration pricing means:
Solution:
Penetration = low introductory price for share. Skimming = high initial price.
Question 8 [1 mark]
Air freight handles approximately what share of world cargo BY VALUE?
Solution:
Air = ~1% by volume but ~35% by value (high-value, time-sensitive goods).
Question 9 [1 mark]
Under FOB (Free On Board), risk transfers from seller to buyer when:
Solution:
Under FOB, risk passes once goods are on board the vessel at origin. FOB is only used for sea/inland-waterway transport.
Question 10 [1 mark]
Which Incoterm puts MAXIMUM responsibility on the BUYER?
Solution:
EXW (Ex Works) — the buyer does everything from the seller's premises onward.
Question 11 [1 mark]
Under a letter of credit, the "issuing bank" is the:
Solution:
The issuing bank acts on behalf of the buyer (applicant). The advising/confirming bank is on the seller's side.
Question 12 [1 mark]
A 10% ad valorem tariff on a CIF value of \(C\$5{,}000\) is:
Solution:
\(0.10 \times 5{,}000 = C\$500\).
Question 13 [1 mark]
EUR/CAD rate is 1.45. To pay EUR 10,000 in CAD costs:
Solution:
\(10{,}000 \times 1.45 = C\$14{,}500\).
Question 14 [1 mark]
A spot transaction settles in:
Solution:
Spot FX trades settle T+1 or T+2 (one or two business days), not literally instantly.
Question 15 [1 mark]
EDC stands for:
Solution:
Export Development Canada — Canada's export-credit and political-risk insurer for Canadian exporters.
Question 16 [1 mark]
Hofstede's "Power Distance Index" tends to be HIGH in:
Solution:
High-PDI cultures (Mexico, Saudi, China) accept hierarchical authority more than low-PDI cultures (Scandinavia, Canada, Israel).
Question 17 [1 mark]
"FCPA" is the abbreviation for the:
Solution:
FCPA = US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, the US equivalent of Canada's CFPOA.
Question 18 [1 mark]
The triple bottom line measures performance on:
Solution:
John Elkington's three-dimensional view of corporate performance: social, environmental, financial.
Question 19 [1 mark]
The U-curve of cultural adjustment has its lowest point during:
Solution:
The "dip" of the U-curve is culture shock, typically 3–9 months in.
Question 20 [1 mark]
CITP is awarded by:
Solution:
FITT issues the CITP®|FIBP® designation for international trade professionals.
