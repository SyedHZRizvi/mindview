Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
An adjusting entry to record supplies used involves:
Solution:
Supplies used reduces the asset and increases the expense.
Question 2 [2 marks]
A trial balance is out by \$72. Most likely cause:
Solution:
\(72 \div 9 = 8\), divisible by 9 → transposition or slide.
Question 3 [2 marks]
Closing entries transfer net income to:
Solution:
Income Summary is a temporary clearing account; its balance is closed to Capital so Capital reflects the period's earnings.
Question 4 [2 marks]
Equipment costs \$24 000, residual \$4 000, useful life 5 years. Compute one year's straight-line depreciation.
Solution:
\( \dfrac{24\,000-4\,000}{5} = \dfrac{20\,000}{5} = 4\,000 \).
Question 5 [2 marks]
An entity receives \$1 200 cash on 1 March for 12 months of advertising service. On 31 March, the amount that should be in Unearned Revenue is:
Solution:
One month earned = \$100; remaining unearned = \(1\,200 - 100 = 1\,100\).
