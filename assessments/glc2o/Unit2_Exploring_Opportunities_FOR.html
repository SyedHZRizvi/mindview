Unit 2: Exploring Opportunities — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Surface what you already know about postsecondary destinations, the labour market, OYAP/trades, and entrepreneurship before Unit 2 instruction. Be honest — gaps here let your teacher target instruction where it actually helps.
Question 1 Pathways
[3 marks]
Name three different postsecondary destinations available to Ontario students after Grade 12. For each, write one sentence about what it typically leads to.
Question 2 Research
[2 marks]
Name one Canadian or Ontario government website you would use to research career and wage information.
Question 3 Trades
[3 marks]
An apprenticeship is different from a college diploma. In your own words, name two ways they differ (think about how training is delivered, how it is paid for, and what the end credential is).
Question 4 Labour Market
[3 marks]
A relative tells you: "All the good jobs require a university degree."
Do you agree with this statement? In 2–3 sentences, say why or why not, and give one example (real or hypothetical).
Question 5 Entrepreneurship
[2 marks]
If you ran a small business at age 16, what would you sell or do? In two sentences, describe what you would offer and who your first customer might be.
Question 6 Community Involvement
[3 marks]
The Ontario OSSD requires 40 hours of community involvement before graduation. List two activities you have done (or would consider doing) that could count toward this requirement.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
What part of Unit 2 are you most uncertain about going in: how to research careers, labour-market trends, postsecondary destinations, OYAP/trades, entrepreneurship, or community involvement? Why?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
