Unit 1: Knowing Yourself — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand A
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Help your teacher (and you) identify what you already know about self-assessment and what is unfamiliar, before Unit 1 instruction begins. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest — this works only if you write what you genuinely think.
Question 1 Self-Knowledge
[3 marks]
List five words or short phrases that describe you as a learner and as a person. (E.g., "patient," "quick to get bored," "good with numbers.") There are no wrong answers.
Question 2 Interests
[3 marks]
Name three activities (in school, after school, online, or at home) where you lose track of time because you're engaged. What do those three activities have in common, if anything?
Question 3 Skills
[3 marks]
Name one skill you have built recently (in school, at work, in volunteering, in a hobby). Give one specific piece of evidence that you have this skill — what did you do that demonstrates it?
Question 4 Values
[3 marks]
What three things matter most to you in a future job? (For example: helping others, making a lot of money, time with family, doing creative work, being respected, having stable hours, learning constantly.) Order them from most to least important.
Question 5 Career Awareness
[3 marks]
Name two careers you are currently curious about. For each, write one sentence about why it interests you.
Question 6 Mindset
[3 marks]
A student writes after failing a quiz: "I'm just not a math person. Why bother trying — I'll never get this."
In two or three sentences, rewrite the student's thought as a growth-mindset response. What would the new thought sound like, and what would the student do next?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
What part of Unit 1 are you most uncertain about going in: RIASEC and interest types, identifying your skills, naming your values, personality, or growth mindset? One sentence is enough.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
