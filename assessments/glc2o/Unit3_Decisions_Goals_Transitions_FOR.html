Unit 3: Decisions, Goals, Transitions — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strands C & D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Surface what you already know about decision-making, goals, résumés, interviews, money, and transitions before Unit 3 instruction. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest — gaps here help your teacher target instruction.
Question 1 Decisions
[3 marks]
Describe a decision you have made recently (school course choice, a friendship, a purchase, a sport). In 3–4 sentences, explain how you made it — what information did you use, who did you talk to, and how did you weigh the options?
Question 2 Goals
[3 marks]
Name one short-term goal (this term) and one long-term goal (one or more years away) related to school, work, or life. For each, describe what "success" would look like.
Question 3 Résumé
[3 marks]
Have you ever written a résumé? If yes, what is on it (briefly)? If no, what experiences (paid work, school projects, volunteering, sports, clubs) could you put on a first résumé?
Question 4 Interviews
[3 marks]
An interviewer asks: "Tell me about a time you worked on a team."
In 4–6 sentences, write your best draft answer — even if you've never had an interview. Try to include a specific situation, what you did, and what happened.
Question 5 Money
[3 marks]
Do you currently track what you spend each week? If yes, how? If no, what categories of spending do you think you would have if you started? (Examples: food, transit, entertainment, subscriptions, savings, gifts.)
Question 6 Transitions
[3 marks]
Name one big change you have already lived through (changing schools, moving cities, starting/leaving a sport, family change, an injury, immigrating to Canada). What did you learn from how that change went, and what would you do differently next time?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which part of Unit 3 are you most nervous about — decisions, goals, résumé writing, interviewing, money, or planning transitions? Why?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
