Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Which entry mode involves the HIGHEST capital commitment and the HIGHEST control?
Solution:
FDI/wholly-owned subsidiary requires the most capital and gives the most control. It is also the hardest to exit.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Under which Incoterm does the SELLER pay the freight to the destination port but risk passes at the ORIGIN port?
Solution:
CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) — seller pays freight + insurance to destination port; risk transfers when goods are loaded on the vessel at origin.
Question 3 [2 marks]
A freight forwarder primarily:
Solution:
Freight forwarders are logistics agents who arrange transport with carriers. Customs brokers (different role) handle HS classification and customs paperwork.
Question 4 [2 marks]
A "3PL" is a:
Solution:
3PL = Third-Party Logistics provider — handles warehousing, picking/packing, distribution, and returns on behalf of a client.
Question 5 [2 marks]
A 40-foot container is equivalent to how many TEUs?
Solution:
1 TEU = 20-foot container. A 40-foot container = 2 TEU.
Question 6 [3 marks]
A shipment from Vietnam to Halifax has unit cost \(C\$22\), ocean freight \(C\$2.50\)/unit, and 6% duty on (unit cost + freight). Calculate the landed unit cost.
Solution:
CIF = \(22 + 2.50 = 24.50\). Duty = \(0.06 \times 24.50 = 1.47\). Landed = \(24.50 + 1.47 = C\$25.97\).
