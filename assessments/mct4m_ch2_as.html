Topic 2.1 — Expanding
Question 1
Expand \( (x+3)(x-5) \).
Solution:
FOIL: \( x^{2} -5x + 3x - 15 = x^{2}-2x-15 \).
Question 2
Expand \( (2x-3)^{2} \).
Solution:
\( (a-b)^{2}=a^{2}-2ab+b^{2} \) with \( a=2x, b=3 \): \( 4x^{2}-12x+9 \).
Topic 2.2 — Factoring
Question 3
Factor \( x^{2}-7x+12 \).
Solution:
Find two numbers multiplying to 12, summing to -7: -3 and -4. So \( (x-3)(x-4) \).
Question 4
Factor \( x^{3}-27 \) (difference of cubes).
Solution:
\( a^{3}-b^{3}=(a-b)(a^{2}+ab+b^{2}) \) with \( a=x, b=3 \): \( (x-3)(x^{2}+3x+9) \).
Topic 2.3 — Remainder Theorem
Question 5
Find the remainder when \( f(x)=x^{3}-2x^{2}+x-5 \) is divided by \( x-3 \).
Solution:
By Remainder Theorem, \( R=f(3)=27-18+3-5=7 \).
Question 6
If \( f(2)=0 \), then which of the following is a factor of \( f(x) \)?
Solution:
Factor theorem: \( f(a)=0 \Leftrightarrow x-a \) is a factor. So \( x-2 \).
Topic 2.4 — Solving Polynomial Equations
Question 7
List the rational candidates for roots of \( 2x^{3}+x^{2}-7x-6=0 \) (positive only).
Solution:
Factors of 6 over factors of 2: \( 1, 2, 3, 6, 1/2, 3/2 \).
Question 8
Solve \( x^{3}-x = 0 \). How many distinct real roots?
Solution:
\( x(x-1)(x+1)=0 \Rightarrow x=-1, 0, 1 \) — three distinct real roots.
Topic 2.5 — Polynomial Inequalities
Question 9
For \( (x-1)(x+2) > 0 \), the solution is:
Solution:
Product is positive when both factors share signs: both \( + \) means \( x>1 \); both \( - \) means \( x<-2 \).
Question 10
At a zero of even multiplicity, the polynomial graph does which of the following?
Solution:
Even multiplicity ⇒ bounce; odd ⇒ cross.
