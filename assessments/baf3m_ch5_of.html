Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
The three duties that should ALWAYS be segregated under good internal control are:
Solution:
The classic split: authorise (manager), custody (cashier), record-keeping (bookkeeper).
Question 2 [2 marks]
A deposit in transit is:
Solution:
The company has recorded it; the bank has not. Add it on the bank side.
Question 3 [2 marks]
Petty-cash fund \$300. Cash on hand \$72. Vouchers total \$226. The fund is over or short by:
Solution:
Cash + vouchers = \(72 + 226 = 298\). Imprest = 300. Short by \$2 (cash shortage). Enter −2.
Question 4 [2 marks]
The CPA Code's value that requires accountants to keep client information private is:
Solution:
Confidentiality is one of the five core values; client information must not be shared without authorisation.
Question 5 [2 marks]
An external user that uses the balance sheet to assess collateral is the:
Solution:
The bank evaluates the assets that could secure the loan if the borrower defaults.
