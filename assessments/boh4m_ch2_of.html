Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Kotter argued that management produces order and consistency, while leadership produces:
Solution:
Kotter: leadership = change and movement.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Lewin's three leadership styles are:
Solution:
Lewin's classic three: autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire.
Question 3 [2 marks]
Maslow's highest level of need is:
Solution:
Top of Maslow's pyramid = self-actualization.
Question 4 [2 marks]
In Herzberg's two-factor theory, "recognition" is a:
Solution:
Recognition = motivator. Hygienes: pay, policy, supervision, working conditions.
Question 5 [3 marks]
List Tuckman's five team-development stages in order, lowest to highest. (Comma-separated, single words.)
Solution:
Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning.
Question 6 [2 marks]
Vroom's expectancy theory: Motivation = ?
Solution:
\( M = E \times I \times V \) — multiplicative.
