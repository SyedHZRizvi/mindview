Unit 5: Media Literacy — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about media texts, advertising, news, and social-media literacy before starting Unit 5. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Media Habits
[3 marks]
Describe in 3–4 sentences your typical daily media consumption. Which platforms or sources do you use most? How much time do you estimate you spend on each per day?
Question 2 Ads
[3 marks]
Describe one advertisement you remember vividly (any medium). What about it made it memorable? What persuasive technique do you think it was using?
Question 3 News
[3 marks]
Distinguish between a news article, an opinion column, and a sponsored content piece. How would you tell them apart on a news website?
Question 4 Skepticism
[3 marks]
"SCIENTISTS CONFIRM: This one fruit removes 99% of belly fat in just two weeks!"
List three specific reasons to be skeptical of this claim. What questions would you ask before sharing or believing it?
Question 5 Bias
[3 marks]
Give an example of how the same news event could be reported differently by two outlets — one favourable and one critical. What specific word choices or framing would change?
Question 6 Algorithms
[3 marks]
In your own words, explain how a social-media algorithm decides what to show you. Why might two students sitting next to each other see entirely different feeds?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Have you ever shared something online and later found out it was inaccurate? Briefly describe what happened and what you learned.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
