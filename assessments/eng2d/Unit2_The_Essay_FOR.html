Unit 2: The Essay — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about the writing process, paragraph structure, and basic grammar before starting the unit. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Process
[3 marks]
Describe in 2–3 sentences how you usually approach a writing task. Which stages of the process do you tend to skip or rush?
Question 2 Thesis
[3 marks]
Write a working thesis statement for an essay on the question: "Is Hughes's 'Mother to Son' a poem of hope or of warning?" Aim for a single specific, debatable, supportable sentence.
Question 3 Paragraph
[4 marks]
Write a short PEEL paragraph (3–5 sentences) supporting the claim "In 'The Tell-Tale Heart,' Poe uses the heart's beating sound to externalise the narrator's guilt." Invent a quotation if you don't remember the exact words.
Question 4 Grammar
[3 marks]
Correct the grammatical errors in each sentence:
(a) "Each of the students were prepared for the test."
(b) "Romeo who is the protagonist are conflicted about love."
(c) "Their going to the store too buy they're books."
Question 5 Persuasion
[3 marks]
Define ethos, pathos, and logos. For each, give a brief example from a real or invented persuasive piece (a speech, an advertisement, an op-ed).
Question 6 Citation
[3 marks]
Write the correct MLA-9 in-text citation for: (a) a quotation from page 24 of To Kill a Mockingbird by Harper Lee; (b) a quotation from Act 1, Scene 2, line 14 of Romeo and Juliet; (c) line 7 of a poem.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 2 topic do you feel least confident about: the writing process, paragraph structure (PEEL/TEXAS), the literary-analysis essay, the persuasive essay, or MLA citation? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
