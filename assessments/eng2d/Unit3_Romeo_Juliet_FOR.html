Unit 3: Romeo and Juliet — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about Shakespeare's language, drama as a literary form, and the world of Romeo and Juliet before studying the play. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Drama
[3 marks]
Define protagonist, antagonist, and tragedy as they apply to a play. For each, give one example from a play, film, or TV show you have seen.
Question 2 Shakespeare
[3 marks]
What do you already know about Shakespeare? Write 3–5 sentences on his life, his theatre (the Globe), or any plays you have read or seen.
Question 3 Language
[3 marks]
Translate the following Shakespearean lines into modern English:
(a) "Wherefore art thou Romeo?"
(b) "What's in a name?"
(c) "But, soft! what light through yonder window breaks?"
Question 4 Inference
[3 marks]
"For never was a story of more woe / Than this of Juliet and her Romeo." — Prince Escalus, closing the play
Knowing nothing else about the play, what can you infer from these closing lines? Identify two clues in the text and explain the inferences they support.
Question 5 Genre
[3 marks]
What makes a story a tragedy rather than a comedy or a romance? Write 3–4 sentences on the features you expect to find.
Question 6 Reading Strategy
[3 marks]
Describe the strategies you usually use when you read a difficult text (poem, play, dense article). Which strategies will you use to read Shakespeare?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
What are you most nervous about as you start reading Romeo and Juliet? What do you hope to gain by the end of the unit?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
