Chapter 4: Global Economic Connections — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about trade, multinational companies, and the global economy before starting Chapter 4.
Question 1 Prereq · Trade
[3 marks]
Name three goods or services Canada commonly imports and three it exports. For each, identify a likely partner country.
Question 2 Prereq · Currency
[2 marks]
When the Canadian dollar weakens against the US dollar, which sector typically benefits and which is hurt? Explain briefly.
Question 3 Prereq · TNCs
[3 marks]
Name two transnational corporations operating in Canada and identify one country each is based in (headquartered).
Question 4 Prereq · Institutions
[3 marks]
Have you heard of the WTO, IMF, or World Bank? Briefly describe what one of them does.
Question 5 Skills · Maps & data
[3 marks]
If you were shown a "trade-balance" map by country, how would you tell at a glance which countries run surpluses vs deficits? What units should the legend use?
Question 6 Knowledge · Migration
[3 marks]
Name three reasons people migrate internationally and identify one Canadian and one global migration corridor (origin → destination).
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 4 topic do you feel least confident about: globalization theory, trade agreements, TNCs, IMF/World Bank, supply chains, or migration/remittances? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
