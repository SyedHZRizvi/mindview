Chapter 1: Quality of Life — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about world geography, basic statistics, and current events before starting Chapter 1. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · World map
[3 marks]
Name three world regions you would expect to find in the "very high" Human Development tier and three you would expect to find in the "low" tier. Give one example country for each region.
Question 2 Prereq · Basic statistics
[3 marks]
Define "median," "mean," and "geometric mean." Give one example of when the median is more useful than the mean for describing incomes.
Question 3 Prereq · Population
[3 marks]
What does a "population pyramid" show, and how would the pyramid of a high-fertility country differ from that of a low-fertility country?
Question 4 Prereq · Inequality
[2 marks]
In your own words, what does it mean to say a country has "high income inequality"? Why might that matter for public policy?
Question 5 Skills · Map reading
[3 marks]
If you were given a choropleth world map shaded by HDI value, what three features should you check before drawing conclusions (legend, classification, projection, etc.)?
Question 6 Knowledge · SDGs
[3 marks]
Name three Sustainable Development Goals you have heard of and briefly describe what each addresses.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 1 topic do you feel least confident about: composite indicators (HDI), inequality measurement (Gini), the demographic transition, urbanization, or the SDGs? Explain what specifically is unclear so your teacher can prioritise instruction.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
