Chapter 5: Geographic Inquiry — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand A
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about research, mapping, and academic writing before the capstone Chapter 5 / culminating task.
Question 1 Prereq · Research
[3 marks]
Describe in three steps how you typically research a topic for a school assignment. What is one weakness you would like to fix?
Question 2 Prereq · Sources
[3 marks]
In your own words, distinguish a primary source from a secondary source. Give one example of each.
Question 3 Prereq · Maps
[3 marks]
Name three elements every analytical map should include (e.g., title, legend, scale).
Question 4 Prereq · Tech
[2 marks]
Have you used Google Earth, Google Maps, or any GIS tool? Describe one task you have done with one of them.
Question 5 Skills · Citation
[3 marks]
Which citation style do you currently use (APA, MLA, Chicago)? Why do citations matter beyond avoiding plagiarism?
Question 6 Knowledge · Inquiry
[3 marks]
For your culminating inquiry, name two possible world-issue topics that interest you and identify one specific question you might investigate.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 5 skill area do you feel least confident about: framing a question, evaluating sources, using GIS, designing maps and visualisations, or making a defensible argument? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
