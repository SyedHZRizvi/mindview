Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [25 marks]
Question 1 [1 mark]
Which is NOT one of Fayol's four functions of management?
Solution:
POLC: Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling.
Question 2 [1 mark]
Mintzberg's "disturbance handler" role belongs to which category?
Solution:
Disturbance handler = decisional. Decisional roles: entrepreneur, disturbance handler, resource allocator, negotiator.
Question 3 [1 mark]
According to Katz, which skill matters most at the top of the organization?
Solution:
Conceptual. Human is equally important at every level; technical declines.
Question 4 [1 mark]
Frederick Taylor is most associated with:
Solution:
Taylor: scientific management (1911).
Question 5 [1 mark]
Lewin's three classic leadership styles are:
Solution:
Lewin: autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire.
Question 6 [1 mark]
Maslow's highest-level need is:
Solution:
Self-actualization sits at the top of Maslow's pyramid.
Question 7 [1 mark]
In Herzberg's two-factor theory, "supervision" is a:
Solution:
Supervision quality is a hygiene factor.
Question 8 [1 mark]
Vroom's expectancy theory: Motivation =
Solution:
\( M = E \times I \times V \).
Question 9 [1 mark]
Tuckman's stages of group development in order are:
Solution:
Forming → Storming → Norming → Performing → Adjourning.
Question 10 [1 mark]
Channel richness is highest in:
Solution:
Face-to-face: highest richness — multi-cue (tone, body language, immediate feedback).
Question 11 [1 mark]
Janis's "groupthink" refers to:
Solution:
Cohesion + harmony > analysis = groupthink.
Question 12 [1 mark]
Thomas-Kilmann's "collaborating" style is:
Solution:
Collaborating = high/high (win/win).
Question 13 [1 mark]
Kotter's first change step is:
Solution:
Step 1 = establish urgency. Without it, people don't change.
Question 14 [1 mark]
"M" in SMART stands for:
Solution:
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
Question 15 [1 mark]
In SWOT, "rising customer demand for plant-based products" is a:
Solution:
External + favourable = opportunity.
Question 16 [1 mark]
Dual reporting (function + product) describes a:
Solution:
Dual reporting = matrix.
Question 17 [1 mark]
Which interview question is FORBIDDEN under the Ontario Human Rights Code?
Solution:
Creed (religion) is a protected ground; asking is illegal.
Question 18 [1 mark]
In behavioural interviewing, STAR stands for:
Solution:
Situation, Task, Action, Result.
Question 19 [2 marks]
A firm has fixed costs of $20,000/month, sells units at $25, variable cost per unit $15. Monthly breakeven units?
Solution:
Contribution margin = $25 − $15 = $10. BE = $20000 / $10 = 2000 units.
Question 20 [2 marks]
Which is NOT one of the four Balanced-Scorecard perspectives?
Solution:
Four: Financial, Customer, Internal Process, Learning & Growth. Marketing is not a scorecard perspective.
Question 21 [2 marks]
"Greatest good for the greatest number" describes:
Solution:
Greatest aggregate benefit = utilitarian.
Question 22 [2 marks]
The Triple Bottom Line is:
Solution:
People, Planet, Profit (Elkington).

📚 Culminating Leadership Case Study Rubric (handed in separately, worth 15% of final mark)

Culminating Investigation Overview

The Culminating Leadership Case Study Investigation is a substantial integrative work completed over 4–6 weeks. It is submitted as a separate written report (3,000–4,500 words) AND an oral presentation (12–15 minutes). It is graded across all four achievement categories.

Components:

  • Research question: focused on one organization and one tension
  • Application of multiple frameworks from across Strands A–D
  • 8–12 credible varied sources with APA citations
  • Coherent argument with evidence-based recommendations
  • Professional written report
  • Engaging oral presentation with visuals
