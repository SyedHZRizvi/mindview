Unit 1: Early Civilizations — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from Grade 10 history (basic chronology, primary vs secondary sources, map skills) before starting Unit 1. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Be honest about what is unclear.
Question 1 Prereq · Chronology
[3 marks]
In your own words, define BCE and CE, and explain why these labels are now generally preferred over BC/AD in academic history writing.
Question 2 Prereq · Geography
[3 marks]
On a mental map, locate three of the following four river-valley civilization heartlands and identify the river(s) each is associated with: Mesopotamia, Egypt, the Indus Valley, Shang China. (You don't need to draw a map; write the river names and modern country.)
Question 3 Skills · Sources
[3 marks]
Distinguish a primary source from a secondary source. For each, give one example you might use in studying ancient Mesopotamia.
Question 4 Skills · Causation
[3 marks]
A historian asks "Why did agriculture spread across the world after about 10,000 BCE?" List at least two possible causes and briefly explain how each might have contributed.
Question 5 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Why is the Indus Valley civilization especially difficult for modern historians to write about, compared to Mesopotamia and Egypt?
Question 6 Skills · Interpretation
[3 marks]
"If a son strike his father, his hands shall be hewn off." — Code of Hammurabi, clause 195
Without further research, what can you reasonably infer about Babylonian society from this single law? What can you NOT yet infer? (Distinguish a careful inference from an unwarranted assumption.)
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 1 topic do you feel least confident about: Neolithic Revolution and origins of cities; Mesopotamia; Egypt; Indus and Shang; or writing and state formation? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
