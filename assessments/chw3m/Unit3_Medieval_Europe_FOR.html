Unit 3: Medieval Europe — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already think you know about medieval Europe — and identify the misconceptions popular culture has given you, so we can address them in class.
Question 1 Prereq · Stereotype
[3 marks]
In one sentence, write the most negative thing you have ever heard about "the Dark Ages." In a second sentence, identify why "Dark Ages" is now a controversial term among professional historians.
Question 2 Knowledge
[3 marks]
What was the Byzantine Empire? Why is it controversial to call it "Byzantine" rather than "Roman"?
Question 3 Skills · Knowledge
[3 marks]
Define feudalism in your own words. Then explain why many modern medievalists are uncomfortable with the term (refer to "the textbook feudal pyramid").
Question 4 Causation
[3 marks]
"The Crusades happened because Pope Urban II preached a sermon at Clermont in 1095."
Critique this single-cause explanation. Name at least two underlying preconditions (theological, political, economic, or social) that made Urban II's sermon trigger such a massive response.
Question 5 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Roughly what percentage of European, Egyptian, and Levantine populations died in the Black Death (1346–53)? Identify two long-term consequences (economic, social, or religious).
Question 6 Skills · Source-Criticism
[3 marks]
When using a medieval Christian chronicler's account of the First Crusade as a primary source, list three questions a careful historian should ask about the chronicler before using their account.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which medieval European topic do you find most surprising or unfamiliar — Byzantium, feudalism, the Church, the Crusades, the Black Death, or the rise of universities and Gothic cathedrals? Explain what you'd like to learn more about.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
