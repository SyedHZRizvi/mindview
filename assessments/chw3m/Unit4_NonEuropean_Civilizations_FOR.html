Unit 4: Non-European Civilizations — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know — and what you've been mis-taught — about the non-European world before 1500 CE. Be honest; this is a starting point.
Question 1 Prereq · Geography
[3 marks]
On a mental map of the medieval world, locate three of the following: the Abbasid capital Baghdad; the Mali Empire's Niger River; the Tang capital Chang'an; the Aztec city Tenochtitlán; the Inca capital Cusco; the Sahelian city Timbuktu.
Question 2 Stereotype
[3 marks]
Without judgement: write one assumption you have heard or held about pre-colonial sub-Saharan Africa. Then write one fact from the world of c. 1300 CE that complicates the assumption (e.g., Timbuktu's library, Mansa Musa's wealth, Great Zimbabwe).
Question 3 Knowledge
[3 marks]
What were the Five Pillars of Islam? Briefly explain why an emphasis on equality of believers before God was politically and socially radical in seventh-century Arabia.
Question 4 Causation
[3 marks]
The Song Chinese had gunpowder, the compass, large-scale printing, and industrial coal-burning iron production by 1100 CE. Why did the Industrial Revolution not begin in Song China? List at least two factors.
Question 5 Source-Criticism
[3 marks]
In the 1900s, European archaeologists at Great Zimbabwe invented elaborate theories that the site had been built by Phoenicians, Sabaeans, or the Queen of Sheba — anyone except indigenous Africans.
What does this episode tell us about how the assumptions historians and archaeologists bring to evidence can shape (or distort) their interpretation?
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
When Cortés met the Mexica (Aztec) in 1519 and Pizarro met the Inca in 1532, why was the Spanish military victory comparatively rapid? Name at least three factors (other than European weaponry).
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which non-European medieval civilization are you most curious to learn about — the Islamic caliphates, Tang/Song China, the Mongols, the Sahelian empires, the Aztec or Inca, or medieval India? Explain why.

Teacher Feedback
