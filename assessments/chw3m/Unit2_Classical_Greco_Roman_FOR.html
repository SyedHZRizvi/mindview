Unit 2: Classical Greco-Roman — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify what you already know about Greek and Roman history before formal study begins. Be honest — this is a diagnostic, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Knowledge
[3 marks]
What is a "polis"? Why was the polis (rather than larger kingdoms or empires) the dominant political unit of classical Greek life from roughly 800 to 323 BCE?
Question 2 Prereq · Knowledge
[3 marks]
In your own words, define democracy. Identify ONE way Athenian democracy was MORE inclusive than modern democracies, and TWO ways it was LESS inclusive.
Question 3 Skills · Causation
[3 marks]
"The Roman Republic fell because of one man's ambition — Julius Caesar."
Critique this single-cause explanation in two sentences. Identify at least one structural problem (military, economic, social, or political) that the Republic was facing before Caesar.
Question 4 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Identify three of the following classical thinkers and one major idea associated with each: Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, Epicurus, Stoics, Hippocrates.
Question 5 Skills · Periodisation
[3 marks]
A textbook says: "The Roman Empire fell in 476 CE." Explain why this date is contested by historians. Name at least one alternative end-date and the criterion that justifies it.
Question 6 Skills · Sources
[3 marks]
When historians read Thucydides's account of Pericles's "Funeral Oration," what should they keep in mind about how Thucydides composed the speech (and why this matters)?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 2 topic do you feel least confident about — Greek poleis and democracy; Greek philosophy and science; Alexander and the Hellenistic world; the Roman Republic; the Roman Empire and Christianity; or the fall of Rome? Explain what is unclear.

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
