Unit 5: Interactions & Inquiry — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strands E + A
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Prepare for the culminating inquiry project by checking your research and writing skills. Be honest — this diagnostic helps target instruction.
Question 1 Skills · Inquiry
[3 marks]
Sketch one good research question that you might pursue for the culminating inquiry. The question must be researchable from accessible sources, debatable in interpretation, and significant for understanding pre-1500 CE world history.
Question 2 Skills · Sources
[3 marks]
For the question you wrote in Q1, list two primary sources and two secondary sources you might consult (even just rough categories like "an Arabic chronicler" + "a modern scholarly book").
Question 3 Knowledge · Trade
[3 marks]
Explain in one sentence each what the Silk Roads and the Indian Ocean trade network were. Identify one good (or technology, religion, or disease) that travelled along each.
Question 4 Skills · Periodisation
[3 marks]
"1492 was the end of the medieval world." Critique this claim from a global perspective. Was 1492 perceived as significant by Ming Chinese, Songhai Africans, or Mughal-eve Indians at the time? Why or why not?
Question 5 Skills · Source-Criticism
[3 marks]
"Marco Polo travelled to China and lived at Khubilai Khan's court for over twenty years, returning to write a definitive account."
What two questions should a careful historian ask about Marco Polo's account before treating it as straightforward eyewitness testimony?
Question 6 Skills · Writing
[3 marks]
Distinguish a thesis statement from a topic. Give an example of each on a CHW3M-relevant subject.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which aspect of the culminating inquiry project feels most challenging — choosing a topic, formulating a question, finding sources, evaluating sources, building an argument, or writing/citing properly? Explain.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
