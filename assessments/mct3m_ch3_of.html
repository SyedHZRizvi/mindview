Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
The exact value of \( \sin 45° \) is:
Solution:
From the 45-45-90 triangle: \( \sin 45° = 1/\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{2}/2 \).
Question 2 [3 marks]
Convert \( 7\pi/4 \) radians to degrees.
Solution:
\( 7\pi/4 \times 180/\pi = 7 \times 45 = 315° \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
The exact value of \( \cos 210° \) is:
Solution:
210° is in Q3 (cosine negative). Reference angle = 210 - 180 = 30°. \( \cos 30° = \sqrt{3}/2 \). With sign: \( -\sqrt{3}/2 \).
Question 4 [2 marks]
For \( y = 5 \cos(4x) + 2 \), the period in degrees is:
Solution:
Period = 360°/k = 360°/4 = 90°.
