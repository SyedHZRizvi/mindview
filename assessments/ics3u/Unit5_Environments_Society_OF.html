Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
1. [3 marks]
For each, state whether it is HARDWARE or SOFTWARE.
  • CPU
  • Microsoft Word
  • RAM
  • Web browser
  • Solid-state drive
  • Python interpreter
2. [3 marks]
Convert between bases.
  1. Binary 10110 to decimal.
  2. Decimal 25 to binary.
  3. Decimal 255 to hexadecimal.
3. [3 marks]
Briefly describe the role of each (one sentence each):
  • CPU
  • RAM
  • Storage (HDD or SSD)
4. [3 marks]
Define each in one sentence:
  1. Operating system
  2. IP address
  3. DNS
Part B: Thinking & Investigation [12 marks]
5. [4 marks]
A friend sends you the screenshot below of an email.
From: support@bnak-of-canada.com Subject: URGENT: Verify account in 24 hours "Click here to confirm your password or your account will be permanently disabled..." Link: http://canada-verify.ru/login
List four red flags that suggest this is a phishing attempt.
6. [4 marks]
Trace what happens, step by step, when you type https://example.com into a browser. Use 4-6 short numbered steps.
7. [4 marks]
Compare the strengths and trade-offs of OPEN-SOURCE software (e.g., Linux, Python, Firefox) and PROPRIETARY software (e.g., Microsoft Office, macOS). Give two strengths of each.
Part C: Communication [12 marks]
8. [6 marks]
Write a 150-200 word response: "Should students be required to learn at least one term of computer science before graduating from high school?" Take a clear position. Reference at least: (a) one concrete benefit, (b) one concrete drawback, and (c) one comparison to another required subject.
9. [6 marks]
Write a 100-150 word response: "Is it ethical for a high-school student to use an AI tool such as ChatGPT to help with their CS assignments?" Take a clear position and address at least: (a) what counts as "help" vs cheating, (b) one risk, and (c) one benefit. Acknowledge a different view.
Part D: Application [12 marks]
10. [4 marks]
Identify three specific Ontario university programs and three specific Ontario college programs related to computer science. For each, name the institution and the program.
11. [4 marks]
Name three career roles a computer-science graduate could pursue, and briefly describe what each role does day-to-day (one or two sentences each).
12. [4 marks]
Identify three landmark contributors to computer science (state name, contribution, and rough decade). At least one of the three should be a Canadian.
