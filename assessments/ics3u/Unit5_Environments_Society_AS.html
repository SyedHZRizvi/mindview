Question 1 — Hardware vs software
Which is software, not hardware?
Solution:
The CPU, RAM, and keyboard are physical (hardware). The web browser is a program (software).
Question 2 — Binary conversion
What is the decimal value of binary 1010?
Solution:
1·8 + 0·4 + 1·2 + 0·1 = 10.
Question 3 — Decimal to binary
What is the binary representation of decimal 13?
Solution:
13 = 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 1·8 + 1·4 + 0·2 + 1·1 = 1101.
Question 4 — RAM vs storage
Which best describes RAM?
Solution:
RAM is volatile working memory. The hard drive / SSD is persistent storage. The CPU runs instructions.
Question 5 — OS responsibilities
Which of these is NOT a job of the operating system?
Solution:
The OS manages CPU scheduling, memory, file system, and devices. It does not write essays.
Question 6 — DNS
What is the role of DNS on the Internet?
Solution:
DNS is the global system that maps domain names to IP addresses.
Question 7 — HTTPS
What does the "S" in HTTPS stand for, and what does it actually do?
Solution:
"S" = Secure. HTTPS encrypts traffic so that someone on the network cannot read the contents.
Question 8 — Phishing red flags
Which of these is the BEST single response to a suspicious email that asks you to click a link and "verify your account in 24 hours"?
Solution:
Never click; verify through a known channel (school helpdesk, the bank's number on your card); report and delete.
Question 9 — Strong passwords
Which is the SAFEST password?
Solution:
Long, random, unique-per-site, with MFA. The other options are all breakable in seconds or minutes.
Question 10 — Open-source vs proprietary
Which of these is OPEN-SOURCE software?
Solution:
The Python interpreter is open-source (PSF licence). The others are proprietary.
Question 11 — Canadian CS
Which of these is a Canadian computer scientist known for foundational work in artificial intelligence (deep learning), at the University of Toronto?
Solution:
Geoffrey Hinton, University of Toronto, was awarded the 2018 ACM Turing Award (jointly with Bengio and LeCun) and the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics for foundational work on deep learning.
Question 12 — Pathways
If a student wants to study Computer Science with a strong co-op program at an Ontario university, which is the best-known example?
Solution:
The University of Waterloo is internationally known for its CS co-op program (although the other Ontario universities listed also offer strong CS programs).
