🔍 Unit 5: Computer Environments and Society — Pre-Unit Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning — Teacher Feedback
📋 Not Graded — Descriptive Feedback Only
Purpose: Check what you already know about how computers work, your personal habits around privacy and cybersecurity, and your interest in CS-adjacent careers. Honest answers help your teacher tailor the discussion.
Question 1 — What is a computer?
In your own words, name three parts (hardware) inside a typical laptop and describe what each does.
Question 2 — OS familiarity
What operating system(s) have you used? (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, Android, iOS, etc.) Briefly describe one task each is good for.
Question 3 — Internet usage habits
Roughly how many websites do you use a username and password for? Do you reuse the same password? Why or why not?
Question 4 — Have you seen phishing?
Have you ever received a suspicious email or text that asked you to click a link, log in, or "verify" something? Briefly describe what it said and what you did.
Question 5 — Personal data
List three pieces of personal information about you that are probably stored somewhere online (no names, no specific accounts). Are you comfortable with that, and why?
Question 6 — AI experience
Have you used an AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, image generators, etc.)? Briefly describe one task you used it for and whether it gave a correct answer.
Question 7 — Career interest
Are you considering computing as a postsecondary path? Which roles or programs interest you (software developer, game designer, cybersecurity, data science, etc.) — or which non-computing field would you combine it with?
Question 8 — Knowledge self-rating
Rate 1-5:
  • I can explain how a computer stores binary numbers.
  • I know how to make a strong password.
  • I can describe what an operating system does.
  • I know what HTTPS protects.
  • I can name at least one historical CS contributor.
