Topic 2.1 — Special Journals
Question 1
A cash sale of merchandise should be recorded in which special journal?
Solution:
The sales journal records credit sales only. Cash sales go in the cash receipts journal because they involve a cash inflow.
Question 2
Terms 2/10 n/30 mean:
Solution:
"2/10 n/30" = "2 percent discount if paid in 10 days; net (full) amount due in 30 days."
Topic 2.2 — Subsidiary Ledgers
Question 3
The AR control account shows \$ 25,000. The schedule of accounts receivable totals \$ 24,500. This means:
Solution:
The sum of subsidiary balances must equal the control account. A \$ 500 difference is a posting error that must be traced and fixed.
Topic 2.3 — Perpetual vs Periodic Inventory
Question 4
Beginning inventory \$ 10,000; net purchases \$ 65,000; ending inventory \$ 14,000. Compute COGS under the periodic system.
Solution:
\( \text{COGS} = \text{BI} + \text{Net Purchases} - \text{EI} = 10{,}000 + 65{,}000 - 14{,}000 = \$ 61{,}000 \).
Question 5
Under the perpetual system, when 5 units are sold at \$ 50 each (cost \$ 30 each):
Solution:
Perpetual records (1) Cash/AR debit and Sales credit; (2) COGS debit and Inventory credit (at cost).
Topic 2.4 — Partnerships
Question 6
Net income \$ 100,000. Partners A and B share in a 3:2 ratio. A's allocation is:
Solution:
A receives \( \dfrac{3}{5} \times 100{,}000 = \$ 60{,}000 \) and B receives \( \dfrac{2}{5} \times 100{,}000 = \$ 40{,}000 \).
Topic 2.5 — Corporations
Question 7
A 2-for-1 stock split requires:
Solution:
A stock split changes only the number of shares — total share capital is unchanged. Only a memo entry is required.
Question 8
A company has 50,000 common shares outstanding. The Board declares a \$ 0.40 per share dividend. Total dividend declared:
Solution:
\( 50{,}000 \times 0.40 = \$ 20{,}000 \). Entry on declaration date: debit Cash Dividends Declared, credit Dividends Payable.
Topic 2.6 — Payroll
Question 9
Employee gross pay \$ 2,000. Deductions: CPP \$ 119, EI \$ 33.20, income tax \$ 260. Net pay is:
Solution:
Total deductions \( = 119 + 33.20 + 260 = \$ 412.20 \). Net pay \( = 2{,}000 - 412.20 = \$ 1{,}587.80 \).
Question 10
If the employee EI deduction is \$ 33.20, the employer's matched EI contribution is:
Solution:
Employer EI = 1.4× employee. \( 33.20 \times 1.4 = \$ 46.48 \).
