Topic 1.1 — Rational Exponents
Question 1
Evaluate \( 16^{3/4} \).
Solution:
\( 16^{3/4} = (\sqrt[4]{16})^{3} = 2^{3} = 8 \).
Question 2
Simplify \( 8^{-2/3} \).
Solution:
\( 8^{-2/3} = 1/8^{2/3} = 1/(\sqrt[3]{8})^{2} = 1/2^{2} = 1/4 \).
Topic 1.2 — Exponential Functions
Question 3
For \( f(x) = 5 \cdot 2^{x} \), what is the \( y \)-intercept?
Solution:
\( f(0) = 5 \cdot 2^{0} = 5 \cdot 1 = 5 \).
Question 4
A population starts at 80 and doubles every hour. What is the population after 4 hours?
Solution:
\( P(t) = 80 \cdot 2^{t} \). At \( t=4 \): \( P(4) = 80 \cdot 16 = 1280 \).
Topic 1.3 — Meaning of a Logarithm
Question 5
Evaluate \( \log_{3} 81 \).
Solution:
\( 3^{?} = 81 \). Since \( 3^{4} = 81 \), \( \log_{3} 81 = 4 \).
Question 6
Which exponential equation is equivalent to \( \log_{2} 32 = 5 \)?
Solution:
\( \log_{b} N = x \;\Leftrightarrow\; b^{x} = N \). So \( \log_{2} 32 = 5 \) is equivalent to \( 2^{5} = 32 \).
Topic 1.4 — Laws of Logarithms
Question 7
Simplify \( \log_{10} 4 + \log_{10} 25 \).
Solution:
Product law: \( \log 4 + \log 25 = \log(4 \cdot 25) = \log 100 = 2 \).
Question 8
\( \log_{10}(M+N) \) equals which expression?
Solution:
The product law applies to products inside a log, not sums. \( \log(M+N) \) cannot be simplified using the laws.
Topic 1.5 — Solving Exponential Equations
Question 9
Solve \( 3^{x} = 81 \).
Solution:
\( 81 = 3^{4} \), so \( 3^{x} = 3^{4} \Rightarrow x = 4 \).
Question 10
Solve \( 2^{x} = 50 \) to two decimal places.
Solution:
\( x = \log 50 / \log 2 \approx 1.6990 / 0.3010 \approx 5.64 \).
