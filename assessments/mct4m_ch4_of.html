Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
A force of 80 N acts at 35° above horizontal. Find the horizontal component (round to 1 dp).
Solution:
\( F_{x}=80\cos 35°\approx 80(0.8192)\approx 65.5 \) N.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Vector \( \vec{v} \) has components \( (-5, 12) \). Find its magnitude.
Solution:
\( |\vec{v}|=\sqrt{25+144}=\sqrt{169}=13 \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
Find the volume of a cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 10 cm. Use \( \pi \approx 3.14 \), round to nearest whole.
Solution:
\( V=\pi r^{2}h=16\pi(10)=160\pi\approx 502.65 \approx 503 \) cm³ (accept 502–503).
Question 4 [1 mark]
Two perpendicular vectors of magnitudes 6 and 8 are added. The resultant has magnitude:
Solution:
\( \sqrt{6^{2}+8^{2}}=10 \).
Question 5 [2 marks]
An inscribed angle in a circle subtends an arc of 140° (central). The inscribed angle is:
Solution:
Inscribed = (1/2)(140°) = 70°.
