Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
A 5-year corporate roadmap is a:
Solution:
Long-term, top management = strategic.
Question 2 [2 marks]
The "M" in SMART goals stands for:
Solution:
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
Question 3 [2 marks]
Which structure groups employees by expertise (marketing, finance, operations)?
Solution:
Grouped by expertise = functional.
Question 4 [2 marks]
The Ontario Human Rights Code protects employees from discrimination based on:
Solution:
The Ontario Human Rights Code lists 17 protected grounds.
Question 5 [3 marks]
List the SMART criteria in order (single words, comma-separated).
Solution:
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
Question 6 [2 marks]
The acronym in behavioural interviewing is:
Solution:
STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result.
