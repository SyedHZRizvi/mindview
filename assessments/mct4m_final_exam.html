Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [25 marks]
Q1 [1]
Evaluate \( 32^{2/5} \).
Solution:
\( (\sqrt[5]{32})^{2}=2^{2}=4 \).
Q2 [1]
\( \log_{2} 16 = \) ?
Solution:
\( 2^{4}=16 \), so \( \log_{2} 16 = 4 \).
Q3 [2]
Simplify \( \log 5 + \log 20 \).
Solution:
\( \log(5\cdot 20)=\log 100=2 \).
Q4 [2]
Solve \( 3^{x}=27 \).
Solution:
\( 27=3^{3} \), so \( x=3 \).
Q5 [1]
Expand \( (x+4)(x-4) \).
Solution:
Difference of squares: \( x^{2}-16 \).
Q6 [2]
Factor: \( x^{2}+5x-14 \).
Solution:
Two numbers multiplying to -14, summing to 5: 7, -2. So \( (x+7)(x-2) \).
Q7 [2]
Remainder when \( f(x)=x^{3}+2x-4 \) is divided by \( x-2 \).
Solution:
\( f(2)=8+4-4=8 \).
Q8 [2]
Convert \( 135° \) to radians (exact).
Solution:
\( 135\pi/180=3\pi/4 \) rad.
Q9 [2]
Find \( \sin(120°) \) exactly.
Solution:
Reference 60°; Q II ⇒ sine positive. \( \sin 120°=\sqrt{3}/2 \approx 0.866 \).
Q10 [2]
Period of \( y=2\sin(\pi x/3) \)?
Solution:
\( T=2\pi/|k|=2\pi/(\pi/3)=6 \).
Q11 [2]
Magnitude of vector \( (8, 6) \).
Solution:
\( \sqrt{64+36}=\sqrt{100}=10 \).
Q12 [2]
Volume of a sphere of radius 6 cm (use \( \pi \approx 3.14 \), round to nearest whole).
Solution:
\( V=(4/3)\pi r^{3}=(4/3)(3.14)(216)\approx 904 \) cm³.
Q13 [2]
An inscribed angle subtends an arc of central angle 90°. Inscribed angle = ?
Solution:
Inscribed = (1/2)(90°) = 45°.
Q14 [2]
Identify the family: \( y=2, 6, 18, 54 \) at equally spaced \( x \).
Solution:
Ratios all 3 ⇒ exponential.

Q15 Solution

Take \( \log \) of both sides: \( (x+1)\log 2 = (x-2)\log 5 \). Distribute: \( x\log 2 + \log 2 = x\log 5 - 2\log 5 \). Group \( x \) terms: \( x(\log 2 - \log 5) = -2\log 5 - \log 2 \). So \( x = \dfrac{-2\log 5 - \log 2}{\log 2 - \log 5} \approx \dfrac{-1.398 - 0.301}{-0.398} \approx 4.27 \).

Q16 Solution

Rational candidates: \( \pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 3, \pm 6 \). Try \( x=1 \): \( 1-7+6 = 0 \). ✓ So \( x-1 \) is a factor. Synthetic-divide: quotient \( x^{2}+x-6 = (x+3)(x-2) \). Solutions: \( x=-3, 1, 2 \).

Q17 Solution

Let \( u=x^{2} \): \( u^{2}-13u+36 \le 0 \). Factor: \( (u-4)(u-9) \le 0 \), so \( 4 \le u \le 9 \), giving \( 4 \le x^{2} \le 9 \). Solution: \( x \in [-3,-2]\cup[2,3] \).

Q18 Solution

Smallest angle is opposite shortest side (8). Use cosine law with \( a=8, b=11, c=13 \): \( \cos A = (11^{2}+13^{2}-8^{2})/(2\cdot 11\cdot 13) = (121+169-64)/286 = 226/286 \approx 0.790 \). \( A = \cos^{-1}(0.790) \approx 38° \).

Q19 Solution

LHS = \( (1-\cos^{2}\theta)/(\sin\theta\cos\theta) = \sin^{2}\theta/(\sin\theta\cos\theta) = \sin\theta/\cos\theta = \tan\theta \). ∎

Q20 Solution

Components of 30 N at 135°: \( (30\cos 135°, 30\sin 135°) = (-21.21, 21.21) \). Sum components: \( R_x = 40+0-21.21 = 18.79 \); \( R_y = 0+50+21.21 = 71.21 \). Magnitude \( |\vec{R}|=\sqrt{18.79^{2}+71.21^{2}} \approx \sqrt{353+5071} \approx 73.6 \) N. Angle \( \tan^{-1}(71.21/18.79) \approx 75.2° \) from positive \( x \).

Q27 Solution

(a) \( A=8000(1+0.054/12)^{12t} \). (b) At \( t=6 \): \( A=8000(1.0045)^{72} \approx 8000(1.3825) \approx \$11{,}060 \). (c) Set \( A=16000 \): \( 2 = (1.0045)^{12t} \). \( t = \log 2/(12\log 1.0045) \approx 0.301/(12 \cdot 0.001951) \approx 12.86 \) years.

Q28 Solution

(a) \( \cos\theta=4/10 \), so \( \theta=\cos^{-1}(0.4) \approx 66.4° \). (b) \( h=\sqrt{10^{2}-4^{2}}=\sqrt{84} \approx 9.17 \) m. (c) New base 5 m, new height \( \sqrt{100-25}=\sqrt{75} \approx 8.66 \) m. Top drops by \( 9.17-8.66 \approx 0.51 \) m.

Q29 Solution

(a) \( V_{cyl}=\pi (1)^{2}(3)=3\pi; V_{hem}=(2/3)\pi=2\pi/3 \). Total \( = 11\pi/3 \approx 11.52 \) m³. (b) Lateral cylinder \( = 2\pi(1)(3) = 6\pi \). Hemisphere surface \( = 2\pi(1)^{2}=2\pi \). Total \( = 8\pi \approx 25.13 \) m². (c) Cost \( \approx 25.13 \times \$80 \approx \$2010 \).

Q30 Solution

(a) Period = 2(6 hours from high to low) = 12 h. (b) Amplitude \( a=(12-4)/2=4 \); axis \( c=8 \); \( k=2\pi/12=\pi/6 \). Cosine works with max at 6 a.m.: \( D(t) = 4\cos(\tfrac{\pi}{6}(t-6))+8 \). (c) \( 10 = 4\cos(\tfrac{\pi}{6}(t-6))+8 \Rightarrow \cos(\tfrac{\pi}{6}(t-6))=0.5 \Rightarrow \tfrac{\pi}{6}(t-6)=\pi/3 \Rightarrow t-6=2 \Rightarrow t=8 \) a.m. (first time after 6 a.m.).

Q31 Solution

(a) Factor: \( P(x) = -2x(x^{2}-12x+36) = -2x(x-6)^{2} \). Zeros: \( x=0 \) (single) and \( x=6 \) (double). (b) Sign analysis: \( P(x)>0 \) only when \( -2x \) is positive and \( (x-6)^{2} \) is positive, i.e. \( x<0 \). But \( x \) represents production so \( x \ge 0 \) ⇒ \( P(x) \le 0 \) for all realistic \( x \). So the factory is never profitable with this model — recommend reviewing cost structure. (c) \( P(4) = -2(4)(4-6)^{2} = -8(4) = -32 \) (thousand $) loss. (d) Conclusion: avoid production at 400-unit batches; redesign.
