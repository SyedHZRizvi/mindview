Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
FITT's CITP designation is issued to:
Solution:
FITT (Forum for International Trade Training) issues the Certified International Trade Professional (CITP®|FIBP®) designation.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Hofstede's "long-term orientation" dimension measures:
Solution:
Long-term orientation (LTO) measures a culture's focus on future rewards — persistence, thrift, perseverance. China and South Korea score high; the US and Australia lower.
Question 3 [2 marks]
CFPOA stands for:
Solution:
CFPOA is Canada's anti-bribery law for transactions involving foreign public officials.
Question 4 [2 marks]
"ESG" reporting refers to:
Solution:
ESG = Environmental, Social, Governance — increasingly used by investors, regulators, and rating agencies to assess corporate responsibility.
Question 5 [2 marks]
The "honeymoon" stage of the U-curve typically lasts:
Solution:
The honeymoon is the initial excitement of arrival, typically 0–3 months, before culture shock sets in.
Question 6 [3 marks]
An expat's base salary is \(C\$120{,}000\). The firm adds a hardship premium of 15%, a housing allowance of \(C\$30{,}000\), and an education allowance of \(C\$25{,}000\). What is the total annual compensation package (gross)?
Solution:
Hardship = \(0.15 \times 120{,}000 = 18{,}000\). Total = \(120{,}000 + 18{,}000 + 30{,}000 + 25{,}000 = C\$193{,}000\).
