Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Evaluate exactly: \( 64^{2/3} \).
Solution:
\( 64^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{64})^{2}=4^{2}=16 \).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Evaluate \( \log_{2} 64 \).
Solution:
\( 2^{6}=64 \), so \( \log_{2} 64 = 6 \).
Question 3 [1 mark]
Which of the following is the horizontal asymptote of \( f(x) = 4 \cdot 2^{x} - 3 \)?
Solution:
The constant shift \( -3 \) moves the asymptote from \( y=0 \) to \( y=-3 \).
Question 4 [2 marks]
Express as a single logarithm: \( 2\log x + \log y - \log z \).
Solution:
Power: \( \log x^{2}+\log y - \log z = \log(x^{2}y) - \log z = \log(x^{2}y/z) \).
Question 5 [2 marks]
Solve \( 5^{x} = 125 \).
Solution:
\( 125 = 5^{3} \), so \( x=3 \).
Question 6 [3 marks]
Solve \( 3^{x+1} = 17 \). Round to three decimal places.
Solution:
Take \( \log \): \( (x+1)\log 3 = \log 17 \). So \( x = \log 17/\log 3 - 1 \approx 2.5789 - 1 = 1.579 \).
