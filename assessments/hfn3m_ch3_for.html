HFN3M Chapter 3: Canada's Food Guide & Meal Planning — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Pre-chapter check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your prior knowledge about food choices and meal planning before starting Chapter 3.
Question 1 Prior · Food Guide
[3 marks]
In your own words, summarise what Canada's Food Guide is. Name what it recommends and one way it might affect a Canadian family's grocery list.
Question 2 Prior · Labels
[4 marks]
Look at the Nutrition Facts table on any package in your kitchen. Identify (a) serving size, (b) total Calories per serving, (c) one nutrient to limit and its %DV, (d) one nutrient to encourage and its %DV.
Question 3 Prior · Lifecycle
[3 marks]
Name one nutrient whose need is higher for a pregnant woman than for a non-pregnant woman. Why?
Question 4 Prior · Special Diets
[3 marks]
Define "vegetarian" and "vegan". Identify one nutrient that vegans must monitor and why.
Question 5 Skills · Planning
[3 marks]
Plan one day of meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) for a typical teenager that you would call "well balanced". You do not need to count grams; describe each meal in a sentence.
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
Name one Canadian food allergen, two common foods that contain it, and one symptom of an allergic reaction.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 3 topic do you most want to learn: the new plate model, reading Nutrition Facts tables, meal planning across the lifespan, or planning for special diets? Why?

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
