Prerequisite Skills (Grade 10 SNC2D)
Question 1 [2 marks]
How many protons, neutrons, and electrons are in a neutral atom of \(^{27}_{13}\text{Al}\)?
Reveal expected answer
13 protons, 14 neutrons, 13 electrons (neutral → equal protons and electrons).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Where on the periodic table do you find the alkali metals, the halogens, and the noble gases?
Reveal expected answer
Alkali metals = Group 1 (Li, Na, K, …). Halogens = Group 17 (F, Cl, Br, …). Noble gases = Group 18 (He, Ne, Ar, …).
Question 3 [3 marks]
Write the formulas of the ionic compounds formed between: (a) Na and Cl; (b) Mg and O; (c) Al and S.
Reveal expected answer
(a) NaCl; (b) MgO; (c) Al₂S₃.
Question 4 [2 marks]
Name the compounds: (a) K₂O, (b) CaCl₂.
Reveal expected answer
(a) Potassium oxide; (b) Calcium chloride.
Question 5 [2 marks]
Draw the Bohr-Rutherford diagram of a fluorine atom (\(Z = 9\)). State the number of valence electrons.
Reveal expected answer
Nucleus with 9p + 10n; first shell: 2 e⁻; second shell: 7 e⁻. Valence electrons = 7.
Question 6 [2 marks]
Is a bond between two identical non-metal atoms (e.g., O₂, H₂) ionic or covalent? Explain.
Reveal expected answer
Covalent (non-polar covalent specifically). Identical atoms have identical electronegativities → \(\Delta\text{EN} = 0\) → electrons shared equally.
Question 7 [3 marks]
For each pair, predict which has the larger atomic radius: (a) Li or Cs; (b) C or F; (c) Na or Mg.
Reveal expected answer
(a) Cs (larger — further down group). (b) C (left of F across period). (c) Na (left of Mg across period).
