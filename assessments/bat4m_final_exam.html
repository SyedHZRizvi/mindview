Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [25 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
A bank statement shows \$ 8,400. Outstanding cheques \$ 600; deposit in transit \$ 300. Adjusted bank balance:
Solution:
\( 8{,}400 - 600 + 300 = \$ 8{,}100 \).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Net credit sales \$ 750,000. Bad-debt rate (percentage-of-sales) is 1.2%. Bad-debt expense:
Solution:
\( 750{,}000 \times 0.012 = \$ 9{,}000 \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
Equipment cost \$ 120,000, residual \$ 20,000, 10-year life. Annual straight-line amortization:
Solution:
\( (120{,}000 - 20{,}000)/10 = 100{,}000/10 = \$ 10{,}000 \).
Question 4 [2 marks]
FIFO under rising prices produces:
Solution:
FIFO sends oldest (lowest) costs to COGS during rising prices, so COGS is lowest and ending inventory (most recent, higher costs) is highest.
Question 5 [2 marks]
Partner A's profit share is 60%. Net income \$ 80,000. A's allocation:
Solution:
\( 0.60 \times 80{,}000 = \$ 48{,}000 \).
Question 6 [2 marks]
EBIT \$ 300,000; interest expense \$ 50,000. Times-interest-earned:
Solution:
\( 300{,}000 / 50{,}000 = 6 \) times.
Question 7 [2 marks]
Net income \$ 90,000; net sales \$ 1,000,000. Profit margin:
Solution:
\( 90{,}000 / 1{,}000{,}000 = 9\% \).
Question 8 [2 marks]
EPS \$ 2.50; share price \$ 40. P/E ratio:
Solution:
\( 40 / 2.50 = 16 \).
Question 9 [2 marks]
Which is NOT a COSO internal-control component?
Solution:
COSO has 5 components: Control Environment, Risk Assessment, Control Activities, Information & Communication, Monitoring. Insurance is not one.
Question 10 [2 marks]
The three legs of the fraud triangle are pressure, opportunity, and:
Solution:
Cressey's fraud triangle: Pressure + Opportunity + Rationalization.
Question 11 [2 marks]
A Canadian publicly traded bank uses what accounting framework?
Solution:
Publicly accountable enterprises (including banks) must use IFRS in Canada since 2011.
Question 12 [3 marks]
Employee gross pay \$ 1,800. Deductions: CPP \$ 107, EI \$ 30, income tax \$ 230. Net pay:
Solution:
Total deductions \( = 107 + 30 + 230 = 367 \). Net pay \( = 1{,}800 - 367 = \$ 1{,}433 \).
