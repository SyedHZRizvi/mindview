Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
An outstanding cheque appears on the bank reconciliation as:
Solution:
The cheque is already recorded by the company but not yet cleared by the bank, so the bank balance must be reduced to match.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Net credit sales \$ 800,000; allowance for doubtful accounts target = 1% of net credit sales (percentage of sales). Compute bad-debt expense.
Solution:
\( 800{,}000 \times 0.01 = \$ 8{,}000 \). Under the percentage-of-sales method the existing allowance balance is ignored.
Question 3 [2 marks]
A trading security purchased for \$ 10,000 is sold for \$ 12,500. The realized gain on disposal is:
Solution:
Gain \( = 12{,}500 - 10{,}000 = \$ 2{,}500 \), assuming no prior fair-value adjustment.
Question 4 [2 marks]
Under FIFO and rising prices, compared to weighted-average:
Solution:
FIFO sends the oldest (lowest under rising prices) costs to COGS, so COGS is lower and ending inventory (most recent, higher costs) is higher.
Question 5 [2 marks]
Machine cost \$ 100,000, residual \$ 10,000, useful life 8 years. The annual straight-line amortization expense is:
Solution:
\( (100{,}000 - 10{,}000)/8 = 90{,}000/8 = \$ 11{,}250 \) per year.
Question 6 [3 marks]
A truck (cost \$ 25,000, accumulated amortization \$ 18,000) is sold for \$ 5,000 cash. Compute the gain or loss on disposal (enter a negative number for a loss).
Solution:
Carrying value \( = 25{,}000 - 18{,}000 = \$ 7{,}000 \). Proceeds \$ 5,000. Loss \( = 5{,}000 - 7{,}000 = -\$ 2{,}000 \).
