Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Convert \( 225° \) to radians (exact form).
Solution:
\( 225 \cdot \pi/180 = 225\pi/180 = 5\pi/4 \).
Question 2 [2 marks]
Evaluate \( \cos 150° \) exactly.
Solution:
Reference 30°; Q II ⇒ cos negative. \( \cos 150° = -\sqrt{3}/2 \approx -0.866 \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
Find the arc length on a circle of radius 12 cm subtended by an angle of 90°.
Solution:
\( \theta = \pi/2 \) rad; \( s = r\theta = 12(\pi/2) = 6\pi \approx 18.85 \) cm.
Question 4 [2 marks]
If \( \cos\theta = -1/2 \) and \( \theta \in [0°, 360°) \), find all values.
Solution:
Reference 60°; cos negative in Q II and Q III. So 120° and 240°.
Question 5 [2 marks]
What is the period of \( y=3\sin(\pi x) \)?
Solution:
Period \( = 2\pi/|k| = 2\pi/\pi = 2 \).
Question 6 [3 marks]
In \( \triangle ABC \), \( A=45° \), \( B=60° \), \( c=10 \). Find \( a \) (round to 2 dp).
Solution:
\( C=180-45-60=75° \). \( a/\sin 45° = 10/\sin 75° \). \( a = 10\sin 45°/\sin 75° \approx 10(0.7071)/(0.9659) \approx 7.32 \).
