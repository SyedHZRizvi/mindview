Topic 4.1 — Vectors and Components
Question 1
A vector has magnitude 50 N at angle 60° above horizontal. Find its horizontal component.
Solution:
\( F_{x}=50\cos 60°=50(0.5)=25 \) N.
Question 2
Find the magnitude of a vector with components \( (3,4) \).
Solution:
\( |\vec{v}|=\sqrt{3^{2}+4^{2}}=\sqrt{25}=5 \).
Topic 4.2 — Vector Addition
Question 3
Two forces act on a point: 10 N east and 24 N north. Find the magnitude of the resultant.
Solution:
\( |\vec{R}|=\sqrt{10^{2}+24^{2}}=\sqrt{676}=26 \) N.
Question 4
Two vectors point in opposite directions: \( \vec{A}=20 \) N right and \( \vec{B}=15 \) N left. Find the resultant magnitude and direction.
Solution:
\( 20-15=5 \) N to the right.
Topic 4.3 — Circles
Question 5
A central angle of 100° subtends an arc. The inscribed angle on the same arc is:
Solution:
Inscribed angle = \( \tfrac{1}{2} \) of central = 50°.
Question 6
A chord of length 12 cm is 5 cm from the centre. What is the radius?
Solution:
Right triangle with legs 5 and 6 (half-chord): \( r=\sqrt{25+36}=\sqrt{61}\approx 7.81 \) cm.
Topic 4.4 — Surface Area & Volume
Question 7
Find the volume of a sphere of radius 3 cm. Round to nearest whole number.
Solution:
\( V=\tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}=\tfrac{4}{3}\pi (27)=36\pi\approx 113 \) cm³.
Question 8
A cone has base radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. What is the slant height?
Solution:
\( \ell=\sqrt{6^{2}+8^{2}}=\sqrt{100}=10 \) cm.
Topic 4.5 — Optimization
Question 9
A rectangle has perimeter 40. Find the dimensions that maximize area.
Solution:
For fixed perimeter, the square gives the maximum area.
Question 10
A pulley of radius 5 cm rotates at 60 rpm. What is the linear (rim) speed in cm/s?
Solution:
\( \omega=60 \cdot 2\pi/60 = 2\pi \) rad/s. \( v=r\omega = 5(2\pi) = 10\pi\approx 31.42 \) cm/s.
