Chapter 2: Canadian Constitutional Law — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your understanding of basic Canadian government, federalism, and the Charter before starting Chapter 2. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prereq · Civics
[3 marks]
In Canada, who is the head of state and who is the head of government? Briefly distinguish these roles.
Question 2 Prereq · Federalism
[3 marks]
Federalism divides legislative power between two orders of government. Name the two orders in Canada and list any three areas of responsibility you can think of for each (e.g., one federal area and one provincial area you encounter in everyday life).
Question 3 Prereq · Charter
[3 marks]
Name any three rights you believe are protected by the Charter of Rights and Freedoms. Explain in one sentence each what those rights mean in everyday life.
Question 4 Skills · Reasoning
[3 marks]
Suppose a province passes a law that limits public protest to certain designated areas, and provides for fines for protesting elsewhere.
Without using technical legal vocabulary, identify two questions you would want to ask in deciding whether this law is fair. What might be the strongest argument for the province, and the strongest argument against?
Question 5 Skills · Definitions
[3 marks]
In your own words, what is the difference between a right and a freedom? Give one example of each that you understand.
Question 6 Knowledge · Indigenous Rights
[2 marks]
In one paragraph, describe what you already know about Indigenous rights in Canadian law (treaty rights, Aboriginal title, or the Truth and Reconciliation Commission). If you know little, say so honestly.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 2 topic do you feel least confident about: federalism, the Charter sections, the Oakes test, the notwithstanding clause, or section 35? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
