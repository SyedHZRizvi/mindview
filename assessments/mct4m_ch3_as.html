Topic 3.1 — Radian Measure
Question 1
Convert \( 60° \) to radians.
Solution:
\( 60 \cdot \pi/180 = \pi/3 \) rad.
Question 2
A circle has radius 10 cm. What is the arc length subtended by an angle of \( \pi/4 \) rad?
Solution:
\( s = r\theta = 10 \cdot \pi/4 = 5\pi/2 \approx 7.85 \) cm.
Topic 3.2 — Unit Circle
Question 3
Find the exact value of \( \sin(30°) \).
Solution:
From the unit circle / special triangles: \( \sin 30°=1/2 \).
Question 4
In which quadrant is \( \cos\theta > 0 \) and \( \sin\theta < 0 \)?
Solution:
CAST rule: cos > 0 in Q I and Q IV; sin < 0 in Q III and Q IV. Intersection: Q IV.
Topic 3.3 — Sine and Cosine Laws
Question 5
In \( \triangle ABC \), \( A=30° \), \( B=60° \), and \( a=5 \). Find \( b \).
Solution:
Sine law: \( b/\sin 60° = 5/\sin 30° \). So \( b = 5\sin 60°/\sin 30° = 5(\sqrt{3}/2)/(1/2) = 5\sqrt{3} \approx 8.66 \).
Question 6
In \( \triangle ABC \), \( a=4 \), \( b=5 \), \( C=60° \). Find \( c \) (cosine law).
Solution:
\( c^{2}=16+25-2(4)(5)(0.5)=41-20=21 \). \( c=\sqrt{21}\approx 4.58 \).
Topic 3.4 — Graphs of Sine and Cosine
Question 7
What is the amplitude of \( y=4\sin(2x)+1 \)?
Solution:
Amplitude is \( |a|=4 \).
Question 8
What is the period of \( y=\sin(3x) \)?
Solution:
Period \( = 2\pi/|k| = 2\pi/3 \).
Topic 3.6 — Identities
Question 9
If \( \sin\theta=3/5 \) and \( \theta \) is in Q I, find \( \cos\theta \).
Solution:
Pythagorean: \( \cos^{2}\theta = 1-(3/5)^{2}=1-9/25=16/25 \). In Q I, \( \cos\theta=4/5=0.8 \).
Question 10
Simplify \( \tan\theta \cdot \cos\theta \) using identities.
Solution:
\( \tan\theta = \sin\theta/\cos\theta \), so \( \tan\theta \cdot \cos\theta = \sin\theta \).
