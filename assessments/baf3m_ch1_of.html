Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Which of the following describes the cost principle?
Solution:
Cost principle: record at the verifiable price paid at acquisition.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Owner's Equity at year-end is calculated as:
Solution:
From \( A = L + OE \), \( OE = A - L \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
A debit will decrease which of the following accounts?
Solution:
Liabilities are credit-normal; a debit decreases them. Cash, Expenses, and Drawings are debit-normal; a debit increases them.
Question 4 [2 marks]
A business has Assets \$48 000 and Owner's Equity \$31 000. What are total Liabilities?
Solution:
\( L = A - OE = 48\,000 - 31\,000 = 17\,000 \).
