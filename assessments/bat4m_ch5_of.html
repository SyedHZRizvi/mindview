Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
The Royal Bank of Canada (TSX-listed) prepares financial statements under:
Solution:
Publicly accountable enterprises (including all TSX-listed banks) must use IFRS in Canada.
Question 2 [2 marks]
"Tone at the top" is part of which COSO component?
Solution:
The control environment encompasses the ethical climate, integrity, and competence values set by senior leadership.
Question 3 [2 marks]
The "five fundamental principles" of the CPA Code do NOT include:
Solution:
The five principles are integrity, objectivity, professional competence & due care, confidentiality, professional behaviour. Profitability is not one.
Question 4 [2 marks]
A CPA auditing a company where her brother is the CEO faces what threat?
Solution:
Familiarity threat — close family relationship compromises objectivity.
Question 5 [2 marks]
In a 2-for-1 stock split:
Solution:
A stock split changes only the number of shares — total share capital and total equity are unchanged.
Question 6 [3 marks]
The Enron scandal involved:
Solution:
Enron used special-purpose entities (SPEs) to keep debt off the balance sheet. Its collapse led to Sarbanes-Oxley in 2002.
