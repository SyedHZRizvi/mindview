Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
For \( y = 3(x - 4)^2 - 7 \), state the vertex.
Solution:
Vertex form \( y = a(x-h)^2+k \) gives vertex \( (h, k) = (4, -7) \).
Question 2 [3 marks]
Solve \( x^2 - 8x + 12 = 0 \). The roots are:
Solution:
\( (x-2)(x-6) = 0 \). Roots: 2 and 6.
Question 3 [2 marks]
The discriminant of \( 3x^2 + 4x + 1 = 0 \) is:
Solution:
\( D = 16 - 4(3)(1) = 16 - 12 = 4 \).
Question 4 [2 marks]
Factor \( x^3 - 27 \).
Solution:
Difference of cubes with \( a=x, b=3 \): \( (x-3)(x^2+3x+9) \).
