HFN3M Chapter 1: Nutrition Basics — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Pre-chapter check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps in your prior knowledge of nutrients, energy, and the food label before starting Chapter 1. You receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Prior · Energy
[3 marks]
Define "kilocalorie" in your own words. How is a food "Calorie" related to a small-c "calorie"? Give one everyday food whose energy you know in Calories.
Question 2 Prior · Macronutrients
[4 marks]
Name the three macronutrients and the energy in kilocalories per gram of each. Which delivers the most energy per gram? Why is alcohol not classified as a macronutrient even though it provides energy?
Question 3 Prior · Labels
[3 marks]
Imagine reading a Nutrition Facts table on a granola bar. Name three pieces of information you would look at before deciding whether to buy it.
Question 4 Prior · Health
[3 marks]
List two health conditions in Canada that are influenced by dietary choices, and briefly state one dietary factor associated with each.
Question 5 Skills · Inquiry
[3 marks]
If you saw a social-media post claiming "Eating only fruit removes toxins from your body in 7 days", what three questions would you ask before believing it?
Question 6 Knowledge
[3 marks]
List three foods you regularly eat that contain animal protein, and three plant-protein-rich foods. Are any of these plant foods complete proteins?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Chapter 1 topic do you feel least confident about: nutrient classes, energy calculations, the role of fibre, fat types, protein needs, or vitamins & minerals? Explain what you find unclear and one strategy you will use to learn it better.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
