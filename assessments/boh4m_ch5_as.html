Topic 5.1 — The Control Process
Question 1
The four steps of the control process are:
Solution:
(1) Set standards (2) Measure (3) Compare (4) Take corrective action.
Question 2
A preflight checklist used by an airline pilot is best classified as:
Solution:
Used BEFORE the work to prevent problems = feed-forward (preventive) control.
Topic 5.2 — Financial & Operational Controls
Question 3
Fixed costs $5,000/month; Price $12; Variable cost $7. What is the monthly breakeven volume?
Solution:
Contribution margin = $12 − $7 = $5. Breakeven = $5000 ÷ $5 = 1000 units.
Question 4
Which is NOT a quality-management standard or framework?
Solution:
PESTLE is an environmental-scanning framework, not a quality-management standard.
Topic 5.3 — Balanced Scorecard
Question 5
Which is NOT one of the four Balanced Scorecard perspectives?
Solution:
Kaplan & Norton's four: Financial, Customer, Internal Process, Learning & Growth.
Topic 5.4 — Ethics
Question 6
"Greatest good for the greatest number" describes which ethical framework?
Solution:
Greatest aggregate benefit = utilitarian.
Question 7
A code of ethics typically states:
Solution:
Code of ethics = principles/values. Code of conduct = specific behavioural rules.
Topic 5.5 — CSR & Triple Bottom Line
Question 8
The "Triple Bottom Line" (Elkington) is:
Solution:
TBL = People, Planet, Profit.
Question 9
In Carroll's CSR pyramid, which responsibility is at the base?
Solution:
Carroll's pyramid (base to top): Economic → Legal → Ethical → Philanthropic.
Topic 5.6 — Culminating Case Study
Question 10
A strong culminating-investigation research question should:
Solution:
Narrow focus = stronger analysis. One organization, one tension, one thesis.
