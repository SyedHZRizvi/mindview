Unit 1: Foundations of Culture — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand B
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps from prior social-science coursework before beginning Unit 1. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Honest answers help your teacher tailor instruction to what you actually need.
Question 1 Prereq · Definitions
[3 marks]
In your own words, define culture. How would you distinguish it from society? Give one example to illustrate the difference.
Question 2 Prereq · Concepts
[3 marks]
List three examples of material culture and three examples of non-material culture. For each non-material example, briefly explain what it does in a society.
Question 3 Skills · Concept Distinction
[3 marks]
A Toronto teenager visiting friends in rural Quebec finds the local way of eating dinner — long, slow, with much conversation, and starting at 8 pm — "weird and inconvenient."
Identify what the teenager is doing in cultural-studies terms (one concept word), and explain how a cultural-relativism approach would reframe the reaction.
Question 4 Disciplines
[3 marks]
Anthropology, sociology, and psychology all study human culture, but they ask different questions and use different methods. In a brief paragraph, distinguish what each discipline would emphasize if studying high-school cafeteria social dynamics.
Question 5 Application
[4 marks]
Consider a familiar cultural practice (a meal, a holiday, a ritual, a sport). Apply the five characteristics of culture (learned, shared, symbolic, integrated, dynamic) to it. Use one sentence per characteristic, giving a specific detail of your example for each.
Question 6 Knowledge
[2 marks]
Explain the difference between methodological cultural relativism (the research stance) and moral / ethical cultural relativism (the philosophical claim). Why do most contemporary social scientists accept the first but reject the strong second?
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
What aspect of "Foundations of Culture" do you find LEAST clear at the moment: defining culture, distinguishing material/non-material, cultural universals vs particulars, ethnocentrism vs cultural relativism, or the three social-science lenses? Explain what specifically is unclear so your teacher can address it.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
