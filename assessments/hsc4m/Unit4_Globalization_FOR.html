Unit 4: Globalization & Cultural Change — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand E
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps before starting Unit 4. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Honest answers help your teacher tailor instruction.
Question 1 Concept · Globalization
[3 marks]
In your own words, what is "globalization"? Identify TWO ways you personally have experienced it in the last week (food, media, technology, communication, products, ideas, etc.).
Question 2 Concept · Digital culture
[3 marks]
How would you describe your relationship with social-media platforms? In 3–5 sentences, identify ONE way the platforms have helped you (cultural connection, learning, community) and ONE way they may have harmed (time spent, anxiety, narrowing exposure).
Question 3 Concept · Appropriation
[3 marks]
"At a Toronto music festival, a non-Indigenous attendee wears a fake feathered headdress as part of their festival costume."
What does the word cultural appropriation mean to you? Identify what concerns are raised in this scenario.
Question 4 Concept · Migration
[3 marks]
What do you know about the migration history of YOUR family or community? Or, if you would prefer, of a friend's family. Try to identify (a) what country/region of origin, (b) when (which generation arrived), (c) why (push and pull factors).
Question 5 Reflection · Revitalization
[3 marks]
Have you heard of any cultural revitalization movements (Māori, Mohawk, Hawaiian, Indigenous land back, Sámi parliament, Welsh language, etc.)? What do you know about one of them?
Question 6 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 4 topic do you feel LEAST prepared for: globalization theories, media/digital culture, cultural appropriation, migration/diaspora, or revitalization? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
