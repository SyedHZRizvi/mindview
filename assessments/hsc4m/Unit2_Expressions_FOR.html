Unit 2: Cultural Expressions — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand C
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps before starting Unit 2. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Honest answers help your teacher tailor instruction.
Question 1 Concept · Language
[3 marks]
What does it mean to say "language is non-material culture"? Give one example of how a language carries cultural values that survive even when the cultural form (food, dress) changes.
Question 2 Concept · Religion
[3 marks]
"For the Inuit, the sea is a relative — a being with whom one is in relationship, not a resource one consumes."
Explain what this passage suggests about Inuit worldview / cosmology. How does it contrast with the dominant European-settler worldview of "the sea"?
Question 3 Concept · Ritual
[3 marks]
Describe a "rite of passage" you have personally experienced or witnessed (birthday, graduation, religious initiation, sports team initiation, university orientation, etc.). Identify, where possible, the three van Gennep stages: separation, liminality, incorporation.
Question 4 Concept · Art
[3 marks]
Explain the difference between the FUNCTION and the FORM of an artwork. Use one specific example (visual art, music, dance, or literature) to show both.
Question 5 Application · Music
[4 marks]
Pick a music genre you listen to. In 4–5 sentences, identify (a) where it originated, (b) what cultural values it expresses or has expressed historically, (c) how it has changed as it travelled (if applicable), and (d) what it means TO YOU in your specific cultural and personal context.
Question 6 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 2 topic do you feel LEAST prepared for: language & worldview, religion & cosmology, ritual, art & aesthetics, or music & dance? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
