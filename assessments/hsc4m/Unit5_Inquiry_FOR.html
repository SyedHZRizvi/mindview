Unit 5: Social-Science Inquiry Skills — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand A
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps before starting Unit 5 and the Independent Study Project (ISP). You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade.
Question 1 Inquiry · Topic-to-question
[4 marks]
Take ONE topic that interests you for your ISP (e.g., a cultural practice, a community, a media phenomenon, a migration story). In 4–5 sentences, narrow it from "broad topic" to a focused, researchable research question. Show the iterations.
Question 2 Methods
[3 marks]
For your ISP question above (or another), identify (a) which method (ethnography, interview, survey, content analysis, secondary-source review) you think would best answer it and (b) why. Be specific.
Question 3 Sources
[3 marks]
Apply the CRAAP test (Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose) to evaluate one source you are considering for the ISP. Identify the source, then walk through at least three of the CRAAP criteria.
Question 4 Ethics
[4 marks]
"For my ISP I want to interview three of my friends about their family religious practices. I think we'll just chat one evening."
Identify FOUR ethical considerations this plan needs to address before the interviews. Be specific.
Question 5 APA
[2 marks]
Convert this informal reference to proper APA 7th edition format (best guess): "The book by Geertz, called 'The Interpretation of Cultures,' was published in 1973 by Basic Books in New York."
Question 6 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which research skill do you feel LEAST confident about: framing a research question, choosing methods, evaluating sources, research ethics, or APA citation? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
