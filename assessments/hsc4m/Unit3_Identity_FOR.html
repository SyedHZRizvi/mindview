Unit 3: Identity & Diversity — Diagnostic

Assessment FOR Learning · Prerequisite check · Strand D
Not Graded — Teacher Feedback Only
Purpose: Identify gaps before starting Unit 3. You will receive descriptive feedback, not a grade. Honest answers help your teacher tailor instruction.
Question 1 Self-reflection · Socialization
[4 marks]
In 4–5 sentences, identify the THREE most influential agents of socialization in your own life (family, peers, school, media, religion, sports, etc.). For each, give one specific example of a value or practice you have learned from them.
Question 2 Concept · Gender
[3 marks]
In your own words, distinguish three terms: sex, gender, and sexuality. Why is the distinction important in social-science work?
Question 3 Concept · Race / Ethnicity
[3 marks]
Define race and ethnicity as social scientists use the terms. How are they related? How are they different?
Question 4 Reflection · Identity
[3 marks]
"My friend Sara doesn't understand why my parents won't let me sleep over at her cottage. She says, 'It's only one night.' My parents say, 'You're not raised that way.'"
What does this short scene reveal about how different families socialise teenagers? Identify two social-science concepts (from Mead, Cooley, or Marcia) you could apply.
Question 5 Intersectionality
[3 marks]
Have you encountered the word "intersectionality" before? In your own words, what do you understand it to mean? (Don't worry if you are not sure — give your best current understanding so your teacher can build on it.)
Question 6 Canada · TRC
[3 marks]
What do you know about Canada's Truth and Reconciliation Commission (2008–2015) and its 94 Calls to Action? Identify what you know and what you would like to learn.
Question 7 Reflection
[ungraded]
Which Unit 3 topic do you feel LEAST prepared for: socialization, gender across cultures, race/ethnicity, intersectionality, or Canadian multicultural/Indigenous identity? Explain what specifically is unclear.

Teacher Feedback

Your teacher will provide descriptive feedback after reviewing your responses.
