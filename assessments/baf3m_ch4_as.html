Topic 4.1 — Income Statement
Question 1
Revenue \$8 500, Total Expenses \$6 200. Net Income =
Solution:
\(8\,500 - 6\,200 = 2\,300\). Net Income \$2 300.
Question 2
The heading of an income statement is best stated as:
Solution:
The income statement covers a period of time, so the heading uses "for the period ended …". The balance sheet uses a single date.
Topic 4.2 — Statement of Owner's Equity
Question 3
Beginning Capital \$12 000, Net Income \$3 500, Drawings \$1 200, no additional investments. Ending Capital =
Solution:
\(12\,000 + 3\,500 - 1\,200 = 14\,300\).
Question 4
A business has Beginning Capital \$8 000, Net Loss \$1 500, and Additional Investment \$2 000. Drawings = 0. Ending Capital =
Solution:
\(8\,000 - 1\,500 + 2\,000 = 8\,500\).
Topic 4.3 — Balance Sheet
Question 5
Which is a current asset?
Solution:
A/R is expected to be collected within 12 months — a current asset. Land is non-current, Mortgage is a liability, Capital is equity.
Question 6
Equipment cost \$8 000, accumulated depreciation \$2 400. Net book value shown on the balance sheet =
Solution:
\(NBV = 8\,000 - 2\,400 = 5\,600\).
Topic 4.4 — Working Capital & Current Ratio
Question 7
Current Assets \$15 000, Current Liabilities \$6 000. Working Capital =
Solution:
\(WC = CA - CL = 15\,000 - 6\,000 = 9\,000\).
Question 8
Current Assets \$24 000, Current Liabilities \$10 000. Current Ratio (to two decimals) =
Solution:
\( \dfrac{24\,000}{10\,000} = 2.40 \). The ratio is healthily above the 2:1 benchmark.
Topic 4.5 — Merchandising & COGS
Question 9
Beginning Inventory \$5 000, Net Purchases \$32 000, Ending Inventory \$7 500. COGS =
Solution:
\(COGS = 5\,000 + 32\,000 - 7\,500 = 29\,500\).
Question 10
Net Sales \$60 000, COGS \$36 000. Gross Profit =
Solution:
\(GP = 60\,000 - 36\,000 = 24\,000\). The gross-profit ratio is \(24\,000 / 60\,000 = 40\%\).
