Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Which of the following is NOT one of Fayol's four functions of management?
Solution:
POLC = Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling. Networking is not one of the four.
Question 2 [2 marks]
According to Katz, which skill is most important for first-line managers?
Solution:
First-line managers need technical skill — they must understand the work they supervise. Conceptual skill rises with level.
Question 3 [2 marks]
Mintzberg's three role categories are:
Solution:
Mintzberg's three role groups are Interpersonal, Informational, Decisional.
Question 4 [2 marks]
Frederick Taylor is best known for which contribution to management thought?
Solution:
Taylor — Principles of Scientific Management (1911). Weber = bureaucracy; Mayo = Hawthorne; Fayol = 14 principles.
Question 5 [3 marks]
List, in order, the four steps a Tim Hortons regional manager would follow when implementing the four management functions for a new breakfast-sandwich launch. (One word for each function.)
Solution:
Planning → Organizing → Leading → Controlling (POLC).
Question 6 [2 marks]
A company's CEO speaks at a charity event on behalf of the firm. Which Mintzberg role is she performing?
Solution:
Speaking to outsiders about the organization = Spokesperson (informational category).
