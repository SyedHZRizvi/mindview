Topic 1.1–1.2 — Exponent Laws & Rational Exponents
Question 1
Simplify \( x^7 \cdot x^{-3} \).
Solution:
Apply product law: \( x^7 \cdot x^{-3} = x^{7+(-3)} = x^4 \).
Question 2
Evaluate \( 16^{3/4} \).
Solution:
\( 16^{3/4} = (\sqrt[4]{16})^3 = 2^3 = 8 \).
Question 3
Evaluate \( 27^{-2/3} \).
Solution:
\( 27^{-2/3} = \dfrac{1}{27^{2/3}} = \dfrac{1}{(\sqrt[3]{27})^2} = \dfrac{1}{3^2} = \dfrac{1}{9} \).
Topic 1.3 — Graphing Exponential Functions
Question 4
For \( y = 5 \cdot 2^x \), what is the y-intercept?
Solution:
Set \( x = 0 \): \( y = 5 \cdot 2^0 = 5 \cdot 1 = 5 \).
Question 5
Which describes the function \( y = 100(0.7)^x \)?
Solution:
Base \( b = 0.7 < 1 \), so exponential decay.
Topic 1.4 — Solving Exponential Equations (Common Base)
Question 6
Solve \( 2^{x+1} = 32 \).
Solution:
\( 32 = 2^5 \), so \( 2^{x+1} = 2^5 \). Equate: \( x + 1 = 5 \), so \( x = 4 \).
Question 7
Solve \( 9^{2x-1} = 27 \).
Solution:
Rewrite both in base 3: \( (3^2)^{2x-1} = 3^3 \Rightarrow 3^{4x-2} = 3^3 \Rightarrow 4x - 2 = 3 \Rightarrow x = 5/4 \).
Topic 1.5 — Solving Exponential Equations (Logarithms)
Question 8
Solve \( 1.05^x = 2 \). Round to 1 decimal place. (Hint: \( \log 2 \approx 0.301 \), \( \log 1.05 \approx 0.0212 \).)
Solution:
Take \( \log \): \( x \log 1.05 = \log 2 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{0.301}{0.0212} \approx 14.2 \).
Question 9
Solve \( 3^{x} = 20 \). Round to 2 decimal places. (Hint: \( \log 20 \approx 1.301 \), \( \log 3 \approx 0.477 \).)
Solution:
\( x = \dfrac{\log 20}{\log 3} = \dfrac{1.301}{0.477} \approx 2.73 \).
Topic 1.6 — Growth, Decay, Half-Life
Question 10
A bacterial culture doubles every 5 hours. Starting at 200 cells, how many cells after 20 hours?
Solution:
\( P(20) = 200 \cdot 2^{20/5} = 200 \cdot 2^4 = 200 \cdot 16 = 3200 \) cells.
Question 11
A radioactive isotope has a half-life of 10 days. What fraction of an initial 80 g remains after 30 days?
Solution:
30 days = 3 half-lives. \( 80 \cdot (1/2)^3 = 80/8 = 10 \) g.
Question 12
\$1000 invested at 5% per year compounded annually. Find the value after 3 years. (Round to nearest cent.)
Solution:
\( A = 1000(1.05)^3 = 1000(1.157625) = \$1157.63 \).
