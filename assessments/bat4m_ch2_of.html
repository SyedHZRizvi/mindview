Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [13 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
Which special journal records cash sales of merchandise?
Solution:
The sales journal records credit sales of merchandise only. Cash sales (any source) are recorded in the cash receipts journal.
Question 2 [2 marks]
Beginning inventory \$ 12,000; net purchases \$ 80,000; ending inventory \$ 15,000. COGS (periodic) is:
Solution:
\( 12{,}000 + 80{,}000 - 15{,}000 = \$ 77{,}000 \).
Question 3 [2 marks]
Partners A and B share profit in a 4:1 ratio. Net income is \$ 90,000. A's share is:
Solution:
A's share \( = (4/5) \times 90{,}000 = \$ 72{,}000 \). B's share \( = \$ 18{,}000 \).
Question 4 [2 marks]
A corporation issues 10,000 no-par common shares at \$ 12 cash. The credit to Common Shares is:
Solution:
\( 10{,}000 \times 12 = \$ 120{,}000 \). Under Canadian no-par rules, the entire proceeds go to Common Shares — no premium account.
Question 5 [3 marks]
Employee gross pay \$ 1,500. CPP \$ 89.25, EI \$ 24.90, income tax \$ 195. Compute net pay.
Solution:
Total deductions \( = 89.25 + 24.90 + 195 = \$ 309.15 \). Net pay \( = 1{,}500 - 309.15 = \$ 1{,}190.85 \).
Question 6 [2 marks]
A 3-for-1 stock split changes:
Solution:
Stock splits change only the number of shares and the per-share value; total share capital and total equity are unchanged. No formal journal entry; a memo is made.
