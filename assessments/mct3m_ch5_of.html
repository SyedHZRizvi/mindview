Part A: Knowledge & Understanding [12 marks]
Question 1 [2 marks]
For a rectangle with fixed perimeter \( P \), the shape that maximises area is:
Solution:
Among rectangles with fixed perimeter, the square maximises area.
Question 2 [3 marks]
A closed cylinder of volume 250 cm³ has the optimum shape with \( h = 2r \). Find \( r \) (round to 2 dp).
Solution:
\( V = \pi r^2 (2r) = 2\pi r^3 = 250 \Rightarrow r^3 = 125/\pi \approx 39.79 \Rightarrow r \approx 3.41 \) cm.
Question 3 [3 marks]
On a 1:50 architectural drawing, a window is 4 cm × 6 cm. Find the actual window area in m².
Solution:
Actual length 4 × 50 = 200 cm = 2 m. Actual width 6 × 50 = 300 cm = 3 m. Area = 2 × 3 = 6 m².
Question 4 [2 marks]
If linear dimensions scale by factor \( k = 4 \), volume scales by:
Solution:
Volume scales by \( k^3 = 4^3 = 64 \).
Question 5 [2 marks]
A square pyramid has base side 6 m and slant height 5 m. The vertical height is:
Solution:
Half-base = 3. \( h = \sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = 4 \) m.
